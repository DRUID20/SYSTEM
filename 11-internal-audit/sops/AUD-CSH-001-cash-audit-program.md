## 1. PURPOSE

To provide a detailed, step-by-step audit program for examining Cash and Bank management at Awdeer Investments Limited. This program covers cash handling, petty cash, bank transactions, bank reconciliations, and cash security to ensure completeness, accuracy, authorization, and safeguarding of all cash and bank assets.

- Petty cash funds (all custodians)
- All bank accounts (operating, savings, foreign currency)

## 5. DETAILED AUDIT STEPS

### CONTROLLED VERSION

---

### Section A: Physical Cash Count

| Step | Procedure | Sample Size | Expected Evidence | W/P Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A.1 | Conduct a surprise physical count of all cash on hand at each cash point. Count all denominations in the presence of the custodian. | 100% of cash points | Signed cash count sheet | |
| A.2 | Agree the physical count total to the cash book balance at the time of count. | 100% | Cash count reconciliation | |
| A.3 | Investigate any shortage or overage. Obtain explanation from custodian and document. | All variances | Custodian explanation memo | |
| A.4 | Verify that cash floats are within authorized limits. Check authorization for the float amount. | 100% of floats | Float authorization document | |
| A.5 | Inspect physical cash storage facilities (safe, cash box, strongroom). Verify they are adequate and secure. | All locations | Observation notes, photos | |
| A.6 | Verify that safe combinations / keys are properly controlled. Check who has access. | All safes | Access control log / key register | |
| A.7 | Check insurance coverage for cash on premises and cash in transit. | All policies | Insurance certificates | |

**Red Flags — Physical Cash:**
- Cash shortage with no explanation or vague explanation
- Cash overage (may indicate unrecorded collections)
- Float exceeding authorized limit
- Multiple persons with safe access but no dual-control requirement
- Cash stored in unsecured location
- Stale or old-denomination notes in the float

---

### Section B: Petty Cash

| Step | Procedure | Sample Size | Expected Evidence | W/P Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B.1 | Count petty cash fund. Verify: Cash + vouchers = Authorized float amount. | 100% of petty cash funds | Petty cash count sheet | |
| B.2 | Select a sample of petty cash vouchers. For each, verify: (a) Proper authorization, (b) Supporting receipt/invoice attached, (c) Amount is within per-transaction limit, (d) Business purpose documented. | 30 vouchers or all if <30 | Sampled vouchers | |
| B.3 | Test petty cash replenishments: (a) Verify supporting vouchers total matches replenishment cheque/transfer, (b) Verify proper approval of replenishment, (c) Check frequency (should not be excessively frequent). | Last 6 replenishments | Replenishment records | |
| B.4 | Verify segregation of duties: Custodian should not approve their own expenditures or prepare their own replenishment requests. | N/A | Process observation / interviews | |
| B.5 | Review petty cash transactions for unusual items: Large amounts, personal expenses, duplicate voucher numbers, altered amounts. | All sampled vouchers | Exception log | |
| B.6 | Verify petty cash per-transaction and monthly limits per policy. | 100% | Policy vs. actual comparison | |

**Red Flags — Petty Cash:**
- Cash + vouchers does not equal the authorized float
- Vouchers without receipts or with photocopied receipts
- Payments exceeding per-transaction limit
- Personal expenses paid through petty cash
- Altered voucher amounts (white-out, overwriting)
- Same vendor appearing repeatedly for large amounts
- Excessive replenishment frequency (more than weekly)

### Section C: Bank Reconciliations

| Step | Procedure | Sample Size | Expected Evidence | W/P Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C.1 | Obtain bank reconciliation statements for all accounts for each month in the audit period. | All accounts, all months | Bank reconciliation files | |
| C.2 | Verify reconciliations are prepared timely (within 5 business days of month-end). | All months | Preparation dates on reconciliations | |
| C.3 | Verify reconciliations are reviewed and approved by an independent person (not the preparer). | All months | Review signature and date | |
| C.4 | For each reconciliation, verify mathematical accuracy. Independently verify: Opening balance + receipts - payments = Closing balance agrees to bank statement. | 3 months (random) | Recalculation working paper | |
| C.5 | Review outstanding cheques: (a) Age analysis, (b) Investigate items outstanding >30 days, (c) Verify eventual clearance. | All outstanding items >30 days | Outstanding cheque analysis | |
| C.6 | Review deposits in transit: (a) Verify they cleared within 2 business days, (b) Investigate items outstanding >5 days. | All deposits in transit | Clearance verification | |
| C.7 | Review other reconciling items (bank charges, interest, errors). Verify they are properly recorded in the next month. | All other reconciling items | Journal entries / adjustments | |
| C.8 | Obtain independent bank confirmations for all accounts at period-end. Compare confirmed balance to recorded balance. | All bank accounts | Bank confirmation letters | |
| C.9 | Verify that all bank accounts are authorized by the Board and included in the reconciliation process. | All accounts | Board minutes / account list | |

**Red Flags — Bank Reconciliations:**
- Reconciliations prepared late (>10 days after month-end)
- No independent review of reconciliations
- Outstanding items older than 60 days without explanation
- Deposits in transit not clearing within 3 days
- Unidentified/unexplained reconciling items
- Differences between bank confirmation and recorded balance
- Unauthorized bank accounts

### Section D: Cash Receipts

| Step | Procedure | Sample Size | Expected Evidence | W/P Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D.1 | Select a sample of receipt book stubs. Verify: (a) Sequential numbering (no gaps), (b) Amount recorded in cash book, (c) Amount deposited in bank. | 30 receipts | Receipt stubs, cash book, bank statement | |
| D.2 | Select a sample of bank deposit slips. Trace to: (a) Cash book entries, (b) Supporting receipt records. | 20 deposit slips | Deposit slips, cash book | |
| D.3 | Test daily banking: Verify that daily collections are banked intact on the same day or next business day. | 15 random days | Daily collection records vs. bank deposits | |
| D.4 | Verify that receipt books are pre-numbered and controlled. Check issuance register. | All active receipt books | Receipt book register | |
| D.5 | Test for lapping: Select customer payments and trace the application across consecutive days. | 10 customer accounts | Customer ledger, bank deposits | |
| D.6 | Verify segregation of duties: Person receiving cash should not be the person recording in the cash book or preparing bank deposits. | N/A | Process observation / interviews | |

**Red Flags — Cash Receipts:**
- Missing receipt numbers (gaps in sequence)
- Late banking (collections held >1 day)
- Collections not banked intact (partial deposits)
- Same person receives cash, records, and deposits
- Customer complaints about payments not credited

### Section E: Cash Payments / Disbursements

| Step | Procedure | Sample Size | Expected Evidence | W/P Ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E.1 | Select a sample of payment vouchers. For each, verify: (a) Proper authorization per approval matrix, (b) Complete supporting documentation (invoice, PO, GRN, contract), (c) Correct payee and amount, (d) Properly recorded in cash book/ledger, (e) Tax treatment (WHT deducted where applicable). | 30 payments or all if <30 | Payment vouchers with support | |
| E.2 | Test for duplicate payments: Run data analytics on payment file for duplicate invoice numbers, amounts, and vendors. | Full population | Duplicate analysis report | |
| E.3 | Test payment authorization limits: Verify no payments exceed the signer's authorized limit. | 30 payments (include all >UGX 10M) | Payment vouchers, authorization matrix | |
| E.4 | Review cheque/EFT controls: (a) Cheques pre-numbered and sequential, (b) Voided cheques properly cancelled and retained, (c) EFT payments have dual authorization. | All cheque stubs for 2 months; all EFTs for 1 month | Cheque register, EFT system log | |
| E.5 | Test bank signatory compliance: Verify signatures on cheques match the authorized signatory list. For amounts above threshold, verify dual signature. | 20 cheques | Cheques, signatory mandate | |
| E.6 | Verify that payment vouchers and supporting documents are stamped "PAID" to prevent re-use. | All sampled payments | Physical inspection | |
| E.7 | Review cancelled/voided cheques: Verify proper authorization and defacement. | All voided cheques in period | Voided cheque register | |

**Red Flags — Payments:**
- Payments without proper authorization
- Missing or incomplete supporting documents
- Duplicate payments (same invoice paid twice)
- Payments to unusual or unknown vendors
- Payments exceeding signatory limits
- Cheque numbers out of sequence or missing
- EFT payments with single authorization above threshold
- Supporting documents not stamped "PAID"
