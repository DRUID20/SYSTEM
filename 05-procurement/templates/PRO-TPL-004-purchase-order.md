### Terms and Conditions

#### 1. Acceptance
This Purchase Order constitutes an offer by Awdeer Investments Limited ("Buyer") to purchase the goods/services described above from the Supplier on the terms stated herein. Supply of goods/services against this PO constitutes acceptance of these terms.

#### 2. Delivery
- Goods must be delivered to the specified delivery address by the required delivery date.
- The Supplier must notify the Buyer at least 2 business days before delivery.
- All goods must be accompanied by a delivery note referencing this PO number.
- Late delivery beyond 5 business days may result in order cancellation or penalty.

#### 3. Quality and Inspection
- All goods must conform to the specifications stated in this PO.
- The Buyer reserves the right to inspect and test goods upon delivery.
- Non-conforming goods will be rejected and returned at the Supplier's cost.
- The Buyer has 5 business days from delivery to report quality issues.

#### 4. Pricing
- Prices are fixed as per this PO and may not be increased without prior written agreement.
- The PO value is the maximum payable — any additional charges not stated herein are not accepted.

#### 5. Invoicing
- Invoices must reference this PO number to be processed.
- Invoices must be original tax invoices showing the Supplier's TIN, VAT registration (if applicable), and comply with URA requirements.
- Invoices not referencing a valid PO will be returned unpaid.

#### 6. Payment
- Payment will be made per the payment terms stated above.
- Payment is subject to satisfactory receipt of goods/services (GRN or Service Acceptance Certificate).
- Withholding Tax (WHT) will be deducted at source as per URA regulations.
- Payment will be made by Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) to the Supplier's bank account on record.

#### 7. Warranty
- The Supplier warrants that all goods are new, free from defects, and fit for purpose.
- Minimum warranty period: 12 months from delivery (or as specified in the PO).
- The Supplier shall replace or repair defective goods at no additional cost within the warranty period.

#### 8. Indemnity and Liability
- The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against all claims arising from defective goods/services.
- The Supplier is responsible for all applicable taxes on their income from this transaction.

#### 9. Confidentiality
- The Supplier shall treat all information related to this PO and the Buyer's business as confidential.

#### 10. Termination
- The Buyer may cancel this PO in writing if the Supplier fails to meet delivery dates or quality requirements.
- Cancellation does not affect the Buyer's rights regarding goods already delivered.

#### 11. Anti-Corruption
- The Supplier warrants that no bribes, gifts, or inducements have been offered to any Awdeer employee in connection with this PO.
- Breach of this clause is grounds for immediate PO cancellation, supplier blacklisting, and legal action.

#### 12. Governing Law
- This PO is governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda.
- Disputes shall be resolved by negotiation, failing which by arbitration in Kampala, Uganda.

#### 13. Amendments
- No amendment to this PO is valid unless in writing and signed by both parties.
- Verbal agreements or changes are not binding.
