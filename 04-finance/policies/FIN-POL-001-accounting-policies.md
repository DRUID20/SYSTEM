## 1. Purpose

This manual establishes the accounting policies governing the preparation of financial statements and management reports for Awdeer Investments Limited and all its subsidiaries and business lines. All accounting transactions must comply with these policies.

Applies to all entities, subsidiaries, branches, and business lines within the Awdeer Investments Limited group: Energy, Real Estate, Logistics, Retail, and Services.

## 4. Revenue Recognition (IFRS 15)

### 4.1 General Principles

Revenue is recognised when:
1. A contract with a customer exists (written or implied)
2. Performance obligations are identified
3. Transaction price is determined
4. Price is allocated to performance obligations
5. Revenue is recognised as/when performance obligations are satisfied

Revenue is measured net of VAT (18%), trade discounts, and volume rebates.

### 4.2 Revenue Recognition by Business Line

#### 4.2.1 Energy (ENRG)

| Revenue Stream | Recognition Point | Policy |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Solar Product Sales | Point in time | On delivery and customer acceptance; title and risk must have transferred |
| Installation Services | Over time | Percentage of completion based on cost-to-cost method; milestone sign-off required |
| Maintenance Contracts | Over time | Straight-line over the contract period (typically 12 months) |
| Energy Trading / Power Sales | Point in time | On meter reading and delivery of power units (kWh) |

**Bundled contracts:** Where solar products and installation are sold as a single contract, the transaction price is allocated to each performance obligation based on standalone selling prices using the adjusted market assessment approach.

#### 4.2.2 Real Estate (REST)

| Revenue Stream | Recognition Point | Policy |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Property Sales | Point in time | On transfer of legal title and vacant possession; full payment or binding finance in place |
| Rental Income | Over time | Straight-line over the lease term per IFRS 16 (lessor accounting) |
| Property Management Fees | Over time | Monthly as services are rendered |
| Development Fees | Over time | Percentage of completion; milestone-based recognition |

**Off-plan sales:** Revenue deferred until construction reaches 80% completion and irrevocable commitment from buyer is secured.

#### 4.2.3 Logistics (LGST)

| Revenue Stream | Recognition Point | Policy |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Freight / Haulage | Over time | On completion of each trip/delivery leg with signed proof of delivery (POD) |
| Warehousing | Over time | Monthly based on contracted storage rates and actual usage |
| Clearing & Forwarding | Point in time | On customs clearance and release of goods |
| Last-Mile Delivery | Point in time | On confirmed delivery with recipient signature or OTP confirmation |

#### 4.2.4 Retail (RETL)

| Revenue Stream | Recognition Point | Policy |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Retail Sales | Point in time | At point of sale (POS transaction) when payment is received |
| Wholesale Sales | Point in time | On delivery and acceptance; for credit sales, on invoice date with confirmed delivery |
| E-Commerce Sales | Point in time | On confirmed delivery to customer; revenue deferred from order date to delivery date |

**Returns policy:** Revenue reversed on validated returns within 7 days with original receipt. Provision for expected returns created monthly based on historical rates.

#### 4.2.5 Services (SRVC)

| Revenue Stream | Recognition Point | Policy |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Consulting & Advisory | Over time | Based on hours delivered or milestones achieved per engagement letter |
| IT Services | Over time | Monthly for retainer contracts; milestone for project-based |
| Training & Capacity Building | Point in time | On completion of training event with attendance records |
| Outsourced Staffing | Over time | Monthly based on staff deployed and confirmed timesheets |

### 4.3 Contract Assets and Liabilities

- **Contract Asset (Accrued Revenue):** Recognised when revenue is earned but not yet invoiced. Transferred to trade receivables upon invoicing.
- **Contract Liability (Deferred Revenue):** Recognised when payment is received before performance obligations are satisfied. Released to revenue as obligations are met.

## 5. Expense Classification Rules

### 5.1 Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) — Class 5

Costs directly attributable to delivering revenue, including:
- Direct materials and purchased goods
- Direct labour (installation crews, drivers, project staff)
- Subcontractor and third-party service costs
- Freight-in for merchandise
- Direct project costs

### 5.2 Operating Expenses — Class 6

Recurring costs of running the business, including:
- Staff costs (salaries, NSSF, medical, training)
- Premises costs (rent, utilities, maintenance)
- Administrative costs (supplies, printing, courier)
- Selling & marketing costs
- Travel & entertainment
- Depreciation & amortization
- Bad debts and bank charges

### 5.3 Administrative & Other Expenses — Class 7

Non-recurring or governance-related costs:
- Directors' fees
- Audit and legal fees
- Regulatory fees and penalties
- CSR and donations
- Foreign exchange losses
- Impairment charges

### 5.4 Classification Decision Rules

| Question | If Yes | If No |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Is the cost directly tied to producing/delivering a specific revenue stream? | Class 5 (COGS) | Continue |
| Is the cost a recurring operational expense? | Class 6 | Continue |
| Is the cost governance, regulatory, or non-recurring? | Class 7 | Continue |
| Is it finance income/cost or disposal gain/loss? | Class 8 | Clarify with FC |

## 6. Accruals and Prepayments Policy

### 6.1 Accruals

**Definition:** Expenses incurred but not yet invoiced or paid at period end.

**Policy:**
- All known expenses above UGX 500,000 must be accrued at month-end
- Recurring accruals (utilities, rent, salaries) are processed through standing journal templates
- Non-recurring accruals require supporting documentation (email, contract extract, estimate)
- Accruals must be reversed in the following period and replaced by actual invoices
- Accrual aging above 90 days must be investigated and resolved

**Minimum accrual threshold:** UGX 500,000 per individual item. Items below this threshold may be expensed in the period the invoice is received.

### 6.2 Prepayments

**Definition:** Payments made in advance for goods or services to be received in future periods.

**Policy:**
- Payments above UGX 1,000,000 for services/goods spanning more than one month must be recorded as prepayments
- Prepayments are amortized on a straight-line basis over the service period
- Common prepayments: rent, insurance, software licenses, subscriptions
- Monthly amortization journals are processed by BD5 of each month
- Prepayment schedules must be maintained and reconciled monthly

## 7. Foreign Exchange (FX) Handling

### 7.1 Functional Currency

All transactions are recorded in UGX. The Company maintains both UGX and USD bank accounts.

### 7.2 Transaction Date Rate

- Foreign currency transactions are recorded at the Bank of Uganda mid-rate on the transaction date
- For invoices, the rate on the invoice date is used
- For payments, the rate on the payment date is used

### 7.3 Month-End Revaluation

- All foreign currency monetary items (bank balances, receivables, payables) are revalued at the Bank of Uganda closing mid-rate on the last business day of the month
- Unrealised FX gains are credited to Account 8040 (Foreign Exchange Gain)
- Unrealised FX losses are debited to Account 7200 (Foreign Exchange Loss)

### 7.4 Realised FX Gains/Losses

- On settlement of foreign currency transactions, the difference between the recorded amount and the settlement amount is recognised as a realised gain or loss
- Realised gains: Account 8040
- Realised losses: Account 7200

### 7.5 Hedging

- No formal hedging instruments are currently used
- Natural hedging is achieved by matching USD receivables with USD payables where possible
- Any hedging arrangement requires Board approval

### 7.6 Tax Treatment (Uganda)

- FX gains are taxable income under the Income Tax Act
- FX losses are deductible if incurred in the production of income
- URA requires gains/losses to be computed using Bank of Uganda rates

## 8. Asset Capitalization

### 8.1 Capitalization Threshold

**An item is capitalised as a fixed asset if ALL of the following criteria are met:**

1. The cost exceeds **UGX 500,000** (five hundred thousand Uganda Shillings)
2. The useful life exceeds **12 months**
3. The item is held for use in the business (not for resale)
4. Future economic benefits are probable

### 8.2 What to Capitalise

| Capitalise | Do Not Capitalise |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Purchase price (net of discounts) | Repairs and maintenance |
| Import duties and non-refundable taxes | Items below UGX 500,000 threshold |
| Delivery and installation costs | Training costs on new equipment |
| Site preparation costs | Relocation costs |
| Professional fees directly attributable | Administrative and general overhead |
| Borrowing costs during construction (IAS 23) | Operating losses during commissioning |
| Testing and commissioning costs | Costs after asset is ready for use |

### 8.3 Capital Work in Progress (CWIP)

- Assets under construction are recorded in Account 1580 (CWIP)
- No depreciation is charged on CWIP
- CWIP is transferred to the appropriate asset class upon commissioning
- Monthly CWIP report must be prepared showing project costs vs budget

## 9. Depreciation and Amortization

### 9.1 Method

**Straight-line method** is applied to all tangible and intangible assets.

### 9.2 Depreciation Rates (Aligned with URA Rates)

| Asset Class | Account | Useful Life | Annual Rate | URA Rate |
|------------|---------|------------|-------------|----------|
| Buildings | 1510 | 20 years | 5% | 5% |
| Motor Vehicles | 1520 | 5 years | 20% | 20% |
| Plant & Machinery | 1530 | 8 years | 12.5% | 12.5% |
| Office Furniture & Fittings | 1540 | 8 years | 12.5% | 12.5% |
| Computer Equipment & IT | 1550 | 4 years | 25% | 25% (pooled) |
| Solar/Energy Equipment | 1560 | 10 years | 10% | 12.5% (note 1) |
| Leasehold Improvements | 1570 | Shorter of lease term or 10 years | Varies | Varies |
| Software | 1600 | 4 years | 25% | N/A |
| Licenses & Permits | 1610 | License period | Varies | N/A |

**Note 1:** Solar equipment is depreciated at 10% for IFRS purposes (reflecting economic life). URA allows 12.5% under plant & machinery. The difference creates a temporary deferred tax adjustment.

### 9.3 Depreciation Rules

- Depreciation commences from the month the asset is available for use
- Depreciation ceases on the earlier of: disposal date or date asset is classified as held for sale
- Residual value is assumed to be nil unless evidence supports otherwise
- Land is not depreciated
- Depreciation is charged to the cost center where the asset is deployed
- Asset useful lives are reviewed at each reporting date

### 9.4 Impairment

- Assets are assessed for impairment indicators at each reporting date (IAS 36)
- If indicators exist, a formal impairment test is performed
- Impairment losses are recognised in Account 7400
- Reversal of impairment is permitted (except for goodwill) up to the original carrying amount

## 10. Intercompany Accounting

### 10.1 Principles

- All intercompany transactions must be at arm's length (transfer pricing rules apply)
- Intercompany invoices must be raised within 5 business days of service delivery
- All intercompany balances must be confirmed and reconciled monthly
- Intercompany balances are eliminated upon consolidation

### 10.2 Intercompany Account Structure

| Account | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| 1101 | Intercompany Receivable |
| 2001 | Intercompany Payable |
| 4900 | Intercompany Revenue (Elimination) |
| 5900 | Intercompany COGS (Elimination) |

### 10.3 Transfer Pricing

- Management fees charged by Corporate to business lines at cost plus a margin of 5%–10%
- Shared services (IT, HR, Finance) allocated based on headcount or revenue proportion
- Asset transfers between entities at fair market value with proper documentation
- All transfer pricing arrangements documented in intercompany agreements

### 10.4 Intercompany Reconciliation Process

1. By BD3 each month, each entity submits intercompany balance confirmations
2. Financial Controller reconciles all intercompany positions by BD5
3. Discrepancies above UGX 1,000,000 must be investigated and resolved before month-end close
4. Unresolved discrepancies are escalated to CFO

## 11. Inventory Accounting

### 11.1 Measurement

- Inventory is measured at the lower of cost and net realisable value (IAS 2)
- Cost includes purchase price, import duties, transport, handling, and other directly attributable costs
- Trade discounts and rebates are deducted from cost

### 11.2 Cost Formulas

| Business Line | Cost Formula |
|--------------|-------------|
| Retail (RETL) | Weighted Average Cost |
| Energy (ENRG) — Solar Products | Weighted Average Cost |
| Logistics (LGST) — Spare Parts & Fuel | First-In, First-Out (FIFO) |
| Real Estate (REST) — Construction Materials | Specific Identification |

### 11.3 Inventory Write-Down

- Slow-moving inventory (no movement for 180 days) must be reviewed for write-down
- Obsolete inventory (no movement for 365 days) must be written off or provided for at 100%
- Write-downs require Financial Controller approval; write-offs above UGX 5,000,000 require CFO approval
- Account 5950 is used for inventory write-downs and shrinkage

### 11.4 Inventory Counts

- Perpetual inventory system maintained in ERP
- Cycle counts performed monthly (minimum 20% of SKUs)
- Full physical count performed at year-end (31 December)
- Count results reconciled to book records within 5 business days
- Variances above 2% of inventory value are investigated

## 12. Provisions and Contingencies (IAS 37)

### 12.1 Provisions

A provision is recognised when:
1. There is a present obligation (legal or constructive)
2. An outflow of resources is probable (>50%)
3. A reliable estimate can be made

Common provisions:
- Warranty provision (Energy — solar product warranties)
- Gratuity / end of service provision
- Legal claims
- Restructuring costs

### 12.2 Contingent Liabilities

Disclosed in notes to financial statements but not recognised. Examples include pending litigation, guarantees, and tax disputes.

## 13. Leases (IFRS 16)

### 13.1 Lessee Accounting

- All leases above 12 months and above UGX 5,000,000 annual value are recorded on the balance sheet
- Right-of-Use (ROU) asset recognised in Account 1800
- Lease liability recognised in Accounts 2080 (current) and 2530 (non-current)
- ROU asset depreciated over the shorter of the lease term and useful life
- Lease liability measured at present value of remaining lease payments using the incremental borrowing rate

### 13.2 Exemptions Applied

- Short-term leases (12 months or less): expensed on a straight-line basis
- Low-value assets (below UGX 5,000,000): expensed on a straight-line basis

### 13.3 Lessor Accounting (Real Estate)

- Operating leases: rental income recognised on a straight-line basis
- Finance leases: assessed case-by-case if substantially all risks and rewards are transferred

### 14.7 Electronic Fiscal Receipting and Invoicing Solution (EFRIS)

- All VAT-registered taxpayers must issue invoices through EFRIS
- The Company must integrate its ERP/POS with URA EFRIS system
- All sales invoices, credit notes, and debit notes must be transmitted electronically
- Non-compliance attracts penalties of UGX 20,000,000 or imprisonment

## 15. Financial Instruments (IFRS 9)

### 15.1 Classification

- Trade receivables: measured at amortised cost (simplified approach for expected credit losses)
- Bank deposits: measured at amortised cost
- Equity investments: measured at fair value through profit or loss (unless FVOCI election)

### 15.2 Expected Credit Losses (ECL)

A provision matrix is applied to trade receivables:

| Aging Bucket | ECL Rate |
|-------------|----------|
| Current (0–30 days) | 1% |
| 31–60 days | 5% |
| 61–90 days | 15% |
| 91–180 days | 40% |
| 181–365 days | 75% |
| Over 365 days | 100% |

Individual assessment performed for balances above UGX 50,000,000.

## 16. Related Party Transactions (IAS 24)

- All related party transactions are disclosed in financial statements
- Transactions must be at arm's length
- Director loans and advances require Board approval
- Related party register maintained by Company Secretary and Finance
- Quarterly disclosure to Board Finance Committee

## 17. Events After the Reporting Period (IAS 10)

- **Adjusting events:** Financial statements adjusted for events that provide evidence of conditions existing at the reporting date
- **Non-adjusting events:** Disclosed in notes if material
- Cut-off date for adjusting events: date financial statements are authorised for issue by the Board
