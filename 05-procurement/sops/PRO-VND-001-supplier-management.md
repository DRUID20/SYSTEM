## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the end-to-end process for managing the supplier lifecycle at Awdeer Investments Limited. It covers supplier identification, pre-qualification, registration onto the Approved Supplier List (ASL), ongoing performance evaluation, relationship management, and supplier suspension or removal. The objective is to maintain a pool of reliable, competitive, and compliant suppliers that deliver consistent value for money.

---

## 2. Scope

**In Scope:**
- All external suppliers of goods, services, and works
- Supplier pre-qualification and registration
- Approved Supplier List (ASL) maintenance
- Supplier performance evaluation and scoring
- Supplier categorization and segmentation
- Supplier relationship management
- Supplier suspension, removal, and blacklisting
- Supplier conflict of interest management

**Out of Scope:**
- Employee contractors and consultants on payroll (governed by HR-CON-001)
- Inter-company supplier relationships (governed by FIN-IC-001)
- Customer management (governed by SAL-CRM-001)

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### 5.1 Supplier Identification and Pre-Qualification

#### Lean Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Identify need for new supplier (category gap, supplier failure, new product/service need) | OM | As needed |
| 2 | Source potential suppliers through: market research, industry contacts, online directories, supplier referrals, Uganda Manufacturers Association, trade shows | OM | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Request supplier to submit: business registration certificate, TIN certificate, URA tax clearance, bank reference letter, 3 trade references, company profile/capability statement, signed Conflict of Interest Declaration | OM | 1–2 weeks |
| 4 | Review submitted documents for completeness and authenticity | OM | 2–3 business days |
| 5 | Verify TIN with URA (online or written confirmation) | OM / FM | 1–2 business days |
| 6 | Contact trade references (minimum 2 of 3) | OM | 3–5 business days |
| 7 | Conduct site visit for suppliers expected to provide >UGX 10,000,000 annually | OM | 1 business day |
| 8 | Complete Pre-Qualification Assessment checklist | OM | 1 business day |
| 9 | Recommend to MD for ASL addition or rejection | OM | 1 business day |
| 10 | MD approves or rejects | MD | 1–2 business days |
| 11 | If approved: add to ASL spreadsheet with category, contact details, bank details, TIN, approval date | OM | Same day |
| 12 | Notify supplier of registration status | OM | 1 business day |

#### Controlled Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Identify need for new supplier based on: category analysis, supplier performance gaps, new business requirements, ASL refresh cycle | PO / PM | As needed or per schedule |
| 2 | Issue Supplier Registration Invitation or receive unsolicited application | PO | As needed |
| 3 | Supplier completes registration form in supplier portal or submits documentation pack: certificate of incorporation from URSB, TIN certificate and URA tax clearance, audited financial statements (last 2 years for Tier 3+ suppliers), bank reference letter, minimum 3 trade references with contact details, company profile with capability statement, quality certifications (ISO, industry-specific), insurance certificates (where applicable), signed Supplier Code of Conduct, signed Conflict of Interest Declaration, director details (names, IDs, other directorships) | Supplier | 2–3 weeks |
| 4 | Procurement Officer conducts initial document review and completeness check | PO | 2 business days |
| 5 | Financial due diligence: FM reviews financial statements, credit checks, bank reference verification | FM | 3–5 business days |
| 6 | Legal due diligence: Legal Officer checks URSB registration, director backgrounds, litigation history, sanctions screening | Legal | 3–5 business days |
| 7 | Reference checks: contact minimum 3 trade references | PO | 5 business days |
| 8 | Cross-reference directors against Awdeer employee/director list (conflict of interest check) | Compliance | 2 business days |
| 9 | Site visit and capability assessment for Tier 3+ suppliers | PM + Technical assessor | 1–2 business days |
| 10 | Procurement Manager compiles Pre-Qualification Report with recommendation | PM | 2 business days |
| 11 | MD approves ASL addition | MD | 2–3 business days |
| 12 | Procurement Officer creates supplier master record in ERP with: legal name, TIN, bank details, contact details, category codes, payment terms, WHT applicability, approved tier level | PO | 1 business day |
| 13 | Notify supplier of approved status and provide Supplier Handbook | PO | 1 business day |

### 5.2 Supplier Categorization and Segmentation

| Category | Criteria | Management Approach |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| **Strategic** | High spend (>UGX 100M/year), critical to operations, limited alternatives | Quarterly review meetings, joint improvement plans, long-term contracts |
| **Leverage** | High spend, many alternatives available | Active competitive sourcing, regular price benchmarking |
| **Bottleneck** | Low spend, limited alternatives, high risk if supply fails | Maintain safety stock, develop alternative suppliers, monitor closely |
| **Routine** | Low spend, many alternatives | Streamline ordering, consolidate suppliers, minimize admin effort |

### 5.3 Supplier Performance Evaluation

#### Lean Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Gather performance data for evaluation period: delivery records, quality/inspection records, pricing history, responsiveness/communication records, compliance status | OM | Annually (Q4) |
| 2 | Complete Supplier Evaluation Scorecard (PRO-TPL-006) for each active supplier | OM | 2 weeks for all suppliers |
| 3 | Score each supplier against weighted criteria: delivery timeliness (25%), quality compliance (25%), price competitiveness (20%), responsiveness (15%), documentation accuracy (10%), compliance (5%) | OM | Per scorecard |
| 4 | Classify supplier: Excellent (90–100), Good (75–89), Satisfactory (60–74), Poor (<60) | OM | Per scorecard |
| 5 | Review results with Finance Manager | OM + FM | 1 meeting |
| 6 | Communicate results to suppliers: top performers — recognition letter, satisfactory — no action, poor — improvement plan letter | OM | 1 week |
| 7 | MD reviews and approves overall ASL status | MD | 1–2 business days |
| 8 | Update ASL with evaluation results and next review dates | OM | Same day |

#### Controlled Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | ERP generates supplier performance data report: on-time delivery rate, quality acceptance rate (first-pass), pricing vs market benchmark, response time metrics, invoice accuracy rate, compliance status (tax, insurance, certifications) | ERP System / PO | Monthly (data collection) |
| 2 | Procurement Officer compiles monthly Supplier Performance Dashboard | PO | Monthly by BD 10 |
| 3 | Procurement Manager reviews dashboard and flags underperformers | PM | Monthly |
| 4 | Quarterly Supplier Review Meetings for Strategic and Bottleneck suppliers: review scorecard, discuss issues, agree improvement actions, review market conditions | PM + relevant DH | Quarterly |
| 5 | Annual formal evaluation using Supplier Evaluation Scorecard (PRO-TPL-006) for ALL active suppliers | PO (data) / PM (evaluation) | Annually (Q4) |
| 6 | Evaluation Committee reviews results for Tier 3+ suppliers | PM + FM + relevant DHs | Annual review meeting |
| 7 | Classification and action: Excellent (90–100) — preferred status, strategic partnership consideration. Good (75–89) — maintain, no action. Satisfactory (60–74) — formal improvement plan (90 days). Poor (<60) — warning letter, 90-day improvement or suspension | PM | Per evaluation |
| 8 | Improvement plans issued to underperforming suppliers with specific targets and timeline | PM | Within 5 BD of evaluation |
| 9 | MD approves: ASL updates, suspensions, preferred status changes | MD | 3–5 business days |
| 10 | Update ERP supplier master with evaluation scores and status | PO | Same day as approval |
| 11 | Formal communication to all evaluated suppliers | PO | Within 1 week |
| 12 | Publish annual Supplier Performance Summary to management | PM | Q1 of following year |

### 5.4 Supplier Issue Management

| Step | Action | Lean | Controlled |
|------|--------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Issue identified (late delivery, quality problem, billing error, compliance lapse) | OM records in log | PO records in ERP |
| 2 | Immediate notification to supplier | OM — phone/email | PM — formal written notice |
| 3 | Supplier provides root cause and corrective action within 5 BD | Supplier responds | Supplier submits formal CAPA |
| 4 | Evaluate supplier response | OM assesses | PM + DH assess |
| 5 | Determine action: accept CAPA, issue warning, apply penalty, escalate | OM decides (MD for penalties) | PM recommends; MD decides |
| 6 | Monitor corrective action implementation | OM tracks | PO tracks in ERP |
| 7 | Close issue and update supplier record | OM updates log | PO closes in ERP |

### 5.5 Supplier Suspension and Removal

| Step | Action | Lean | Controlled |
|------|--------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Trigger identified: two consecutive poor evaluations, fraud/corruption, persistent non-compliance, critical incident | OM identifies | PM identifies; system flags |
| 2 | Prepare Suspension/Removal Recommendation with evidence | OM prepares memo | PM prepares formal report |
| 3 | Review by Finance Manager (outstanding POs, balances) | FM reviews | FM + AP review |
| 4 | Legal review (contract implications, obligations) | N/A (Lean) | Legal Officer reviews |
| 5 | MD approval for suspension/removal | MD approves | MD approves (Board for fraud >UGX 50M) |
| 6 | Issue formal Suspension/Removal Notice to supplier | OM sends letter | PM sends registered letter |
| 7 | Manage transition: identify alternative suppliers, re-route open POs | OM manages | PM + PO manage |
| 8 | Update ASL — mark as suspended/removed with reason and date | OM updates spreadsheet | PO updates ERP (blocks new POs) |
| 9 | Blacklist (for fraud/corruption): add to Blacklisted Suppliers Register | OM maintains list | Compliance Officer maintains register |
| 10 | Archive supplier file with all evaluation and incident records | OM files | PO archives in ERP |

### 5.6 Supplier Reactivation

| Condition | Process | Approval |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Suspension period expired + evidence of improvement | Re-evaluation using scorecard; must score ≥75 | PM + MD |
| Suspension for non-compliance + compliance restored | Verify all certifications current; probationary period (6 months) | PM + FM + MD |
| Blacklisted supplier | Not eligible for reactivation for minimum 3 years; requires Board approval | Board |

## 8. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Target | Measurement Method | Frequency |
|-----|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| ASL coverage (% of spend through ASL) | ≥ 80% | ASL supplier spend / total procurement spend | Quarterly |
| Supplier evaluation completion rate | 100% of active suppliers | Evaluations completed / active suppliers | Annually |
| Average supplier score | ≥ 75 | Average across all evaluated suppliers | Annually |
| New supplier onboarding time | ≤ 15 BD (Lean) / ≤ 20 BD (Controlled) | Average days from application to ASL approval | Quarterly |
| Supplier concentration risk | No single supplier >30% of category spend | Spend analysis by supplier per category | Quarterly |
| Conflict of interest declarations collected | 100% | Declarations received / required declarations | Annually |
| Supplier issues resolved within SLA | ≥ 90% | Issues resolved within 5 BD / total issues | Monthly |
| Supplier suspension rate | ≤ 5% of ASL annually | Suspended suppliers / total ASL count | Annually |
| Preferred supplier utilization | ≥ 50% of spend through preferred suppliers | Preferred supplier spend / total spend | Quarterly |
| Supplier diversity (per category) | ≥ 3 active suppliers per category | Count of suppliers per procurement category | Semi-annually |
