## 1. Purpose

To establish the standard operating procedure for preventing, detecting, and responding to bribery and corruption at Awdeer Investments Limited, in compliance with the Anti-Corruption Act 2009 of Uganda and in alignment with international best practices.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- All directors, officers, and employees of Awdeer Investments Limited
- All contractors, consultants, and agents acting on behalf of the company
- All business partners, joint venture partners, and intermediaries
- All business lines and operations across all locations

This SOP covers:

- Prevention of bribery (giving and receiving)
- Gifts and hospitality management
- Conflict of interest declarations
- Third-party due diligence (agents, intermediaries, consultants)
- Political and charitable contributions
- Investigation of suspected bribery or corruption
- Reporting to relevant authorities (Inspectorate of Government, Uganda Police)

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Process A: Gifts and Hospitality Management

#### Step A1: Receive or Offer Gift/Hospitality

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Staff member receives or intends to offer a gift or hospitality |
| **Who** | Any staff member |
| **Thresholds** | See main document Section 6.3 for value thresholds |
| **Rule** | When in doubt, declare it. Never accept cash gifts regardless of amount |

#### Step A2: Assess Against Thresholds

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Determine the value and assess against the Gifts and Hospitality Thresholds |
| **Who** | Staff member with line manager |
| **Token items (up to UGX 100,000)** | May accept; record if frequent from same source |
| **Modest (UGX 100,001–200,000)** | Notify line manager; record in gift register |
| **Significant (UGX 200,001–500,000)** | Pre-approval from MD required |
| **Above UGX 500,000** | Decline and report to Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Government officials** | Pre-approval from MD for any value |

#### Step A3: Record in Gift Register

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Record the gift/hospitality in the company gift register |
| **Who** | Staff member (report); Legal/Compliance Officer (record) |
| **Details Recorded** | Date, staff member name, counterparty name and organisation, description, estimated value, context/reason, action taken (accepted/declined/returned), approval obtained |
| **Timeline** | Within 3 business days of receipt/offer |

#### Step A4: Quarterly Gift Register Review

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Review gift register for patterns or concerns |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Look For** | Frequent gifts from single source, gifts around contract award dates, gifts exceeding thresholds, patterns suggesting quid pro quo |
| **Escalation** | Report concerns to MD |

---

### Process B: Conflict of Interest Declaration

#### Step B1: Annual Declaration

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | All staff and directors complete annual Conflict of Interest Declaration |
| **Who** | All staff (complete); Legal/Compliance Officer (collect and review) |
| **Form** | LEG-TPL-003 Conflict of Interest Declaration Form |
| **Timeline** | January of each year (or within 30 days of joining the company) |
| **Content** | Personal business interests, family members in business relationships with the company, directorships/shareholdings in other entities, any other potential conflicts |

#### Step B2: Ad-Hoc Declaration

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Declare any new conflict as soon as it arises during the year |
| **Who** | Any staff member or director |
| **How** | Submit updated COI declaration to Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Timeline** | Within 5 business days of the conflict arising |

#### Step B3: Review and Manage Conflicts

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Review all declarations and determine appropriate management actions |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer (review); MD (approve management actions) |
| **Actions** | No conflict identified (file), minor conflict (note and monitor), material conflict (recuse from decisions, reassign duties), serious conflict (refer to MD/Board for resolution) |
| **Record** | Management decision recorded against each declared conflict |

---

### Process C: Third-Party Anti-Bribery Due Diligence

#### Step C1: Identify Need for Due Diligence

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Before engaging agents, intermediaries, consultants, or JV partners, assess bribery risk |
| **Who** | Requesting department + Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Triggers** | New agent/intermediary appointment, government-facing contracts, high-value consultant engagements, joint ventures |

#### Step C2: Conduct Due Diligence

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Perform ABC-specific due diligence on the third party |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Checks** | Business registration and ownership, director/beneficial owner background, history of corruption allegations or convictions, sanctions screening, references from reputable organisations, site visit (for high-risk engagements) |
| **Timeline** | Within 10 business days |

#### Step C3: Risk Assessment and Approval

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Assess the bribery risk level and obtain appropriate approval |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer (assess); MD (approve) |
| **Low Risk** | Standard engagement terms with ABC clauses |
| **Medium Risk** | Enhanced monitoring, ABC training for the third party |
| **High Risk** | MD approval required; enhanced contractual protections; audit rights; ongoing monitoring |
| **Unacceptable** | Do not engage; report reasons |

#### Step C4: Contractual Protections

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Include ABC clauses in all third-party contracts |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Required Clauses** | Anti-bribery warranty and undertaking, right to audit, obligation to report suspected bribery, termination for ABC breach, compliance with applicable anti-corruption laws |

---

### Process D: Investigation of Suspected Bribery or Corruption

#### Step D1: Receive Report

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Receive report of suspected bribery or corruption |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer (or MD if Legal/Compliance Officer is implicated) |
| **Source** | Internal report, whistleblowing channel, audit finding, external information |
| **Timeline** | Log within 24 hours of receipt |

#### Step D2: Preliminary Assessment

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Assess credibility and severity of the allegation |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer + MD |
| **Determine** | Is there a prima facie case? What is the potential impact? Is immediate action needed (suspension, asset protection)? Should authorities be notified? |
| **Timeline** | Within 3 business days |
| **Legal Counsel** | Engage external legal counsel for serious allegations |

#### Step D3: Formal Investigation

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Conduct a thorough investigation |
| **Who** | Investigation team appointed by MD (may include external investigators) |
| **Process** | Preserve evidence, interview witnesses, review documents and records, obtain expert advice where needed, maintain confidentiality |
| **Timeline** | Within 30 business days (extendable with MD approval) |
| **Documentation** | Detailed investigation report with findings, evidence, and recommendations |

#### Step D4: Outcome and Corrective Action

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Determine outcome and implement corrective measures |
| **Who** | MD (or Board if MD implicated) |
| **Possible Outcomes** | Allegation unsubstantiated (close with record), policy violation confirmed (disciplinary action per HR policy), criminal conduct suspected (report to Uganda Police / Inspectorate of Government), civil recovery action (through Legal/Compliance Officer) |
| **Systemic Issues** | If investigation reveals control weaknesses, implement process improvements |

#### Step D5: Reporting

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Report outcome to appropriate stakeholders |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Internal** | MD and Board (for material matters), reporter/whistleblower (outcome without details, if not anonymous) |
| **External** | Inspectorate of Government, Uganda Police (if criminal), Financial Intelligence Authority (if money laundering element) |
| **Timeline** | External reporting per statutory timelines. Internal reporting within 5 business days of investigation conclusion |

## 9. Lean vs Controlled Version

### Lean Version (Small Team < 15 people)

| Aspect | Lean Approach |
|--------|---------------|
| **Policy Communication** | MD communicates policy at annual staff meeting and in employment contracts |
| **Training** | MD delivers brief ABC awareness session annually (30 minutes). New joiners receive policy during induction |
| **Gift Register** | Simple spreadsheet maintained by HR & Admin Officer |
| **COI Declarations** | Simple one-page form completed annually. MD reviews all declarations |
| **Third-Party Due Diligence** | Basic background check for agents/intermediaries. MD approves all agent appointments |
| **Investigations** | MD leads investigation with external lawyer support for serious matters |
| **Reporting** | MD reports any incidents to Board (informal). No formal annual report unless incidents occur |
| **Tools** | Spreadsheet, physical forms, simple filing |

### Controlled Version (Mid-size Team 15-100+ people)

| Aspect | Controlled Approach |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Policy Communication** | Formal ABC policy document. Annual acknowledgment signed by all staff. Posters in common areas |
| **Training** | Structured ABC training programme (classroom/online). Role-specific training for high-risk functions (procurement, sales, government relations). Annual refresher |
| **Gift Register** | ERP-based gift register with automated alerts and quarterly reports |
| **COI Declarations** | Online COI declaration system. Legal/Compliance Officer reviews all. MD reviews flagged conflicts. Board reviews director COIs |
| **Third-Party Due Diligence** | Formal risk-based due diligence process. Enhanced due diligence for high-risk third parties. Sanctions and PEP screening. External due diligence providers for complex cases |
| **Investigations** | Formal investigation protocol. External investigators for serious matters. Legal counsel engaged. Board updated on material matters |
| **Reporting** | Quarterly ABC dashboard to MD. Annual ABC compliance report to Board. Statistics on training, declarations, incidents |
| **Tools** | ERP compliance module, online training platform, case management system |

## 10. Key Offences Under Anti-Corruption Act 2009

| Offence | Section | Maximum Penalty |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| Corruption | Section 2 | 10 years imprisonment |
| Embezzlement | Section 19 | 14 years imprisonment |
| Causing financial loss | Section 20 | 14 years imprisonment |
| Abuse of office | Section 11 | 7 years imprisonment |
| Bribery of a public official | Section 5 | 10 years imprisonment |
| Bribery by a public official | Section 7 | 10 years imprisonment |
| Conflict of interest (public officials) | Section 9 | 7 years imprisonment |
| Fraud | Penal Code Act | 7 years imprisonment |

> **Note:** Private sector corruption is also covered. Any person who corruptly gives or receives a benefit commits an offence.
