## Instructions

This matrix defines the financial and non-financial authority limits for each role within Awdeer Investments Limited. All amounts are in Uganda Shillings (UGX). This matrix must be:

1. Approved by the Board of Directors annually
2. Acknowledged in writing by each authorised person
3. Reviewed whenever there is a change in organisational structure or key personnel
4. Available to all staff who initiate or approve transactions

**Key principles:**
- No person may approve their own expenditure or transactions
- Authority limits are per transaction (not per payment instalment)
- Cumulative transactions with the same counterparty within 30 days are treated as a single transaction
- Dual-signature controls apply above specified thresholds
- Any transaction exceeding a person's authority must be escalated to the next level
