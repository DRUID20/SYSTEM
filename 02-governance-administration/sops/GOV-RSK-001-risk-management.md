## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the process for identifying, assessing, treating, monitoring, and reporting risks across Awdeer Investments Limited and its subsidiaries. It ensures a consistent, structured approach to risk management aligned with the company's risk appetite and strategic objectives.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP covers:

- Enterprise-wide risk identification and assessment
- Risk register maintenance and updates
- Quarterly risk review process
- Incident and near-miss reporting
- Risk escalation procedures
- Reporting to management and the Board

It applies to all departments, subsidiaries, and business activities of Awdeer Investments Limited.

**Risk categories covered:**
- Strategic risks
- Financial risks
- Operational risks
- Compliance / legal risks
- External / environmental risks

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

### 3.1 Key Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Board / Audit & Risk Committee** | Sets risk appetite; oversees risk management framework; reviews top risks quarterly |
| **Managing Director (MD)** | Accountable for enterprise risk management; chairs management risk review; escalates critical risks to Board |
| **CFO / Risk Coordinator** | Coordinates risk management process; consolidates risk register; facilitates quarterly reviews; reports to Board |

| **Company Secretary (CoSec)** | Ensures risk reporting is included in Board packs; documents risk-related Board decisions |
| **All Staff** | Report risks, incidents, and near-misses to their line manager; comply with risk controls |

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Phase 1: Risk Identification (Ongoing + Quarterly)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Each HOD / Risk Owner reviews their area for new, changed, or emerging risks | HOD / Risk Owner | Ongoing; formal review in Week 1 of last month of each quarter |
| 1.2 | Consider all risk categories: strategic, financial, operational, compliance, external | HOD / Risk Owner | — |
| 1.3 | Review recent incidents, near-misses, audit findings, and regulatory changes for risk implications | HOD / Risk Owner | — |
| 1.4 | Document new risks using the Risk Register template (GOV-TPL-006) with: Risk ID, description, category, cause, potential consequence | HOD / Risk Owner | Within 3 business days |
| 1.5 | Submit new/updated risk entries to CFO / Risk Coordinator | HOD / Risk Owner | Week 1 of last month in quarter |

**Risk identification sources:**
- Departmental operations review
- Financial performance analysis
- Customer / stakeholder feedback
- Regulatory updates
- Industry news and competitor analysis
- Staff suggestions and observations
- Audit reports and findings
- Insurance claims history

### Phase 2: Risk Assessment (Quarterly)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | For each risk, assess **Likelihood** using the 1-5 scale (see Governance Framework Section 8.3) | HOD / Risk Owner | Week 1 |
| 2.2 | For each risk, assess **Impact** using the 1-5 scale (financial thresholds in UGX) | HOD / Risk Owner | Week 1 |
| 2.3 | Calculate **Risk Score** = Likelihood x Impact | HOD / Risk Owner | — |
| 2.4 | Determine **Risk Rating**: Low (1-4), Medium (5-9), High (10-15), Critical (16-25) | HOD / Risk Owner | — |
| 2.5 | Assess **current controls** in place and rate their effectiveness: Effective / Partially effective / Ineffective | HOD / Risk Owner | — |
| 2.6 | Determine **Residual Risk Score** (risk score after considering existing controls) | HOD / Risk Owner | — |
| 2.7 | Compare residual risk against the company's **risk appetite** to determine if additional action is needed | CFO | Week 2 |

**Likelihood Scale (Summary):**

| Score | Level | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | < 5% |
| 2 | Unlikely | 5-20% |
| 3 | Possible | 20-50% |
| 4 | Likely | 50-80% |
| 5 | Almost Certain | > 80% |

**Impact Scale (Summary):**

| Score | Level | Financial Impact (UGX) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Negligible | < 5M |
| 2 | Minor | 5M - 25M |
| 3 | Moderate | 25M - 100M |
| 4 | Major | 100M - 500M |
| 5 | Critical | > 500M |

### Phase 3: Risk Treatment (Quarterly)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | For each risk exceeding appetite, select treatment strategy: Accept, Mitigate, Transfer, or Avoid | HOD / Risk Owner | Week 2 |
| 3.2 | For **Mitigate**: Define specific actions, responsible person, deadline, and expected effect on risk score | HOD / Risk Owner | Week 2 |
| 3.3 | For **Transfer**: Identify insurance coverage, contractual transfer, or outsourcing arrangement | HOD / CFO | Week 2 |
| 3.4 | For **Avoid**: Recommend cessation of the risk-generating activity to MD | HOD / MD | Week 2 |
| 3.5 | For **Accept**: Document rationale for acceptance; ensure within risk appetite | HOD / CFO | Week 2 |
| 3.6 | Assign a **target risk score** (what the risk score should be after treatment) | HOD / Risk Owner | Week 2 |
| 3.7 | Set review date for each action item | HOD / Risk Owner | — |
| 3.8 | Record all treatment plans in the risk register | HOD / Risk Owner | — |

### Phase 4: Quarterly Risk Review Meeting

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | CFO consolidates all risk register updates into a single master register | CFO | Week 2 of last month in quarter |
| 4.2 | CFO prepares quarterly risk summary report: Top 10 risks, new risks, closed risks, overdue actions, risk heat map | CFO | Week 2 |
| 4.3 | MD convenes management risk review meeting | MD | Week 3 |
| 4.4 | Each HOD presents their risk updates (new risks, changes, action progress) | HODs | At meeting |
| 4.5 | Management discusses and challenges risk assessments; agrees on priorities | All management | At meeting |
| 4.6 | MD confirms which risks require Board attention | MD | At meeting |
| 4.7 | CFO updates risk register with outcomes of management review | CFO | Within 2 business days |
| 4.8 | CoSec includes risk report in Board pack | CoSec | 7 days before Board meeting |

### Phase 5: Board / Audit & Risk Committee Review (Quarterly)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | CFO / Risk Coordinator presents risk report to Board or Audit & Risk Committee | CFO | At Board / Committee meeting |
| 5.2 | Board reviews top risks, risk heat map, and mitigation progress | Board | At meeting |
| 5.3 | Board provides direction on risk appetite, specific risks, or required actions | Board | At meeting |
| 5.4 | CoSec records risk-related Board decisions and actions | CoSec | At meeting |
| 5.5 | Actions assigned and tracked via Board action tracker | CoSec | Post-meeting |

### Phase 6: Incident & Near-Miss Management

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Staff member identifies an incident (actual loss, breach, failure) or near-miss | Staff member | Immediately |
| 6.2 | Staff member reports to line manager verbally and in writing | Staff member | Within 4 hours (verbal) / 24 hours (written) |
| 6.3 | Line manager assesses severity and notifies HOD | Line manager | Within 24 hours |
| 6.4 | HOD notifies CoSec / CFO for logging in incident register | HOD | Within 24 hours |
| 6.5 | CoSec logs incident in the incident register with: date, description, category, immediate actions taken | CoSec | Same day |
| 6.6 | **For material incidents** (financial impact > UGX 25M or regulatory/reputational risk): MD notified immediately | HOD | Immediately |
| 6.7 | **For critical incidents** (financial impact > UGX 100M or threatens business continuity): Board Chairperson notified | MD | Within 4 hours |
| 6.8 | Root cause analysis conducted | HOD / Assigned investigator | Within 5 business days |
| 6.9 | Corrective and preventive actions identified | HOD | Within 5 business days |
| 6.10 | Actions implemented and tracked | HOD | Per action deadlines |
| 6.11 | Risk register updated to reflect incident and any changed risk scores | CFO / HOD | Within 5 business days |
| 6.12 | Incident summary included in next quarterly risk report | CFO | Next quarterly report |
| 6.13 | Lessons learned shared with relevant teams | HOD / CoSec | Within 10 business days |

### Phase 7: Annual Risk Framework Review

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | CoSec coordinates annual review of the risk management framework | CoSec | Q4 (October-December) |
| 7.2 | CFO prepares annual risk management report: summary of all risks, incidents, trend analysis, framework effectiveness | CFO | November |
| 7.3 | MD reviews and recommends any changes to risk appetite or framework | MD | November |
| 7.4 | Board reviews and approves: updated risk appetite, any framework changes, risk management priorities for next year | Board | December Board meeting |
| 7.5 | Updated framework published and communicated | CoSec | January |

## 6. Controls & Approvals

| Control | Description | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk appetite boundary** | No risk rated "Critical" (16-25) may be accepted without explicit Board approval | Board / MD |
| **Quarterly review mandate** | Risk register must be reviewed at least quarterly; no exceptions | MD / CFO |
| **Dual assessment** | Risk scores above 15 must be validated by CFO before inclusion in Board report | CFO |
| **Action tracking** | All mitigation actions must have an owner and deadline; overdue actions escalated to MD | CFO |
| **Incident reporting** | All material incidents reported to MD within 24 hours; critical incidents to Board within 4 hours | HODs / MD |
| **Root cause analysis** | All incidents with financial impact > UGX 5M require documented root cause analysis | HOD |
| **Segregation** | Risk owners cannot rate their own risk as "Accepted" without CFO/MD validation | CFO / MD |
| **Independence** | Audit & Risk Committee includes at least 1 non-executive director | Board |
| **Annual review** | Risk framework and appetite reviewed by Board annually | CoSec / Board |
