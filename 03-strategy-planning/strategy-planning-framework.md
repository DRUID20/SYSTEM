## 1. Purpose & Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This framework establishes how Awdeer Investments Limited sets strategic direction, translates strategy into operational plans, tracks performance, manages its project portfolio, and evaluates investment decisions. It ensures that every shilling spent and every initiative launched is aligned with the company's strategic goals.

### 1.2 Scope

This framework applies to:
- All business lines (Energy/Fuel, Real Estate, Logistics, Retail, Professional Services)
- All departments (Finance, Operations, Sales & Marketing, HR, IT, Procurement)
- All staff involved in planning, budgeting, performance tracking, or project execution
- All investment decisions above UGX 5,000,000

### 1.3 Objectives

| # | Objective |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | Align all departmental activities with the 3-year strategic plan |
| 2 | Ensure disciplined annual planning that integrates budgets, targets, and initiatives |
| 3 | Create a single source of truth for performance measurement (OKRs/KPIs) |
| 4 | Drive accountability through quarterly business reviews |
| 5 | Ensure projects are evaluated, prioritized, and tracked systematically |
| 6 | Provide a rigorous investment appraisal methodology for capital allocation |

## 2. Guiding Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Strategy-First | Every initiative must trace back to a strategic objective |
| Measurability | If it cannot be measured, it cannot be managed |
| Accountability | Every KPI, project, and initiative has one named owner |

| Discipline | Planning and review cycles run on schedule — no exceptions |

## 3. Annual Planning Cycle

### 3.1 Overview

The annual planning cycle runs from **October to December** each year, producing the operational plan and budget for the following financial year (January-December). The cycle integrates strategic review, financial budgeting, target-setting, and initiative planning into a single coordinated process.

### 3.2 Planning Timeline

| Phase | Period | Activities | Output | Owner |
|-------|--------|-----------|--------|-------|
| **Phase 1: Strategic Review** | October 1–15 | Review 3-year strategy, assess market conditions, evaluate prior year performance, SWOT refresh | Strategic Direction Paper | MD |
| **Phase 2: Target Cascading** | October 16–31 | MD sets company-level objectives; department heads draft departmental OKRs aligned to company goals | Draft OKR Sheets | MD + Dept Heads |
| **Phase 3: Budget Development** | November 1–20 | Each department builds bottom-up budgets; Finance consolidates; revenue forecasting by Sales | Draft Consolidated Budget | Finance Manager/CFO |
| **Phase 4: Initiative Planning** | November 1–20 (parallel with Phase 3) | Department heads propose key initiatives/projects with business cases | Initiative Register + Business Cases | Dept Heads |
| **Phase 5: Integration & Challenge** | November 21–30 | MD and Finance review all budgets and initiatives; challenge sessions with each department; resource arbitration | Revised Budget + Prioritized Initiatives | MD + Finance |
| **Phase 6: Board Approval** | December 1–15 | Present final Annual Plan + Budget to the Board for approval | Approved Annual Plan & Budget | MD (presents) / Board (approves) |
| **Phase 7: Cascade & Launch** | December 16–31 | Communicate approved plan to all staff; finalize individual KPIs; set up tracking dashboards | Signed KPI Scorecards, Configured Dashboards | HR + All Dept Heads |

### 3.3 Planning Inputs

| Input | Source | When |
|-------|--------|------|

## 4. OKR/KPI Framework

### 4.1 Framework Structure

Awdeer uses a hybrid **OKR + KPI** approach:

- **OKRs (Objectives & Key Results):** Used for strategic, transformational goals that push the company forward. Set quarterly, aligned to annual objectives.
- **KPIs (Key Performance Indicators):** Used for operational, ongoing metrics that ensure the business runs smoothly. Tracked monthly/weekly.

### 4.2 OKR Hierarchy

```
Company Vision (3-year)
    └── Annual Company Objectives (3–5)
            └── Quarterly Company OKRs (3–5 per quarter)
                    └── Department OKRs (2–3 per department per quarter)
                            └── Individual OKRs (1–2 per person per quarter)
```

### 4.3 OKR Rules

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Objective format | Qualitative, inspiring, time-bound. "Become the most trusted fuel distributor in Central Uganda by Q4" |
| Key Result format | Quantitative, measurable, verifiable. "Increase repeat customer rate from 45% to 65%" |
| Number of OKRs | 3–5 objectives per entity per quarter; 2–4 key results per objective |
| Scoring | 0.0 to 1.0 scale. 0.7–0.8 = target performance. 1.0 = stretch achieved |
| Cadence | Set at start of quarter; scored at end of quarter during QBR |
| Ownership | Every OKR has exactly one owner (person, not team) |

### 4.5 KPI Scoring Method

Each KPI is scored on a Red-Amber-Green (RAG) scale:

| Score | Colour | Meaning | Threshold |
|-------|--------|---------|-----------|
| 3 | Green | On or above target | ≥ 100% of target |
| 2 | Amber | Slightly below target; corrective action needed | 80–99% of target |
| 1 | Red | Significantly below target; escalation required | < 80% of target |

## 5. Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Process

### 5.1 Purpose

The QBR is the primary mechanism for holding the organization accountable to the annual plan. It provides a structured forum to review performance, address gaps, celebrate wins, and adjust course for the next quarter.

### 5.2 QBR Schedule

| Quarter | Review Period | QBR Date | Covers |
|---------|--------------|----------|--------|
| Q1 QBR | January–March | Third week of April | Q1 actuals, Q2 outlook |
| Q2 QBR (Mid-Year) | April–June | Third week of July | H1 actuals, full-year forecast revision |
| Q3 QBR | July–September | Third week of October | Q3 actuals, Q4 outlook, pre-planning for next year |
| Q4 QBR (Year-End) | October–December | Third week of January (following year) | Full year actuals, preliminary next-year review |

### 5.3 QBR Agenda

| # | Agenda Item | Duration | Presenter | Purpose |
|---|------------|----------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Opening & Review of Previous QBR Actions | 10 min | MD | Set context; track action completion |
| 2 | Financial Performance Review | 20 min | CFO/Finance Manager | P&L, cash flow, budget variance, key financial KPIs |
| 3 | Sales & Revenue Review | 15 min | Sales Manager | Pipeline, revenue by business line, customer metrics |
| 4 | Operations Review | 15 min | Operations Manager | Fulfilment, inventory, logistics, operational KPIs |
| 5 | HR & People Review | 10 min | HR Manager | Headcount, turnover, engagement, compliance |
| 6 | IT & Systems Update | 10 min | IT Administrator | System health, projects, security |
| 7 | Procurement & Vendor Review | 10 min | Procurement Manager | Spend analysis, supplier performance, savings |
| 8 | OKR Scoring & Review | 20 min | All Dept Heads | Score current quarter OKRs; present next quarter OKRs |
| 9 | Project Portfolio Status | 15 min | Project Sponsor/MD | Status of all active projects; gate decisions |
| 10 | Risk & Compliance Update | 10 min | MD/Legal | Top risks, mitigation status, compliance issues |
| 11 | Strategic Discussion Topic | 15 min | MD or Guest | Deep-dive on one strategic topic per QBR |

| | **Total** | **~2.5 hours** | | |

| Deliverable | Due | Responsible |

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|

| Revenue is ≥ 10% below budget at mid-year | Trigger cost containment review |
| Cash reserves fall below 2 months operating costs | Trigger liquidity crisis protocol |
| Any project is ≥ 30% over budget or ≥ 2 months behind schedule | Project escalated to MD for continue/cancel decision |

## 6. Project Portfolio Management

### 6.1 Overview

All strategic initiatives, capital projects, and significant operational improvements are managed through the project portfolio management (PPM) process. This ensures projects are properly evaluated, resourced, tracked, and delivered.

### 6.2 Project Classification

| Category | Budget Threshold | Approval Authority | Oversight |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Minor** | < UGX 25M | Department Head | Department Head |
| **Standard** | UGX 25M–100M | MD | MD + Finance |
| **Major** | UGX 100M–500M | MD + Board Notification | MD + Monthly Review |
| **Strategic** | > UGX 500M | Board Approval | Board + Monthly Steering |

### 6.3 Project Lifecycle

```
Idea → Business Case → Approval Gate → Project Charter → Execution → Monitoring → Closure → Post-Implementation Review
```

#### Stage Details

| Stage | Key Activities | Gate Criteria | Decision Maker |
|-------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| **1. Idea Submission** | Idea captured on Initiative Register; initial screening | Aligned with strategy? Resource feasible? | Department Head |
| **2. Business Case Development** | Full business case prepared (STR-TPL-005); financial analysis (NPV/IRR/payback) | Positive NPV? IRR > hurdle rate? Payback acceptable? | Finance validates |
| **3. Approval Gate** | Business case presented to approving authority; prioritization against other projects | Budget available? Resources available? Priority rank? | Per classification table |
| **4. Project Charter** | Scope, deliverables, timeline, budget, team, risks documented (STR-TPL-004) | Charter signed by Sponsor and Project Lead | Project Sponsor |
| **5. Execution** | Work performed per plan; milestones tracked; issues managed | Milestones on track? Budget on track? | Project Lead |
| **6. Monitoring** | Monthly status reports; variance analysis; risk updates; change requests managed | No Red flags unaddressed | Project Sponsor |
| **7. Closure** | Deliverables accepted; budget reconciled; lessons learned captured; team released | All deliverables accepted? Budget closed? | Project Sponsor |
| **8. Post-Implementation Review (PIR)** | Benefits realization assessed 3–6 months after closure | Were projected benefits achieved? | MD |

### 6.4 Project Governance

| Forum | Frequency | Attendees | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Project Status Meeting | Weekly/Bi-weekly | Project Lead + Team | Execution tracking |
| Project Sponsor Check-in | Monthly | Sponsor + Project Lead | Escalation, decisions |
| QBR Portfolio Review | Quarterly | MD + All Sponsors | Portfolio-level review, prioritization |
| Board Project Update | Quarterly | MD presents to Board | Strategic/Major project oversight |

### 6.5 Change Control

| Change Type | Approval Required |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Scope change (minor, no budget impact) | Project Sponsor |
| Budget increase ≤ 10% of approved budget | MD |
| Budget increase > 10% or timeline extension > 1 month | Original approval authority (re-approval) |
| Cancellation of project | Original approval authority |

## 7. Business Case & Investment Appraisal

### 7.1 When a Business Case Is Required

| Scenario | Required? |
|----------|-----------|
| Any capital expenditure > UGX 25M | Yes |
| New business line or market entry | Yes |
| New system or technology implementation | Yes |
| Significant process change affecting multiple departments | Yes |
| Hiring ≥ 3 positions for a new function | Yes |
| Routine operational spending within approved budget | No |
| Emergency repairs/replacements within emergency authority | No (but documented post-hoc) |

### 7.2 Investment Appraisal Methodology

All business cases must include quantitative financial analysis using the following methods:

#### 7.2.1 Net Present Value (NPV)

```
NPV = Σ [Cash Flow_t / (1 + r)^t] - Initial Investment

Where:
  r = Discount rate (Awdeer standard: 18% — reflecting Uganda risk-free rate ~12% + company risk premium ~6%)
  t = Time period (years)
```

**Decision Rule:** NPV > 0 = proceed (subject to other criteria)

#### 7.2.2 Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

The IRR is the discount rate at which NPV = 0.

**Decision Rule:** IRR > hurdle rate (18%) = proceed

#### 7.2.3 Payback Period

```
Payback Period = Time taken for cumulative net cash flows to equal the initial investment
```

**Decision Rule:**

| Project Category | Maximum Payback Period |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| IT / Technology | 2 years |
| Equipment / Vehicles | 3 years |
| Real Estate / Infrastructure | 5 years |
| New Business Line | 3 years |

#### 7.2.4 Sensitivity Analysis

All business cases for projects > UGX 100M must include sensitivity analysis showing NPV/IRR under:
- **Base case** — Management's best estimate
- **Optimistic case** — Revenue +15%, costs -10%
- **Pessimistic case** — Revenue -15%, costs +10%
- **Break-even case** — What revenue/cost level makes NPV = 0

### 7.3 Example: Investment Appraisal (Fuel Tanker Purchase)

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Investment | UGX 450,000,000 |
| Annual net cash inflow | UGX 180,000,000 |
| Project life | 5 years |
| Salvage value | UGX 50,000,000 |
| Discount rate | 18% |

| Year | Cash Flow (UGX) | PV Factor (18%) | Present Value (UGX) |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| 0 | (450,000,000) | 1.000 | (450,000,000) |
| 1 | 180,000,000 | 0.847 | 152,460,000 |
| 2 | 180,000,000 | 0.718 | 129,240,000 |
| 3 | 180,000,000 | 0.609 | 109,620,000 |
| 4 | 180,000,000 | 0.516 | 92,880,000 |
| 5 | 230,000,000 | 0.437 | 100,510,000 |
| **NPV** | | | **134,710,000** |

- **NPV:** UGX 134,710,000 (Positive — proceed)
- **IRR:** ~30% (Above 18% hurdle — proceed)
- **Payback:** 2.5 years (Within 3-year threshold — proceed)

### 7.4 Non-Financial Evaluation

In addition to financial metrics, all business cases are evaluated on:

| Criterion | Weight | Scale |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Strategic alignment | 25% | 1–5 (5 = direct strategic priority) |
| Financial return (NPV/IRR) | 30% | 1–5 (5 = exceeds hurdle significantly) |
| Risk level | 20% | 1–5 (5 = low risk) |
| Resource availability | 15% | 1–5 (5 = resources readily available) |
| Urgency / Time sensitivity | 10% | 1–5 (5 = must act now) |

**Minimum weighted score for approval:** 3.0 / 5.0

## 8. Lean vs Controlled Versions

### 8.1 Annual Planning Cycle

| Element | Lean (< 15 staff) | Controlled (15–100+ staff) |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Planning lead | MD (does everything) | MD leads; Dept Heads own their sections |
| Budget development | MD + Finance Manager build top-down | Bottom-up by department, consolidated by CFO |
| Challenge sessions | Informal review between MD and FM | Formal sessions: MD + CFO challenge each department |
| Board approval | MD/Owner approves (or informal board) | Full Board presentation and resolution |
| OKR cascading | Company → Individual (skip department) | Company → Department → Individual |
| Timeline | Compressed: 4 weeks (November) | Full: 10 weeks (October–December) |
| Documentation | Annual Plan on 3–5 pages | Full Annual Plan document (STR-TPL-001) |

### 8.2 OKR/KPI Management

| Element | Lean | Controlled |
|---------|------|-----------|
| KPI count | 3–5 per department, 15–20 company total | 5–10 per department, 40–60 company total |
| Tracking tool | Google Sheets / simple spreadsheet | ERP dashboard + dedicated KPI module |
| Review frequency | Monthly informal check-in with MD | Monthly department reviews + formal quarterly QBR |
| OKR setting | MD sets with input from staff | Collaborative: Dept Heads propose, MD approves |
| Individual KPIs | Verbal agreement | Written scorecard, signed by employee and manager |

### 8.3 QBR Process

| Element | Lean | Controlled |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Duration | 1–1.5 hours | 2.5–3 hours |
| Attendees | MD + all staff (small team meeting) | MD + Dept Heads + Finance (functional leads) |
| Prep required | Basic financial summary + KPI sheet | Full QBR deck (STR-TPL-003) per agenda |
| Formality | Discussion-based; MD takes notes | Structured presentation; formal minutes and action log |
| Frequency | Quarterly (can be shortened to monthly) | Quarterly (with monthly Dept Head check-ins) |

### 8.4 Project Portfolio Management

| Element | Lean | Controlled |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Project tracking | Simple task list (spreadsheet or Trello) | Project charter + monthly status reports |
| Business case | Required for > UGX 25M | Required for > UGX 25M; full template for > UGX 100M |
| Approval | MD approves all | Per classification table (Section 6.2) |
| Governance | MD reviews monthly | QBR portfolio review + monthly sponsor check-ins |
| Change control | MD decides informally | Formal change request per Section 6.5 |
| PIR | Verbal lessons learned | Written Post-Implementation Review |
