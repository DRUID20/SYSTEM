# Awdeer Investments Limited — Executive Summary

## Purpose

This document serves as the complete Business Operating System (BOS) playbook for Awdeer Investments Limited. It is designed to be implementation-ready and covers every aspect of running the business: governance, finance, operations, HR, compliance, IT, and internal audit.

## What This Playbook Delivers

1. **Governance Framework** — Board packs, resolutions, Delegation of Authority, risk register
2. **Strategy & Planning** — Annual planning, OKRs/KPIs, QBRs, project portfolio management
3. **Finance Engine** — Chart of accounts, month-end close, AR/AP, tax compliance (URA-ready), management reporting, asset management
4. **Procurement System** — Requisition-to-payment, supplier management, fraud controls
5. **Inventory & Logistics** — Stock management, cycle counts, fleet controls
6. **Sales & Marketing** — CRM pipeline, quotation-to-contract, customer management
7. **HR System** — Recruitment to exit, payroll, performance, disciplinary
8. **Legal & Compliance** — Contract management, compliance register, AML/KYC, data protection
9. **IT Operations** — Tools stack, access control, backup, cyber hygiene
10. **Internal Audit** — Audit charter, programs for all areas, CAPA tracker
11. **ERP Blueprint** — Module mapping, master data, user roles, approval flows, dashboards
12. **Implementation Roadmap** — Phased 30/60/90-day and 6/12-month plan

## Design Principles

| Principle | What It Means |
|-----------|---------------|
| Dual-version | Every process has a Lean (small team) and Controlled (mid-size) version |
| Audit-ready | Every transaction has a trail: who, what, when, why, approved by whom |
| Segregation of Duties | No single person controls end-to-end (request ≠ approve ≠ pay ≠ reconcile) |
| Uganda-Ready | Tax workflows for VAT, WHT, PAYE, NSSF; URA filing calendar built in |
| ERP-Mapped | Every process maps to an ERP module (Odoo/SAP/Zoho) |
| Scale-Ready | Modular design: add business lines, branches, subsidiaries without redesign |
