## 1. Purpose

To establish the standard operating procedure for customer and supplier due diligence (Know Your Customer / Know Your Supplier) to prevent money laundering and terrorist financing in compliance with the Anti-Money Laundering Act 2013, the Anti-Terrorism Act 2002, and the Financial Intelligence Authority (FIA) guidelines of Uganda.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- All new and existing customers of Awdeer Investments Limited
- All new and existing suppliers and service providers
- Agents, intermediaries, and brokers acting for or on behalf of the company
- Joint venture partners and consortium members
- All business lines: Energy/Fuel, Real Estate, Logistics, Retail, Professional Services

**Particularly high-risk sectors for AML purposes:**
- Real estate transactions (property purchases and sales)
- High-value fuel/energy transactions
- Cash-intensive retail operations

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Customer/Supplier Onboarding — Collect KYC Documents

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Collect required identification and verification documents |
| **Who** | Sales staff (customers) / Procurement staff (suppliers) |
| **Timeline** | Before first transaction or within 5 business days for existing relationships |

#### Required Documents — Individuals

| Document | Required | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| National Identity Card (NIN) | Yes | Uganda National ID or foreign national equivalent |
| Passport (for non-Ugandans) | Yes | Valid, not expired |
| Proof of address | Yes | Utility bill, bank statement, LC letter (within 3 months) |
| TIN certificate | Yes (for businesses) | URA Tax Identification Number |
| Photograph | Yes | Passport-size or digital |

#### Required Documents — Companies/Entities

| Document | Required | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Certificate of incorporation (URSB) | Yes | Certified copy |
| Memorandum and Articles of Association | Yes | Current version |
| TIN certificate (URA) | Yes | Valid |
| Tax clearance certificate | Recommended | For suppliers above UGX 20M |
| Trading licence | Yes | Current year |
| Board resolution authorising the relationship | For high-value | Above UGX 100M |
| Director/shareholder identification (NIN/passport) | Yes | For all directors and shareholders holding 25%+ |
| Beneficial ownership declaration | Yes | Identify ultimate beneficial owners (25%+ ownership) |
| Bank account details | Yes | Must match registered entity name |
| Certificate of compliance (URSB) | Recommended | Confirms company in good standing |
| Reference letters | For new suppliers | Above UGX 20M |

### Step 2: Verify Documents

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Verify the authenticity and validity of submitted documents |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer (or designated staff) |
| **Verification Methods** | Cross-check NIN with National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) data, verify URSB registration on URSB online portal, verify TIN on URA e-portal, check trading licence with issuing local authority, confirm bank details with issuing bank (for high-value relationships) |
| **Timeline** | Within 3 business days of receipt |

### Step 3: Risk Assessment and Rating

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Assign a risk rating to the customer or supplier |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |

#### Risk Rating Criteria

| Factor | Low Risk | Medium Risk | High Risk |
|--------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Entity Type** | Regulated entity (bank, government) | Established Ugandan company | Newly registered, complex ownership, foreign entity |
| **Geography** | Uganda only | Regional (EAC) | High-risk jurisdictions (FATF grey/black list) |
| **Transaction Size** | Below UGX 20M per transaction | UGX 20M – 100M | Above UGX 100M |
| **Transaction Type** | Standard goods/services | Recurring large transactions | Cash-intensive, property, cross-border |
| **Industry** | Low-risk sectors | Moderate-risk | Gambling, mining, weapons, crypto |
| **PEP Status** | No PEP connection | Associate of PEP | PEP or close family member |
| **Adverse Media** | None | Minor concerns | Significant negative coverage |

#### Risk Rating Output

| Rating | Due Diligence Level | Approval Required | Monitoring |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|------------|
| **Low** | Standard Due Diligence (SDD) | Department Head | Annual review |
| **Medium** | Standard Due Diligence + additional checks | Legal/Compliance Officer + Department Head | Semi-annual review |
| **High** | Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) | MD approval required | Quarterly review |

### Step 4: Standard Due Diligence (SDD)

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Complete standard verification for low and medium-risk relationships |
| **Who** | Sales/Procurement staff with Legal/Compliance Officer oversight |
| **Checks** | Verify identity documents, confirm business registration, verify TIN, check against basic sanctions lists, confirm nature and purpose of business relationship, record source of funds (for transactions above UGX 50M) |
| **Documentation** | Complete KYC form and file all verified documents |
| **Approval** | Department Head approves relationship |

### Step 5: Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Conduct enhanced verification for high-risk relationships |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Additional Checks** | Detailed source of funds / source of wealth verification, beneficial ownership investigation (trace to natural persons), PEP screening (Politically Exposed Persons), sanctions screening (UN, OFAC, EU, local lists), adverse media search, site visit to business premises, references from banking institutions, senior management approval (MD) |
| **Documentation** | Detailed EDD report with findings and recommendation |
| **Approval** | MD approval required before relationship is established |
| **Timeline** | Within 10 business days |

### Step 6: Approve or Decline

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Make decision on whether to establish or continue the relationship |
| **Who** | Per risk rating approval level |
| **Approve** | Customer/supplier activated in ERP. KYC file stored securely |
| **Decline** | Relationship not established. Record reasons. If suspicious, consider STR |
| **Conditions** | May approve with conditions (transaction limits, enhanced monitoring, restricted payment methods) |

### Step 7: Ongoing Monitoring

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Continuously monitor relationships for suspicious activity or changes |
| **Who** | All staff (front-line awareness); Legal/Compliance Officer (systematic review) |
| **Transaction Monitoring** | Review transactions against expected patterns. Flag unusual size, frequency, or method. Cash transactions above UGX 20M automatically flagged |
| **Periodic Review** | Low risk: annually. Medium risk: semi-annually. High risk: quarterly |
| **Trigger Events** | Significant change in transaction pattern, change in ownership or directors, adverse media, regulatory inquiry, request for unusual payment methods |
| **KYC Refresh** | Update KYC documents at periodic review or upon trigger event |

### Step 8: Suspicious Transaction Reporting

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Report suspicious transactions to the Financial Intelligence Authority (FIA) |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Triggers** | See Red Flags list (main document Section 7.4) |
| **Internal Reporting** | Staff reports concern to Legal/Compliance Officer immediately |
| **STR Filing** | Legal/Compliance Officer files STR with FIA within 24 hours of determination that transaction is suspicious |
| **Tipping Off** | Strictly prohibited. Do not inform the customer/supplier that an STR has been filed. Offence under Section 17 of the AML Act 2013 |
| **Record Keeping** | Maintain copy of STR and all supporting documentation for minimum 10 years |
| **Proceed or Freeze** | Legal/Compliance Officer determines whether to proceed with, delay, or refuse the transaction (in consultation with FIA if needed) |

## 6. Controls and Approvals

| Control | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **No Transaction Without KYC** | No customer or supplier transacts without minimum KYC completion |
| **Risk-Based Approach** | Due diligence level proportionate to assessed risk |
| **Segregation** | Person conducting KYC cannot be sole approver of the relationship |
| **Cash Transaction Reporting** | All cash transactions above UGX 20,000,000 reported internally |
| **STR Independence** | Legal/Compliance Officer files STRs independently; no management override |
| **Record Retention** | KYC records retained for minimum 10 years after end of relationship (AML Act requirement) |
| **Training** | All front-line staff trained on AML awareness within 30 days of joining |
| **Sanctions Screening** | All new relationships screened against sanctions lists |
| **Periodic Re-screening** | All existing relationships re-screened at periodic review |
| **Audit Trail** | All KYC decisions documented with rationale |

## 9. Lean vs Controlled Version

### Lean Version (Small Team < 15 people)

| Aspect | Lean Approach |
|--------|---------------|
| **KYC Collection** | Sales/procurement staff collects documents using a simple checklist |
| **Verification** | Finance Manager verifies TIN on URA portal. MD reviews ID documents |
| **Risk Assessment** | Simple low/medium/high rating by MD |
| **SDD** | Checklist-based: ID verified, TIN verified, business registration checked |
| **EDD** | External lawyer engaged for high-risk relationships |
| **Monitoring** | Finance Manager flags unusual transactions during payment processing |
| **STR** | MD consults external lawyer and files with FIA if warranted |
| **Records** | Physical KYC file per customer/supplier + scanned copies |
| **Training** | MD provides basic AML awareness at annual staff meeting |
| **Tools** | Paper checklist, spreadsheet register, physical filing |

### Controlled Version (Mid-size Team 15-100+ people)

| Aspect | Controlled Approach |
|--------|---------------------|
| **KYC Collection** | Structured onboarding forms in ERP. Document upload portal |
| **Verification** | Legal/Compliance Officer verifies all documents. Online registry checks. Third-party verification services for complex cases |
| **Risk Assessment** | Formal risk scoring matrix. Legal/Compliance Officer assigns rating |
| **SDD** | Full checklist with documented verification steps |
| **EDD** | Legal/Compliance Officer conducts. External due diligence providers for complex cases. PEP/sanctions screening via commercial database |
| **Monitoring** | ERP transaction monitoring with automated alerts. Quarterly relationship reviews. Annual KYC refresh programme |
| **STR** | Legal/Compliance Officer files independently. External counsel consulted. FIA liaison established |
| **Records** | ERP-based KYC module with document management, version control, and audit trail |
| **Training** | Formal AML training programme. Online modules for front-line staff. Specialist training for compliance team |
| **Tools** | ERP compliance module, sanctions screening tool, PEP database, case management system |

## 10. Red Flags Reference Guide

### Customer Red Flags

| Red Flag | Risk Indicator |
|----------|---------------|
| Customer reluctant to provide identification | Identity concealment |
| Transactions inconsistent with known business profile | Potential laundering |
| Unusually large cash payments (above UGX 20M) | Cash laundering |
| Requests to structure transactions below reporting thresholds | Structuring / smurfing |
| Complex ownership structures with no clear commercial rationale | Shell company risk |
| Customer is a PEP with no declaration | Corruption risk |
| Transactions involving sanctioned countries | Sanctions evasion |
| Customer rushes transaction and refuses due diligence | Avoiding scrutiny |
| Payment from unrelated third parties | Layering |
| Property transactions at significantly above/below market value | Value manipulation |

### Supplier Red Flags

| Red Flag | Risk Indicator |
|----------|---------------|
| Supplier cannot provide valid business registration | Unregistered entity |
| Invoices from entity different from contracted supplier | Invoice fraud / laundering |
| Requests for payment to bank accounts in different names | Diversion of funds |
| Supplier pricing significantly different from market | Kickback / overbilling |
| Supplier newly registered with no track record but awarded large contracts | Front company risk |
| Beneficial owners are PEPs or related to company staff | Conflict / corruption |
| Supplier shares address with other suspicious entities | Shell company cluster |
