## 1. PURPOSE

To establish standardized procedures for conducting unannounced spot checks and surprise audits across Awdeer Investments Limited. Spot checks serve as a deterrent to fraud and non-compliance by maintaining an unpredictable audit presence, and provide real-time assurance on the effectiveness of internal controls.

---

## 2. SCOPE

This SOP applies to:
- All scheduled spot checks under the annual spot-check plan
- All surprise audits triggered by specific events or suspicions
- All business locations, departments, and operational areas
- All Internal Audit staff authorized to conduct spot checks
- All employees and processes subject to spot checking

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

### CONTROLLED VERSION

#### Phase 1: Spot-Check Planning

**Step 1.1: Establish Annual Spot-Check Schedule**

At the beginning of each year, the Head of IA develops the spot-check schedule:

| Area | Frequency | Duration | Auditor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petty cash count | Monthly (random day) | 1-2 hours | Audit Assistant |
| Cash register/till verification | Bi-weekly | 1 hour | Audit Assistant |
| Stores physical count (sample) | Monthly | 2-4 hours | Senior Auditor - Operations |
| Procurement files (sample of 5 POs) | Monthly | 2 hours | Senior Auditor - Operations |
| Attendance/time records | Monthly (random day) | 1-2 hours | Audit Assistant |
| Fixed asset spot verification | Quarterly (sample 10 assets) | 3 hours | Audit Assistant |
| Bank reconciliation review | Monthly | 1-2 hours | Senior Auditor - Finance |
| Revenue collection verification | Bi-weekly | 1-2 hours | Senior Auditor - Finance |

**Step 1.2: Randomize Timing**

- Head of IA randomly selects specific dates within each month
- Dates are kept confidential and known only to the Head of IA and the assigned auditor
- Dates are never communicated to auditees in advance
- Head of IA uses a random number method to select dates (e.g., random day between business day 1 and 20 of each month)

**Step 1.3: Prepare Spot-Check Kit**

Each auditor maintains a ready-to-go spot-check kit containing:
- Blank spot-check forms for each area
- Calculator
- Camera (for photographic evidence if needed)
- Clipboard and pens
- Copy of relevant policies and thresholds
- Prior spot-check results for reference

#### Phase 2: Conducting the Spot Check

**Step 2.1: Arrive Unannounced**

- Auditor proceeds to the audit area without prior notice
- Present audit credentials/authority if challenged
- Explain the purpose briefly: "This is a routine internal audit spot check"
- Request immediate access to relevant records, cash, assets, or documents
- If access is denied, document the refusal and escalate to Head of IA immediately

**Step 2.2: Perform the Spot Check**

Follow the specific checklist for each area. General steps:

**Cash and Petty Cash:**
1. Request the custodian to present all cash on hand
2. Count all cash in the presence of the custodian
3. Record denominations and total
4. Compare physical count to the cash book/register balance
5. Investigate any shortage or overage
6. Review recent transactions for proper authorization
7. Check physical security of the cash storage
8. Both auditor and custodian sign the count sheet

**Stores/Inventory:**
1. Select 10-20 items from the stores records
2. Physically locate and count each selected item
3. Compare physical count to the records balance
4. Select 5-10 items from the physical shelves
5. Trace shelf items back to the stores records
6. Note any discrepancies, damaged goods, or expired items
7. Check storage conditions and security
8. Check that recent issues have proper requisitions

**Attendance/Time Records:**
1. Obtain the current day's attendance record
2. Physically verify the presence of a sample of recorded employees
3. Check for late arrivals and unauthorized absences
4. Compare attendance records to leave approvals
5. Check biometric system accuracy (if applicable)

**Procurement Files:**
1. Select 5 recent purchase orders at random
2. Verify each PO has proper authorization
3. Check for competitive quotations per policy
4. Verify goods received notes match PO quantities
5. Check invoice amounts match PO and GRN
6. Verify proper segregation of duties (requester != approver)

**Bank Reconciliation:**
1. Obtain the latest bank reconciliation
2. Verify it was prepared within the required timeframe
3. Check for stale reconciling items (>30 days)
4. Trace a sample of reconciling items to subsequent clearance
5. Verify the reconciliation was reviewed and approved

**Fixed Assets:**
1. Select 10 assets from the asset register at random
2. Physically verify the existence and location of each asset
3. Check asset tags/labels are present and readable
4. Note the condition of each asset
5. Select 5 physical assets and trace to the register
6. Check for assets that appear to be unrecorded

**Revenue Collection:**
1. Select a sample of recent sales transactions
2. Verify receipts were issued for all collections
3. Verify collections were banked intact and timely
4. Compare daily collection records to bank deposits
5. Check for any unauthorized discounts or write-offs

**Step 2.3: Handle Exceptions**

If an exception is found during the spot check:

| Severity | Action |
|---|---|
| Minor (e.g., missing signature, minor filing error) | Document on spot-check form; discuss with staff |
| Moderate (e.g., cash shortage <UGX 500,000; control bypass) | Document on form; notify department head same day |
| Serious (e.g., significant cash shortage; suspected fraud; missing assets) | Stop the spot check; secure evidence; notify Head of IA immediately; Head of IA notifies MD |
| Critical (e.g., confirmed theft; major irregularity) | Secure all evidence; Head of IA notifies MD and ARC Chairman; initiate formal investigation |

#### Phase 3: Documentation

**Step 3.1: Complete the Spot-Check Form**

Immediately after the spot check, the auditor completes:
- Date, time, and location of spot check
- Area checked and specific items examined
- Results (satisfactory / exceptions noted)
- Description of any exceptions found
- Auditee staff present during the check
- Evidence collected (photos, copies, count sheets)
- Auditee acknowledgment signature
- Auditor signature

**Step 3.2: Update the Spot-Check Register**

Enter the results in the cumulative spot-check register:

| Date | Area | Location | Auditor | Result | Exceptions | Severity | Action Taken | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Date] | [Area] | [Location] | [Name] | Pass/Fail | [Description] | Minor/Mod/Serious | [Action] | Open/Closed |

**Step 3.3: File Documentation**

- Completed spot-check forms filed in Internal Audit spot-check folder
- Evidence attached (photos, count sheets, copies)
- Filed chronologically by date

#### Phase 4: Follow-Up and Reporting

**Step 4.1: Exception Follow-Up**

For moderate and serious exceptions:
1. Issue a spot-check exception memo to the department head within 2 business days
2. Require a written response and corrective action within 5 business days
3. Log the exception in the CAPA tracker if it represents a control failure
4. Follow up on corrective action per the CAPA schedule

**Step 4.2: Trend Analysis**

Monthly, the Head of IA reviews the spot-check register for:
- Areas with recurring exceptions
- Patterns suggesting systemic issues
- Improvement or deterioration trends
- Areas requiring increased audit focus

**Step 4.3: Quarterly Reporting**

Include in the quarterly ARC report:
- Number of spot checks conducted vs. planned
- Summary of results (pass/fail rates by area)
- Significant exceptions and actions taken
- Trend analysis and recommendations

#### Phase 5: Surprise Audit (Ad-Hoc)

**Step 5.1: Trigger Events for Surprise Audit**

A surprise audit is a more comprehensive, unannounced engagement triggered by:
- Whistleblower report or anonymous tip
- Pattern of spot-check failures (3+ consecutive)
- Management suspicion of fraud or irregularity
- Unusual financial patterns detected
- Significant policy violation reported

**Step 5.2: Surprise Audit Authorization**

- Head of IA assesses the trigger and determines scope
- MD authorization required for comprehensive surprise audits
- ARC Chairman informed for potential fraud investigations

**Step 5.3: Surprise Audit Execution**

1. Arrive unannounced with a team (minimum 2 auditors for witness purposes)
2. Secure access to all relevant records and assets immediately
3. If fraud is suspected:
   - Preserve all evidence (do not alter original documents)
   - Take photographs of physical evidence
   - Document chain of custody for all evidence
   - Restrict access to the area being investigated
   - Interview relevant staff separately
4. Complete a comprehensive review of the area using the full audit program
5. Report findings to Head of IA and MD within 24 hours of fieldwork completion

**Step 5.4: Evidence Preservation for Investigations**

If the surprise audit reveals potential fraud:
- All original documents secured in a locked cabinet under IA control
- Electronic evidence copied with IT assistance (maintain chain of custody)
- Photographs dated and annotated
- Witness statements obtained and signed
- All evidence handling documented in an evidence log
- External authorities (police, if required) notified by MD
