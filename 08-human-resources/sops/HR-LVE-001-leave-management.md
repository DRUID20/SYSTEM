## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the procedures for requesting, approving, tracking, and reporting on employee leave and managing attendance across Awdeer Investments Limited. It ensures compliance with the Employment Act, 2006 and internal leave policy provisions.

## 2. Scope

This SOP covers all leave types (annual, sick, maternity, paternity, compassionate, study, unpaid) and attendance management for all employees across all locations.

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Process A: Annual Leave Request

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| A.1 | Employee checks leave balance (system or with HR) | Employee | Before request | Ensure sufficient balance |
| A.2 | Employee completes Leave Request Form (HR-TPL-005) | Employee | Minimum 14 days before leave | 30 days notice for 5+ consecutive days |
| A.3 | Submit form to line manager for approval | Employee | Same day | Physical form or system submission |
| A.4 | Line manager reviews: check team coverage, work commitments, concurrent leave (max 30% of team) | Line Manager | Within 2 working days | May discuss alternative dates if conflict |
| A.5 | Line manager approves or rejects with reason | Line Manager | Within 2 working days | Signature on form / system approval |
| A.6 | Controlled: Leave of 6+ days forwarded to HR Manager / Department Head for second approval | Line Manager → HR Manager | Within 1 additional day | — |
| A.7 | HR Officer records leave in tracking system | HR Officer | Within 1 working day of approval | Update leave balance |
| A.8 | HR Officer confirms approval to employee | HR Officer | Same day | Email / system notification |
| A.9 | Employee arranges handover before leave | Employee | Before leave starts | Written handover if 5+ days |
| A.10 | On return: employee marks return in system / with HR | Employee | First day back | — |
| A.11 | HR Officer updates actual leave taken | HR Officer | Day of return | Reconcile with approved dates |

### Process B: Sick Leave

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| B.1 | Employee notifies line manager of illness (call/SMS/email) | Employee | Before start of work day (or ASAP) | Must notify, not just text |
| B.2 | Line manager acknowledges and notifies HR | Line Manager | Same day | Email to HR Officer |
| B.3 | For 1–2 days: employee returns and submits leave form retrospectively | Employee | First day back | Line manager approves |
| B.4 | For 3+ consecutive days: employee submits medical certificate | Employee | Within 2 days of return | From registered medical practitioner |
| B.5 | HR Officer verifies medical certificate | HR Officer | Upon receipt | May verify with medical facility if concerns |
| B.6 | HR Officer records sick leave and updates balance | HR Officer | Within 1 working day | First 14 days: full pay; next 14 days: half pay (per policy) |
| B.7 | If sick leave exceeds 28 days: HR Manager reviews medical situation | HR Manager | Day 28 | Consider medical board, extended leave options |
| B.8 | Repeated/patterned sick leave: HR Manager investigates | HR Manager | As identified | Monday/Friday patterns; frequent short absences |

### Process C: Maternity Leave

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| C.1 | Employee notifies HR of pregnancy | Employee | As soon as practicable | Confidential |
| C.2 | Employee submits medical certificate confirming pregnancy and expected date | Employee | At least 4 weeks before leave | Employment Act s.56 |
| C.3 | Employee and line manager agree leave start date (4 weeks before expected delivery) | Employee + Line Manager | At least 4 weeks before | Plan handover |
| C.4 | HR Officer records maternity leave (60 working days at full pay) | HR Officer | Upon approval | Employment Act s.56 |
| C.5 | Line manager arranges temporary cover | Line Manager | Before leave starts | Internal or temporary hire |
| C.6 | Employee commences maternity leave | Employee | Agreed date | — |
| C.7 | HR maintains contact (with employee's consent) for updates | HR Officer | During leave | Respectful, minimal contact |
| C.8 | Employee confirms return date (at least 2 weeks before return) | Employee | 2 weeks before | May request extension (unpaid) |
| C.9 | Employee returns to same or equivalent position | Line Manager + HR | On return date | Employment Act s.56(5) — no disadvantage |
| C.10 | Lean: Nursing breaks provided (1 hour per day for 6 months post-return) | Line Manager | Post-return | Employment Act s.56A |

### Process D: Paternity Leave

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| D.1 | Employee notifies HR and line manager of expected birth | Employee | In advance | — |
| D.2 | Employee submits leave request with birth notification/certificate | Employee | Within 7 days of birth | Employment Act s.57 |
| D.3 | Line manager approves (4 working days) | Line Manager | Same day | Cannot be unreasonably refused |
| D.4 | HR Officer records paternity leave | HR Officer | Same day | Full pay |

### Process E: Compassionate Leave

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| E.1 | Employee notifies line manager and HR of bereavement/emergency | Employee | As soon as possible | May be verbal initially |
| E.2 | Line manager grants immediate leave (up to 5 days per year) | Line Manager | Immediately | Formal form completed on return |
| E.3 | Employee submits supporting documentation on return | Employee | First day back | Death certificate, hospital letter, LC letter |
| E.4 | HR Officer records compassionate leave | HR Officer | Upon return | — |

### Process F: Study Leave

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| F.1 | Employee submits study leave request with supporting evidence (exam schedule, course details) | Employee | At least 30 days in advance | Maximum 10 days per year |
| F.2 | Line manager reviews and recommends | Line Manager | Within 3 working days | Consider work impact |
| F.3 | HR Manager reviews and approves/rejects | HR Manager | Within 3 working days | Must be relevant to role or approved development plan |
| F.4 | Controlled: MD approval required | MD | Within 3 working days | — |
| F.5 | HR Officer records study leave | HR Officer | Upon approval | — |
| F.6 | Employee submits proof of attendance/exam after leave | Employee | Within 5 working days of return | Attendance certificate or exam confirmation |

### Process G: Attendance Management

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| G.1 | Employees sign in (register / biometric / system) daily | Employee | On arrival | — |
| G.2 | HR Officer reviews attendance records weekly | HR Officer | Weekly (Monday) | Identify late arrivals, early departures, absences |
| G.3 | Generate weekly attendance exception report | HR Officer | Weekly | Share with line managers |
| G.4 | Line manager addresses minor attendance issues informally | Line Manager | As they occur | Verbal reminder |
| G.5 | If pattern continues: formal verbal warning | Line Manager | After 3 occurrences | Document in file |
| G.6 | Continued issues: escalate per disciplinary SOP (HR-DIS-001) | Line Manager + HR | As needed | Written warning → final warning |
| G.7 | AWOL (absent 3+ days without contact): HR Manager initiates contact | HR Manager | Day 3 of no-contact | Phone, emergency contact, letter to address |
| G.8 | AWOL 5+ days with no contact: issue show-cause letter | HR Manager | Day 5 | 48-hour response window |
| G.9 | If no response to show-cause: initiate disciplinary hearing | HR Manager | Day 8 | Per HR-DIS-001 |

## 6. Controls and Approvals

| Control | Description | Lean | Controlled |
|---------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Leave balance check | Balance verified before approval | Manual check | System auto-check |
| Concurrent leave limit | Max 30% of department on leave simultaneously | Manager checks | System enforces |
| Advance notice | 14 days for annual leave; 30 days for 5+ days | Manager enforces | System enforces |
| Medical certificate | Required for sick leave 3+ days | HR checks | HR verifies + may contact provider |
| Maternity documentation | Medical certificate and notification timeline | HR checks | HR + Legal check |
| Carry-over limit | Maximum 5 days carried to next year; used by March 31 | HR tracks | System auto-forfeit |
| Leave accrual for payroll | Monthly leave data reconciled with payroll | Spreadsheet | System integration |
| Attendance monitoring | Regular review of attendance records | Weekly manual review | Daily automated report |
| AWOL procedure | Structured escalation process | HR Manager manages | HR Manager + Legal |
| Approval authority | Leave approved per authority matrix | Manager signature | System workflow with audit trail |

## 9. Lean vs Controlled Version Summary

| Aspect | Lean | Controlled |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Leave request | Paper form (HR-TPL-005) | System-based (HRIS / ERP) |
| Balance tracking | Spreadsheet updated by HR Officer | Real-time system balance |
| Approval workflow | Manager signs form; HR records | System workflow with notifications |
| Concurrent leave check | Manager judgment | System enforcement |
| Attendance tracking | Manual sign-in register | Biometric / system clock-in |
| Reporting | Monthly manual report | Automated weekly/monthly reports |
| Carry-over management | HR Officer tracks manually | System auto-calculates and notifies |
| AWOL management | HR Manager handles | HR Manager + Legal counsel |
| Leave accrual for payroll | Manual reconciliation | System auto-feeds payroll |
| Audit trail | Physical forms filed | Digital workflow with timestamps |
