# Awdeer Investments Limited — Templates Pack Index

## Overview

This index catalogs every template in the Business Operating System. Templates are stored in their respective department folders and follow the naming convention: `[DEPT]-TPL-[SEQ]-[description].md`

All templates are designed to be copy-paste ready and can be converted to Google Docs, Sheets, or ERP forms.

## How to Use

1. Navigate to the relevant department folder
2. Open the template file
3. Copy content into Google Docs/Sheets or import into ERP
4. Customize company-specific fields (marked with `[PLACEHOLDER]`)
5. Get approval via the policy approval process (GOV-TPL-005)
6. Store approved version in the controlled document folder
