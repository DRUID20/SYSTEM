## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the Legal, Compliance & Risk management framework for Awdeer Investments Limited. It provides the policies, processes, and controls required to:

- Manage contracts throughout their lifecycle
- Maintain compliance with all applicable Ugandan legislation and regulatory requirements
- Prevent bribery, corruption, and money laundering
- Protect personal data in accordance with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019
- Manage incidents, whistleblowing reports, and litigation
- Embed a culture of ethical conduct and legal awareness across the organisation

---

## 2. Scope

This framework applies to all directors, employees, contractors, agents, and third parties acting on behalf of Awdeer Investments Limited across all business lines (Energy/Fuel, Real Estate, Logistics, Retail, Professional Services).

### 4.3 Contract Review Checklist (Summary)

Before any contract is signed, the following must be verified:

1. **Parties** — correct legal names, registration numbers, authority to contract
2. **Scope** — clear deliverables, specifications, service levels
3. **Commercial Terms** — pricing, payment terms, penalties, currency
4. **Duration** — start date, end date, renewal mechanism, termination clauses
5. **Liability** — indemnities, limitation of liability, insurance requirements
6. **Compliance** — anti-bribery clauses, data protection, regulatory compliance
7. **Dispute Resolution** — governing law (Laws of Uganda), arbitration/mediation clauses
8. **Tax Implications** — WHT, VAT, stamp duty obligations
9. **Confidentiality** — NDA provisions, information handling
10. **Force Majeure** — provisions for unforeseeable circumstances

> Full checklist: see template `LEG-TPL-001-contract-review-checklist.md`

## 6. Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy

### 6.1 Policy Statement

Awdeer Investments Limited has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption in any form. All directors, employees, contractors, and agents are prohibited from offering, giving, soliciting, or accepting any bribe, whether directly or through intermediaries, in accordance with the Anti-Corruption Act 2009 of Uganda.

### 6.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is strictly prohibited:

1. **Bribery** — Offering, promising, giving, or accepting any financial or other advantage to induce or reward improper performance of a function
2. **Facilitation Payments** — Payments to government officials to expedite routine actions
3. **Kickbacks** — Receiving or paying commissions or rebates in exchange for contract awards or favourable treatment
4. **Gifts & Hospitality** — Offering or accepting gifts or hospitality that could reasonably be perceived as intended to influence business decisions (gifts exceeding UGX 200,000 in value must be declared and approved)
5. **Political Donations** — No company funds shall be used for political contributions without Board approval
6. **Conflicts of Interest** — Failing to disclose personal or financial interests that may conflict with company duties

### 6.3 Gifts and Hospitality Thresholds

| Type | Threshold (UGX) | Action Required |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Token / branded items | Up to 100,000 | No approval needed; record if frequent |
| Modest gifts / meals | 100,001 – 200,000 | Notify line manager; record in gift register |
| Significant gifts / hospitality | 200,001 – 500,000 | Pre-approval by MD; record in gift register |
| Above threshold | Above 500,000 | Decline and report to Legal/Compliance Officer |
| Government officials | Any value | Pre-approval by MD in all cases |

### 6.4 Conflict of Interest

All employees and directors must complete an annual Conflict of Interest Declaration (see `LEG-TPL-003-conflict-of-interest-declaration.md`). Any new conflict arising during the year must be disclosed immediately to the Legal/Compliance Officer.

## 7. AML/KYC Principles

### 7.1 Policy Statement

Awdeer Investments Limited is committed to preventing money laundering and terrorist financing in compliance with the Anti-Money Laundering Act 2013. All business relationships and transactions will be subject to appropriate due diligence.

### 7.2 Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

| Risk Level | Due Diligence Required |
|------------|----------------------|
| **Standard** | Verify identity (National ID, passport, or driving permit), verify business registration (URSB certificate, TIN), confirm nature of business |
| **Enhanced** | All standard measures plus: source of funds verification, senior management approval, beneficial ownership identification, ongoing monitoring |
| **Simplified** | Government entities, regulated financial institutions — reduced documentation but identity still verified |

### 7.3 Supplier Due Diligence

All new suppliers must provide:

1. Certificate of incorporation / business registration (URSB)
2. Tax identification number (TIN) and tax clearance certificate (URA)
3. Trading licence
4. Director/owner identification
5. Bank account details (matching registered entity name)
6. References (for contracts above UGX 20,000,000)

### 7.4 Red Flags

The following situations require escalation to the Legal/Compliance Officer:

- Unusually large cash transactions (above UGX 20,000,000)
- Reluctance to provide identification or business documentation
- Transactions with no apparent business rationale
- Requests to structure transactions to avoid reporting thresholds
- Customers or suppliers in sanctioned jurisdictions
- Frequent changes in ownership or company structure
- Payments from or to unrelated third parties

### 7.5 Suspicious Transaction Reporting

Where a suspicious transaction is identified, it must be reported to the Legal/Compliance Officer, who will file a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with the Financial Intelligence Authority (FIA) within 24 hours of identification.

> **Warning:** Tipping off the subject of an STR is a criminal offence under the Anti-Money Laundering Act 2013.

## 8. Data Protection and Privacy Policy

### 8.1 Policy Statement

Awdeer Investments Limited is committed to protecting the personal data of employees, customers, suppliers, and other stakeholders in accordance with the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (DPPA) of Uganda. Personal data will be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

### 8.2 Data Protection Principles

All processing of personal data must adhere to the following principles (Section 3, DPPA 2019):

1. **Accountability** — The company is responsible for and must demonstrate compliance with data protection principles
2. **Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency** — Data must be processed lawfully with a valid legal basis and the data subject must be informed
3. **Purpose Limitation** — Data must be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
4. **Data Minimisation** — Only data that is necessary for the stated purpose shall be collected
5. **Accuracy** — Data must be accurate and kept up to date
6. **Storage Limitation** — Data must not be kept longer than necessary for its purpose
7. **Integrity and Confidentiality** — Appropriate security measures must protect data from unauthorised access, loss, or destruction

### 8.3 Legal Bases for Processing

| Legal Basis | When Applicable |
|-------------|----------------|
| Consent | Marketing communications, non-essential cookies, data sharing with third parties |
| Contract | Processing necessary to perform an employment contract or service agreement |
| Legal Obligation | Tax reporting (URA), NSSF contributions, regulatory filings |
| Legitimate Interest | Fraud prevention, internal audit, business analytics (with balancing test) |
| Vital Interest | Emergency medical situations for employees |
| Public Interest | Where required by law for public functions |

### 8.4 Data Subject Rights

Under the DPPA 2019, data subjects have the following rights:

1. Right to be informed about data collection and processing
2. Right of access to their personal data
3. Right to correction of inaccurate data
4. Right to deletion of data (where no legal basis for retention)
5. Right to object to processing
6. Right not to be subject to automated decision-making

Data subject requests must be acknowledged within 3 business days and fulfilled within 30 days.

### 8.5 Data Breach Notification

| Action | Timeline | Responsible |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Identify and contain breach | Immediately upon discovery | Person discovering the breach |
| Report internally | Within 24 hours | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| Assess severity and scope | Within 48 hours | Legal/Compliance Officer + IT |
| Notify PDPO (if required) | Within 72 hours of becoming aware | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| Notify affected data subjects | Without undue delay (if high risk) | Legal/Compliance Officer + MD |
| Complete investigation and report | Within 30 days | Legal/Compliance Officer |

### 8.6 Data Retention Schedule

| Data Category | Retention Period | Legal Basis |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Employment records | 10 years after termination | Employment Act 2006, Income Tax Act |
| Financial records & tax | 10 years | Income Tax Act (Cap 340) |
| Customer contracts | 6 years after expiry | Limitation Act |
| Supplier records | 6 years after last transaction | Limitation Act, AML Act |
| CCTV footage | 90 days (unless incident) | Legitimate interest |
| Recruitment records (unsuccessful) | 1 year after decision | DPPA 2019 |
| Board minutes | Permanently | Companies Act 2012 |
| Health & safety records | 10 years | Occupational Safety and Health Act |

## 9. Incident Management and Whistleblowing Procedure

### 9.1 Purpose

This procedure establishes the process for reporting, investigating, and resolving incidents including fraud, misconduct, policy violations, health and safety events, and other irregularities. It also provides a safe channel for whistleblowing.

### 9.2 Types of Reportable Incidents

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Financial | Fraud, theft, misappropriation, falsification of records |
| Compliance | Regulatory breach, licence non-compliance, tax evasion |
| Ethical | Bribery, corruption, conflict of interest, harassment |
| Operational | Property damage, system failures, data breaches |
| Health & Safety | Workplace injury, unsafe conditions, near misses |
| Legal | Contract disputes, litigation threats, regulatory investigations |

### 9.3 Reporting Channels

| Channel | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Direct Reporting** | Report to immediate supervisor or line manager |
| **Legal/Compliance Officer** | For compliance, ethical, and legal matters |
| **HR Manager** | For employment-related and harassment matters |
| **Anonymous Whistleblowing** | Written submission (sealed envelope marked "Confidential — Whistleblowing") to MD or Board Chair |
| **Email Hotline** | Dedicated email address accessible only by Legal/Compliance Officer and MD |
| **External** | Uganda Police, Inspectorate of Government, Financial Intelligence Authority (as required by law) |

### 9.4 Whistleblower Protections

In accordance with the Whistleblowers Protection Act 2010 of Uganda:

1. No person shall be subjected to retaliation for making a good-faith report
2. Whistleblower identity will be protected to the maximum extent possible
3. Retaliatory actions (termination, demotion, harassment) are prohibited and will result in disciplinary action
4. Anonymous reports will be accepted and investigated
5. The company will not require whistleblowers to identify themselves to receive protection

### 9.5 Investigation Framework

| 1 | Receive and log report | Within 24 hours | Legal/Compliance Officer |

| 5 | Conduct investigation | Within 30 business days | Investigation team |

## 10. Litigation and Dispute Management Framework

### 10.1 Purpose

This framework governs the management of actual or potential litigation, regulatory proceedings, and commercial disputes involving Awdeer Investments Limited.

### 10.2 Dispute Resolution Hierarchy

Disputes should be resolved at the lowest appropriate level:

1. **Negotiation** — Direct communication between parties to resolve the matter
2. **Mediation** — Engagement of a neutral third-party mediator (Centre for Arbitration and Dispute Resolution — CADER)
3. **Arbitration** — Binding determination by an arbitrator (per Arbitration and Conciliation Act, Cap 4)
4. **Litigation** — Court proceedings as a last resort (Commercial Court of Uganda for commercial disputes)

### 10.3 Litigation Management Process

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Receive claim / threat of proceedings | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| 2 | Assess merits and potential exposure | Legal/Compliance Officer + External counsel |
| 3 | Report to MD (and Board if material) | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| 4 | Appoint external legal counsel (if required) | MD / Board |
| 5 | Develop litigation strategy | Legal/Compliance Officer + External counsel |
| 6 | Preserve documents and evidence | Legal/Compliance Officer + IT |
| 7 | Manage proceedings | External counsel (supervised by Legal/Compliance Officer) |
| 8 | Settlement authority (per thresholds below) | Per authority matrix |
| 9 | Record outcome and lessons learned | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| 10 | Update risk register | Legal/Compliance Officer |

### 10.4 Settlement Authority

| Settlement Value (UGX) | Authority Required |
|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Up to 10,000,000 | Managing Director |
| 10,000,001 – 50,000,000 | Managing Director + CFO/Finance Manager |
| 50,000,001 – 200,000,000 | Managing Director + Board Chair |
| Above 200,000,000 | Board Resolution |

### 10.5 Legal Provisions

All company contracts shall include:

- Governing law: Laws of the Republic of Uganda
- Dispute resolution: Negotiation → Mediation → Arbitration (preferred) or Litigation
- Arbitration seat: Kampala, Uganda
- Court jurisdiction: Courts of Uganda (Commercial Division where applicable)

## 11. Lean vs Controlled Version Summary

### Lean Version (Small Team < 15 people)

| Process Area | Lean Approach |
|--------------|---------------|
| **Contract Management** | MD reviews and approves all contracts. Simple contract register in spreadsheet. Legal review by external lawyer for contracts above UGX 20M. HR & Admin Officer maintains contract files. |
| **Compliance Register** | HR & Admin Officer maintains a spreadsheet-based compliance calendar with quarterly review by MD. External accountant handles URA filings. |
| **Anti-Bribery** | MD communicates zero-tolerance policy. Annual staff acknowledgment. Gift register maintained by HR & Admin Officer. |
| **AML/KYC** | Basic customer/supplier verification (ID, TIN, registration). Finance Manager conducts checks. Red flags reported to MD. |
| **Data Protection** | Basic data handling guidelines. MD acts as data controller. Password protection and locked filing cabinets. Annual awareness reminder. |
| **Incident/Whistleblowing** | Open-door policy with MD. Anonymous written reports accepted. MD investigates with HR & Admin Officer support. |
| **Litigation** | External lawyer engaged as needed. MD manages with legal advice. Dispute register maintained in spreadsheet. |

### Controlled Version (Mid-size Team 15-100+ people)

| Process Area | Controlled Approach |
|--------------|---------------------|
| **Contract Management** | Dedicated Legal/Compliance Officer manages full lifecycle. ERP-based contract module. Multi-level approval per DoA. External law firm on retainer. |
| **Compliance Register** | Dedicated compliance register with automated alerts. Legal/Compliance Officer monitors all deadlines. Quarterly compliance report to MD and Board. |
| **Anti-Bribery** | Formal ABC policy with annual training. Gift register system. Annual conflict of interest declarations. Third-party due diligence for agents/intermediaries. |
| **AML/KYC** | Formal CDD/EDD procedures. Risk-based approach. Dedicated KYC files. STR procedures with FIA. Ongoing monitoring of high-risk relationships. |
| **Data Protection** | Full DPPA 2019 compliance programme. Data protection impact assessments. Data register. Breach notification procedures. Registration with PDPO. Annual training. |
| **Incident/Whistleblowing** | Formal incident management system. Multiple reporting channels. Dedicated investigation procedure. Case management register. Board reporting for serious matters. |
| **Litigation** | Litigation management system. External counsel panel. Settlement authority matrix. Document preservation protocols. Quarterly litigation report to Board. |
