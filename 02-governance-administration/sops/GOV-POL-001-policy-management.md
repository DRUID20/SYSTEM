## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines how policies, SOPs, and related governance documents are created, reviewed, approved, published, communicated, and retired within Awdeer Investments Limited. It ensures consistent quality, proper version control, and timely review of all company policies.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP covers the full lifecycle of:

- Policies (Level 2 documents — Board-approved statements of intent)
- Standard Operating Procedures (Level 3 — management-approved process documents)
- Templates and forms (Level 4 — supporting documents)

It applies to all departments and subsidiaries of Awdeer Investments Limited.

**Exclusions:**
- The Governance Framework itself (GOV-FW-001) follows its own approval process (Board-level)
- External legal agreements and contracts (managed by Legal)

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

### 3.1 Key Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Process Owner** | Identifies the need for a new or updated policy; drafts content; ensures implementation |
| **Head of Department (HOD)** | Reviews policy for departmental relevance and accuracy; ensures team compliance |
| **Company Secretary (CoSec)** | Coordinates policy lifecycle; maintains policy register; ensures version control |
| **Managing Director (MD)** | Approves SOPs and templates; recommends policies to Board |
| **Board of Directors** | Approves all Level 2 policies |
| **All Staff** | Read, understand, and comply with applicable policies |

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Phase 1: Initiation

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Identify need for new or revised policy (regulatory change, Board direction, audit finding, operational need) | Process Owner / CoSec | Ongoing |
| 1.2 | Process Owner completes Policy Approval Form (GOV-TPL-005) Section 1: Request Details | Process Owner | 2 business days |
| 1.3 | CoSec reviews request for duplication (does a similar policy already exist?) | CoSec | 2 business days |
| 1.4 | CoSec assigns document ID and number per naming convention | CoSec | 1 business day |
| 1.5 | MD confirms go-ahead for drafting (for new policies); for reviews, proceed automatically | MD | 2 business days |

### Phase 2: Drafting

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Process Owner drafts policy using the standard template structure | Process Owner | 5-10 business days |
| 2.2 | Draft must include: Purpose, Scope, Definitions, Policy statements, Roles & responsibilities, Compliance requirements, Related documents, Version history | Process Owner | — |
| 2.3 | CoSec reviews draft for formatting, numbering, consistency with other policies | CoSec | 3 business days |
| 2.4 | CoSec returns draft with formatting comments (if any) | CoSec | — |
| 2.5 | Process Owner incorporates formatting feedback | Process Owner | 2 business days |
| 2.6 | Document version set to 0.1 (draft) | CoSec | — |

### Phase 3: Review

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | HOD reviews draft for technical accuracy, operational feasibility | HOD | 5 business days |
| 3.2 | Cross-functional review if policy impacts multiple departments (CoSec coordinates) | Relevant HODs | 5 business days |
| 3.3 | Legal review if policy has legal/regulatory implications | CoSec / Legal | 5 business days |
| 3.4 | Process Owner incorporates all review comments | Process Owner | 3 business days |
| 3.5 | Updated version (e.g., 0.2, 0.3) circulated as needed until reviewers are satisfied | CoSec | — |
| 3.6 | Final draft prepared for approval | Process Owner | — |

### Phase 4: Approval

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | CoSec completes Policy Approval Form (GOV-TPL-005) Section 2: Approval routing | CoSec | 1 business day |
| 4.2 | **For SOPs and Templates:** MD reviews and approves; signs approval form | MD | 5 business days |
| 4.3 | **For Policies (Level 2):** MD recommends to Board; included in next Board pack or circulated as written resolution | MD / CoSec | Next Board meeting |
| 4.4 | Board considers and approves policy (or returns with comments) | Board | At Board meeting |
| 4.5 | CoSec updates version to 1.0 (first approved version) and records approval date | CoSec | 1 business day |
| 4.6 | CoSec files signed Policy Approval Form | CoSec | Same day |

### Phase 5: Publication & Communication

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | CoSec publishes approved document in the document management system | CoSec | Within 2 business days of approval |
| 5.2 | CoSec updates the Master Policy Register | CoSec | Same day |
| 5.3 | CoSec sends communication to all affected staff (email / notice) with summary of key points | CoSec | Within 3 business days |
| 5.4 | Process Owner / HOD conducts training or briefing for affected teams | Process Owner / HOD | Within 10 business days |
| 5.5 | Staff acknowledge receipt and understanding (sign-off or electronic confirmation) | All affected staff | Within 5 business days of training |
| 5.6 | Training records filed | HR / Process Owner | Same day |

### Phase 6: Monitoring & Compliance

| Step | Action | Responsible | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Process Owner monitors compliance with the policy in day-to-day operations | Process Owner | Ongoing |
| 6.2 | CoSec conducts spot checks on policy compliance | CoSec | Quarterly |
| 6.3 | Non-compliance incidents reported and logged | Process Owner / CoSec | As they occur |
| 6.4 | Material non-compliance escalated to MD | CoSec | As needed |
| 6.5 | Policy compliance included in annual governance report | CoSec | Annual |

### Phase 7: Periodic Review

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | CoSec generates list of policies due for review (from policy register) | CoSec | Quarterly (per review cycle in Governance Framework Section 5.5) |
| 7.2 | CoSec notifies Process Owners of policies due for review | CoSec | 30 days before review due date |
| 7.3 | Process Owner reviews policy and determines: (a) No changes needed — confirm current version, (b) Minor updates needed — proceed to Phase 2-4, (c) Major revision needed — proceed to Phase 1-5 | Process Owner | Within 20 business days |
| 7.4 | Process Owner completes Policy Approval Form indicating review outcome | Process Owner | — |
| 7.5 | If no changes: MD (or Board for policies) confirms the policy remains in force; next review date updated | MD / Board | 5 business days |
| 7.6 | If changes: Follow Phase 2-5 above | — | As per Phase 2-5 timelines |
| 7.7 | CoSec updates policy register with new review date | CoSec | 1 business day |

### Phase 8: Retirement / Archival

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | Process Owner identifies that a policy is no longer needed (superseded, activity ceased, etc.) | Process Owner | As needed |
| 8.2 | MD (SOPs) or Board (Policies) approves retirement | MD / Board | 5 business days |
| 8.3 | CoSec marks policy as "Retired" in policy register with retirement date | CoSec | 1 business day |
| 8.4 | Document moved to archive folder; retained per retention schedule (3 years after replacement) | CoSec | Same day |
| 8.5 | Staff notified that policy is no longer in effect | CoSec | Within 2 business days |

## 6. Controls & Approvals

| Control | Description | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Document numbering** | All documents assigned unique ID per naming convention before drafting begins | CoSec |
| **Version control** | All drafts tracked with version numbers (0.x for drafts, x.0 for approved) | CoSec |
| **Approval authority** | Policies require Board approval; SOPs require MD approval; Templates require MD/CFO approval | CoSec |
| **Dual review** | All policies reviewed by at least HOD + CoSec before approval | CoSec |
| **No self-approval** | Policy drafter cannot be the sole approver | CoSec |
| **Review scheduling** | Policy register tracks review dates; CoSec generates quarterly review lists | CoSec |
| **Overdue escalation** | Policies overdue for review by > 30 days escalated to MD | CoSec |
| **Communication confirmation** | Staff must acknowledge receipt and understanding of new/updated policies | Process Owner |
| **Audit trail** | Policy Approval Form retained for each approval cycle | CoSec |
