## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for receiving, logging, investigating, resolving, and learning from customer complaints. It ensures every complaint is acknowledged promptly, handled fairly, resolved within defined service levels, and analysed for systemic improvements. The process supports customer retention, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement across all business lines of Awdeer Investments Limited.

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Receive Complaint

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Receive the customer complaint through any channel |
| **Who** | Any Awdeer staff member who first receives the complaint |
| **Channels** | Phone call, email, walk-in, letter, social media message, WhatsApp |
| **Immediate response** | Listen actively, show empathy, assure the customer the matter will be addressed, do not argue or assign blame |
| **SLA** | Immediate acknowledgement during the interaction; formal logging within same business day |
| **Notes** | If the staff member cannot resolve immediately (most cases), inform the customer that the complaint will be formally logged and a designated person will follow up |

### Step 2: Log Complaint

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Record the complaint in the complaint register using the Customer Complaint Form (SAL-TPL-005) |
| **Who** | Receiving staff member or Sales Executive |
| **Required Information** | Complaint reference number (auto-generated: `AWD-CMP-YYYY-NNNN`); Date and time received; Customer name and contact details; Order/contract reference (if applicable); Description of complaint (customer's own words where possible); Channel received through; Business line affected |
| **SLA** | Within 2 hours of complaint receipt during business hours |
| **Tool** | CRM complaint/ticket module (Controlled) or complaint register spreadsheet (Lean) |

### Step 3: Assign Severity and Complaint Owner

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Classify the complaint severity and assign a complaint owner responsible for resolution |
| **Who** | Sales Manager |
| **Severity Levels** | |
| | **Critical**: Product/service failure causing financial loss, safety issue, contract breach, legal/regulatory risk. Resolution target: 24 hours initial response, 3 business days full resolution |
| | **Major**: Significant quality issue, repeated delivery delays, billing error > UGX 1,000,000, escalated customer. Resolution target: 1 business day acknowledgement, 5 business days resolution |
| | **Minor**: Small billing discrepancy, minor delay, communication gap, one-time issue. Resolution target: 1 business day acknowledgement, 7 business days resolution |
| | **Feedback**: Suggestion for improvement, general positive/negative comment. Resolution target: Acknowledge within 2 business days, log for review |
| **Complaint Owner** | Typically the Sales Executive managing the account; or relevant department head if issue is in their area (Operations for delivery, Finance for billing) |
| **SLA** | Within 4 hours of complaint being logged |
| **Notifications** | Critical: MD notified immediately; Major: Sales Manager and MD notified same day |

### Step 4: Acknowledge Complaint to Customer

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Send formal acknowledgement to the customer confirming receipt and providing the complaint reference number |
| **Who** | Complaint Owner (Sales Executive or assigned person) |
| **Content** | Thank customer for bringing the matter to attention; Confirm complaint reference number; Name and contact details of the person handling the complaint; Expected timeline for investigation and resolution; Assurance that the matter is being taken seriously |
| **Channel** | Same channel as complaint was received (or email if complaint was verbal) |
| **SLA** | Per severity: Critical: within 4 hours; Major: within 1 business day; Minor: within 1 business day; Feedback: within 2 business days |

### Step 5: Investigate Root Cause

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Investigate the complaint to determine what happened, why, and what the appropriate resolution is |
| **Who** | Complaint Owner, with support from relevant departments |
| **How** | Review order/delivery/service records; Interview relevant staff; Check system records and documentation; Identify whether the issue is isolated or systemic; Document findings |
| **SLA** | Investigation completed: Critical: within 1 business day; Major: within 3 business days; Minor: within 5 business days |
| **Notes** | If investigation will take longer than the SLA, update the customer with progress and revised timeline |

### Step 6: Determine Resolution

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Determine the appropriate resolution and obtain necessary approvals |
| **Who** | Complaint Owner proposes; Sales Manager approves; MD approves (for critical or financial remedies) |
| **Resolution Options** | Explanation and apology (if Awdeer was not at fault, explain clearly); Correction of error (re-delivery, corrected invoice, service redo); Replacement of product; Partial or full refund / credit note; Discount on future order; Compensation for damages (requires MD approval for any amount > UGX 5,000,000) |
| **Approval Required** | Apology/explanation: Complaint Owner; Correction/replacement at Awdeer's cost: Sales Manager; Credit note / refund up to UGX 5,000,000: Sales Manager + Finance Manager; Credit note / refund > UGX 5,000,000: MD; Any compensation > UGX 10,000,000: MD + legal advice |
| **SLA** | Resolution determined within investigation SLA |

### Step 7: Communicate Resolution to Customer

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Contact the customer with the proposed resolution |
| **Who** | Complaint Owner |
| **How** | Phone call followed by written confirmation (email); Explain what was found, what action is being taken, and the timeline for implementation; Ask the customer if the resolution is acceptable |
| **SLA** | Same day as resolution is determined and approved |
| **If customer rejects resolution** | Escalate to Sales Manager (or MD for critical); attempt alternative resolution; document the customer's position |

### Step 8: Implement Resolution

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Execute the agreed resolution |
| **Who** | Complaint Owner + relevant department |
| **Examples** | Operations re-delivers or replaces product; Finance issues credit note or refund; Sales applies discount to next order |
| **SLA** | Per the timeline communicated to the customer |
| **Tracking** | Update complaint register with implementation status |

### Step 9: Follow Up with Customer

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Contact the customer after resolution implementation to confirm satisfaction |
| **Who** | Complaint Owner or Sales Manager (for Major/Critical) |
| **How** | Phone call or email: confirm resolution was implemented; ask if customer is satisfied; invite further feedback |
| **SLA** | 3 business days after resolution implementation |
| **Optional** | Send Customer Feedback Survey (SAL-TPL-006) for Major/Critical complaints |

### Step 10: Close Complaint

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Close the complaint in the register and complete the record |
| **Who** | Complaint Owner; Sales Manager confirms closure |
| **Record** | Update complaint register with: resolution implemented, date closed, customer satisfaction confirmed (Yes/No), total resolution cost (if any), lessons learned |
| **SLA** | Within 2 business days of customer confirmation |

### Step 11: Monthly Review and Corrective Action

| Detail | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Action** | Review all complaints from the month to identify trends, systemic issues, and improvement opportunities |
| **Who** | Sales Manager (facilitates); MD (reviews); relevant department heads (participate) |
| **Agenda** | Total complaints: volume by severity and category; Resolution time: average vs SLA; Repeat issues: same customer, same problem type; Systemic issues: patterns requiring corrective action; Customer satisfaction with resolution; Cost of complaints (credit notes, replacements, etc.) |
| **Output** | Monthly complaint summary report to MD; Corrective action requests to relevant departments (logged in CAPA tracker if applicable); Process improvements identified and assigned |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |

## 6. Controls & Approvals

| Control Point | Control Description | Owner |
|--------------|-------------------|-------|
| Mandatory logging | Every complaint must be logged, regardless of perceived severity | Sales Manager enforces |
| Unique complaint reference | Sequential numbering prevents gaps or duplicates | CRM/system |
| Severity classification | Standardised severity levels ensure appropriate urgency and resources | Sales Manager |
| SLA tracking | Response and resolution times tracked against defined SLAs | Sales Manager / CRM |
| Approval for financial remedies | Credit notes, refunds, and compensation require appropriate approval per threshold | Sales Manager / MD / Finance |
| Customer acknowledgement | Written acknowledgement sent within SLA; recorded in complaint file | Complaint Owner |
| Escalation triggers | Automatic escalation if SLA is about to be breached or if customer is dissatisfied with resolution | Sales Manager / MD |
| Root cause documentation | Investigation findings documented for every Major/Critical complaint | Complaint Owner |
| Closure verification | Complaint not closed until customer confirms satisfaction (or escalation is complete) | Sales Manager |
| Monthly review | All complaints reviewed for patterns and systemic issues | Sales Manager + MD |
| Segregation | Complaint owner cannot approve own financial remedies | Sales Manager / MD |
| Audit trail | Full complaint history retained: logging, investigation, resolution, communications | CRM / complaint register |

## 8. KPIs

| NPS Impact | Change in NPS score correlated with complaint trends | Positive trend | Quarterly |

## 9. Lean Version — Summary

| Element | Lean Approach |
|---------|--------------|
| Complaint receipt | Any staff logs in shared spreadsheet; Sales Lead is primary handler |
| Complaint form | Simplified spreadsheet row: Date, Customer, Issue, Severity, Status, Resolution, Date Closed |
| Severity assignment | Sales Lead assigns; escalates Critical to MD immediately |
| Acknowledgement | Phone call or WhatsApp message to customer same day |
| Investigation | Sales Lead investigates with relevant colleague; informal |
| Resolution approval | Sales Lead resolves Minor; MD approves Major/Critical and any financial remedy |
| Follow-up | Sales Lead calls customer after resolution |
| Monthly review | MD reviews complaint spreadsheet monthly; discusses patterns with Sales Lead |
| Reporting | Monthly count: total, open, closed, average days to resolve |
| Corrective action | MD and Sales Lead agree on fixes informally; tracked in spreadsheet |

## 10. Controlled Version — Summary

| Element | Controlled Approach |
|---------|-------------------|
| Complaint receipt | Logged in CRM complaint/helpdesk module; auto-generated reference number |
| Complaint form | Full SAL-TPL-005 form completed and attached to CRM record |
| Severity assignment | Sales Manager assigns per defined criteria; CRM routes and triggers alerts |
| Acknowledgement | Auto-generated acknowledgement email from CRM; personalised by Complaint Owner |
| Investigation | Structured investigation: interviews, record review, timeline analysis, root cause documented |
| Resolution approval | Tiered approval in CRM: Sales Manager for minor; MD for critical/financial |
| Follow-up | Automated follow-up survey (SAL-TPL-006) triggered 3 days after resolution |
| Monthly review | Formal monthly meeting with complaint dashboard: volume, trends, SLA performance, cost |
| Reporting | Dashboard: complaint volume by category/severity, resolution time, customer satisfaction, cost |
| Corrective action | Formal CAPA process for systemic issues; tracked in CAPA register; auditable |
| SLA monitoring | CRM tracks SLA countdown; auto-escalation if approaching breach |
| Analytics | Trend analysis: complaint hotspots by business line, product, customer, root cause |

## 11. Escalation Quick Reference

| Timeframe | Action | Who |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Complaint received | Log and assign | Receiving staff / Sales Manager |
| Within 4 hours | Acknowledge (Critical) | Complaint Owner |
| Within 1 business day | Acknowledge (Major/Minor) | Complaint Owner |
| SLA - 1 day (approaching breach) | Escalate to Sales Manager | Complaint Owner / CRM auto |
| SLA breached | Escalate to MD | Sales Manager |
| Customer rejects resolution | Escalate to MD | Sales Manager |
| Customer threatens legal action | Escalate to MD + Legal | Sales Manager |
| Complaint reveals systemic issue | Escalate to MD + department heads | Sales Manager |
| Financial remedy > UGX 5M | MD approval required | Sales Manager |
