## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines how documents are created, named, stored, version-controlled, accessed, archived, and destroyed within Awdeer Investments Limited. It ensures that the company maintains an organised, secure, and compliant document management system that meets the requirements of the Uganda Companies Act 2012, URA tax record-keeping obligations, and internal governance standards.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP covers all company documents including:

- Governance documents (frameworks, policies, SOPs, templates)
- Board and shareholder meeting documents (minutes, resolutions, registers)
- Financial records (accounts, tax filings, audit reports, bank statements)
- Legal documents (contracts, agreements, licences, permits)
- HR records (employment contracts, payroll, leave records)
- Operational documents (project files, correspondence, reports)
- Investment records (deal memos, valuations, portfolio reports)

It applies to documents in all formats:
- Digital files (documents, spreadsheets, presentations, emails)
- Physical documents (signed originals, statutory books)

**Exclusions:**
- Personal files of employees (governed by HR policy)
- IT system logs and backups (governed by IT policy)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Guidelines |

#### 5.1 Naming Convention Rules

**Controlled documents (policies, SOPs, templates):**
```
[DEPT]-[TYPE]-[SEQ]-[short-description].[ext]
```
Example: `GOV-SOP-001-board-meetings.md`

**Meeting documents:**
```
[YYYY-MM-DD]-[meeting-type]-[description].[ext]
```
Example: `2026-03-15-board-meeting-minutes.pdf`

**Financial documents:**
```
[YYYY-MM]-[report-type]-[description].[ext]
```
Example: `2026-03-monthly-management-accounts.xlsx`

**Contracts and legal documents:**
```
[YYYY-MM-DD]-[counterparty]-[document-type].[ext]
```
Example: `2026-01-15-xyz-ltd-service-agreement.pdf`

**Correspondence:**
```
[YYYY-MM-DD]-[from/to]-[subject].[ext]
```
Example: `2026-02-10-ura-tax-assessment-response.pdf`

**General rules:**
- All lowercase
- Use hyphens (-) instead of spaces
- No special characters other than hyphens
- Keep descriptions short but meaningful (3-5 words)
- Include date in ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD) where applicable
- File extension must match content (.md, .pdf, .xlsx, .docx)

#### 5.2 Classification Guidelines

| Classification | Definition | Marking | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Public** | No restrictions; can be shared externally | None required | Company brochure, public policy statements |
| **Internal** | For company staff only; not for external distribution | "Internal" in header | SOPs, general procedures, templates |
| **Confidential** | Restricted to named roles/individuals; sensitive business information | "Confidential" in header | Financial reports, board packs, HR records, contracts |
| **Restricted** | Highest sensitivity; Board and specified C-suite only | "Restricted" in header | M&A documents, legal strategy, Board minutes on sensitive matters |

### Phase 3: Version Control

| Step | Action | Responsible | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | When updating a controlled document, increment the version number appropriately | Document Owner | 0.x = draft; x.0 = major approved version; x.y = minor update |
| 3.2 | Update the version history table within the document | Document Owner | Record: version, date, author, change description, approver |
| 3.3 | Follow the applicable approval workflow (per GOV-POL-001 Policy Management SOP) | Document Owner | — |
| 3.4 | Once new version is approved, replace the current version in the active folder | CoSec | — |
| 3.5 | Move the superseded version to the archive folder with "SUPERSEDED" prefix | CoSec | Example: `SUPERSEDED-GOV-SOP-001-board-meetings-v1.0.md` |
| 3.6 | Update the Master Policy Register / document register | CoSec | — |
| 3.7 | Notify affected staff of the updated document | CoSec / Document Owner | — |

**Version numbering rules:**

| Change Type | Version Change | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Initial draft | 0.1 | First working draft |
| Subsequent drafts | 0.2, 0.3, etc. | Incorporating review comments |
| First approved version | 1.0 | Approved by MD or Board |
| Minor amendment (editorial, typo, clarification) | 1.1, 1.2, etc. | No material change to content |
| Major revision (material content change) | 2.0, 3.0, etc. | Requires full approval cycle |

### Phase 5: Retention & Archival

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | CoSec maintains a retention schedule aligned with legal requirements (see Governance Framework Section 7.3) | CoSec | Standing |
| 5.2 | Annually, CoSec identifies documents that have reached end of retention period | CoSec | January |
| 5.3 | CoSec prepares a destruction list for MD review | CoSec | February |
| 5.4 | MD reviews and approves destruction list (or extends retention where warranted) | MD | February |
| 5.5 | Documents approaching end of active use but still within retention period are moved to archive | CoSec | Ongoing |
| 5.6 | Archived documents organised by year and category in the archive folder | CoSec | — |

### Phase 6: Document Destruction

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | MD-approved destruction list received by CoSec | CoSec | Per Phase 5 |
| 6.2 | Verify no legal holds or ongoing investigations require retention beyond schedule | CoSec / Legal | Before destruction |
| 6.3 | **Digital documents:** Permanently delete from all storage locations including backups | IT / CoSec | — |
| 6.4 | **Physical documents:** Shred using cross-cut shredder; witness required for Confidential/Restricted documents | CoSec | — |
| 6.5 | Prepare destruction certificate recording: document description, retention period, destruction date, method, witness | CoSec | Same day |
| 6.6 | MD signs destruction certificate | MD | Within 2 business days |
| 6.7 | Destruction certificate filed permanently in governance records | CoSec | Same day |

**Documents that must NEVER be destroyed:**
- Statutory minute books
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Share registers and certificates
- Statutory registers (directors, charges, etc.)
- Board resolutions (originals)
- Company seal records (if applicable)

## 6. Controls & Approvals

| Control | Description | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Naming convention enforcement** | All documents must follow the standard naming convention; non-compliant files flagged during audit | CoSec |
| **Classification marking** | Confidential and Restricted documents must be clearly marked in header | Document Owner |
| **Access restriction** | IT enforces folder-level access permissions; individual overrides require MD approval | IT / MD |
| **Version control** | Controlled documents must have version history; only current version in active folder | CoSec |
| **Approval before publication** | No controlled document published without proper approval (per GOV-POL-001) | CoSec |
| **Backup** | All digital documents backed up daily; tested monthly | IT |
| **Physical security** | Confidential/Restricted physical documents in locked storage; key held by CoSec/MD | CoSec |
| **Destruction authorisation** | All document destruction requires MD written approval + destruction certificate | MD / CoSec |
| **Legal hold** | No documents under legal hold may be destroyed regardless of retention period | CoSec / Legal |
| **Annual audit** | Document control system audited annually; findings reported to MD | CoSec |
