## 1. HR System Overview and Philosophy

### 1.1 Purpose

This document establishes the complete Human Resources management framework for Awdeer Investments Limited, operating under the laws of the Republic of Uganda. It covers the full employee lifecycle from recruitment through exit, ensuring compliance with the Employment Act 2006, the National Social Security Fund (NSSF) Act, the Workers Compensation Act, and all applicable Ugandan labour regulations.

### 1.2 Philosophy

Awdeer Investments Limited believes that people are its most valuable asset. Our HR philosophy is built on five pillars:

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Fairness** | Equal treatment regardless of gender, ethnicity, religion, or background, in line with Uganda's Equal Opportunities Commission Act |
| **Transparency** | Clear policies, open communication, and documented processes |
| **Development** | Continuous investment in employee growth and career progression |
| **Compliance** | Full adherence to Ugandan employment law and international best practices |
| **Accountability** | Clear roles, responsibilities, and performance expectations |

### 1.3 Scope

This HR system applies to:

- All full-time and part-time employees of Awdeer Investments Limited
- Contract and temporary workers (where specified)
- Interns and industrial trainees
- All branches and operational locations within Uganda

### 1.4 Legal Framework

| Legislation | Relevance |
|-------------|-----------|
| Employment Act, 2006 | Core employment rights, contracts, termination, leave |
| National Social Security Fund (NSSF) Act, Cap 222 (as amended 2022) | Mandatory pension contributions |
| Income Tax Act (as amended) | PAYE, employment income taxation |
| Workers Compensation Act, Cap 225 | Workplace injury and compensation |
| Occupational Safety and Health Act, 2006 | Workplace safety standards |
| Equal Opportunities Commission Act, 2007 | Non-discrimination |
| The Employment (Sexual Harassment) Regulations, 2012 | Anti-harassment |
| Local Governments Act (LST provisions) | Local Service Tax deductions |

## 2. Organisational Design

### 2.1 Principles

The organisational structure at Awdeer Investments Limited follows these design principles:

1. **Span of control** — No manager supervises more than 8 direct reports
2. **Clear reporting lines** — Every employee has exactly one direct supervisor
3. **Role clarity** — Each position has a documented job description with clear deliverables
4. **Scalability** — The structure accommodates growth without requiring fundamental redesign

### 2.2 Organisational Hierarchy

```
Level 1: Board of Directors
    │
Level 2: Managing Director / CEO
    │
Level 3: Directors / Department Heads
    │
Level 4: Senior Managers
    │

    │

    │
Level 7: Junior Staff / Assistants
```

## 3. Job Grades and Salary Bands Framework

### 3.1 Grade Structure

**ASSUMPTION:** Five (5) job grades are established as the foundation of the compensation framework.

| Grade | Title | Typical Roles | Min Experience |
|-------|-------|---------------|----------------|
| **G1** | Junior / Assistant | Office Assistant, Junior Accountant, Intern, Trainee | 0–2 years |
| **G2** | Officer | Accounts Officer, Sales Officer, HR Officer, IT Officer | 2–4 years |
| **G3** | Manager | Department Manager, Team Lead, Project Manager | 4–7 years |
| **G4** | Senior Manager | Senior Department Manager, Regional Manager, Head of Unit | 7–10 years |
| **G5** | Director | Department Director, C-Suite, General Manager | 10+ years |

### 3.2 Salary Bands (UGX per Month — Gross)

**ASSUMPTION:** These bands reflect the Ugandan professional services market and are subject to annual review.

| Grade | Minimum (UGX) | Midpoint (UGX) | Maximum (UGX) |
|-------|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| G1 — Junior | 800,000 | 1,200,000 | 1,600,000 |
| G2 — Officer | 1,500,000 | 2,250,000 | 3,000,000 |
| G3 — Manager | 3,000,000 | 4,500,000 | 6,000,000 |
| G4 — Senior Manager | 5,000,000 | 7,500,000 | 10,000,000 |
| G5 — Director | 8,000,000 | 12,000,000 | 18,000,000 |

### 3.3 Salary Band Rules

1. **New hires** typically enter at the minimum or midpoint of their grade band
2. **Promotions** move the employee to the minimum of the next grade band (or current salary + 15%, whichever is higher)
3. **Annual increments** are capped at 10% of the current gross salary, subject to performance appraisal results
4. **Market adjustments** may be applied based on annual salary survey benchmarking
5. **Salary above band maximum** triggers a review — either promote the employee or freeze increments

### 3.4 Benefits by Grade

|---------|----|----|----|----|-----|
| NSSF (statutory) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

| Airtime allowance | — | UGX 50,000 | UGX 100,000 | UGX 200,000 | UGX 300,000 |

## 5. Payroll Workflow and Approvals

### 5.1 Uganda Payroll Statutory Context

#### 5.1.1 Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) — Income Tax Act (as amended)

Monthly PAYE brackets (individual, resident):

| Annual Chargeable Income (UGX) | Monthly Equivalent (UGX) | Rate |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------|------|
| 0 – 2,820,000 | 0 – 235,000 | 0% |
| 2,820,001 – 4,020,000 | 235,001 – 335,000 | 10% |
| 4,020,001 – 4,920,000 | 335,001 – 410,000 | 20% |
| Above 4,920,000 | Above 410,000 | 30% |

*Note: An additional 10% surcharge applies to employment income exceeding UGX 120,000,000 per annum (UGX 10,000,000 per month).*

#### 5.1.2 National Social Security Fund (NSSF)

| Contribution | Rate | Basis |
|-------------|------|-------|
| Employee contribution | 5% | Gross salary |
| Employer contribution | 10% | Gross salary |
| **Total** | **15%** | Gross salary |

- Mandatory for all employees aged 16 and above
- Employer remits both contributions to NSSF by the 15th of the following month
- Reference: NSSF Act, Cap 222 (as amended)

#### 5.1.3 Local Service Tax (LST)

Charged based on the employee's monthly gross income:

| Monthly Income Band (UGX) | Annual LST (UGX) | Monthly Deduction (UGX) |
|---------------------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Below 100,000 | Nil | Nil |
| 100,000 – 200,000 | 10,000 | Approx. 833 |
| 200,001 – 300,000 | 20,000 | Approx. 1,667 |
| 300,001 – 400,000 | 30,000 | Approx. 2,500 |
| 400,001 – 500,000 | 40,000 | Approx. 3,333 |
| 500,001 – 1,000,000 | 60,000 | Approx. 5,000 |
| 1,000,001 – 2,000,000 | 80,000 | Approx. 6,667 |
| 2,000,001 – 3,000,000 | 100,000 | Approx. 8,333 |
| 3,000,001 – 4,000,000 | 120,000 | Approx. 10,000 |
| 4,000,001 – 5,000,000 | 130,000 | Approx. 10,833 |
| Above 5,000,000 | 150,000 | Approx. 12,500 |

- Deducted monthly and remitted to the relevant local government authority
- Reference: Local Governments Act

### 5.2 Payroll Processing Workflow

```
Step 1: Payroll Data Collection (by 20th of each month)
    ├── Attendance and leave records
    ├── New hires, terminations, changes
    ├── Overtime, allowances, deductions
    └── Loan repayments, advances
        │
Step 2: Payroll Preparation (21st – 23rd)
    ├── HR Officer compiles payroll input sheet
    ├── Calculate gross pay per employee
    ├── Apply statutory deductions (PAYE, NSSF, LST)
    ├── Apply non-statutory deductions (loans, advances, SACCO)
    └── Generate net pay schedule
        │
Step 3: Payroll Review (24th – 25th)
    ├── HR Manager reviews for accuracy
    ├── Finance Manager verifies calculations and budget
    └── Flag exceptions and resolve queries
        │
Step 4: Payroll Approval
    ├── Lean: Finance Manager + MD approval
    └── Controlled: HR Manager → Finance Manager → CFO → MD
        │
Step 5: Payment Execution (by 28th)
    ├── Finance processes bank transfer file
    ├── Salary credited to individual bank accounts
    └── Payslips generated and distributed
        │
Step 6: Statutory Remittances (by 15th of following month)
    ├── PAYE → URA e-tax portal
    ├── NSSF → NSSF portal
    └── LST → Local government
        │
Step 7: Reconciliation and Filing
    ├── Reconcile payroll to GL
    ├── File statutory returns
    └── Archive payroll records
```

## 6. Code of Conduct and Conflict-of-Interest Policy

### 6.1 Code of Conduct

#### 6.1.1 Purpose

This code defines the standards of behaviour expected of all employees, officers, and directors of Awdeer Investments Limited.

#### 6.1.2 General Principles

All employees shall:

1. **Act with integrity** — Be honest, ethical, and trustworthy in all dealings
2. **Respect others** — Treat colleagues, customers, suppliers, and stakeholders with dignity
3. **Protect company assets** — Use company resources responsibly and only for authorised purposes
4. **Maintain confidentiality** — Safeguard proprietary and sensitive information
5. **Comply with laws** — Follow all Ugandan laws, regulations, and company policies
6. **Avoid conflicts of interest** — Disclose any situation that may compromise impartiality
7. **Report misconduct** — Report suspected fraud, theft, harassment, or policy violations

#### 6.1.3 Specific Prohibitions

The following are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate dismissal:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| **Fraud and theft** | Falsifying records, stealing company property, embezzlement |
| **Harassment** | Sexual harassment, bullying, intimidation (ref: Employment (Sexual Harassment) Regulations, 2012) |
| **Substance abuse** | Being under the influence of alcohol or drugs at work |
| **Discrimination** | Unfair treatment based on gender, ethnicity, religion, disability |
| **Bribery and corruption** | Accepting or offering bribes, kickbacks, or improper payments |
| **Gross insubordination** | Wilful refusal to follow lawful instructions |
| **Unauthorised disclosure** | Sharing confidential business information |
| **Moonlighting** | Taking external employment without written approval |

#### 6.1.4 Acknowledgement

Every employee must sign the Code of Conduct acknowledgement form during onboarding and annually thereafter.

### 6.2 Conflict-of-Interest Policy

#### 6.2.1 Definition

A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests, relationships, or activities could interfere — or appear to interfere — with their professional duties and obligations to Awdeer Investments Limited.

#### 6.2.2 Examples of Conflicts

- Owning a significant interest in a supplier, customer, or competitor
- Employing or supervising a close family member
- Accepting gifts or hospitality valued above UGX 200,000 from a business contact
- Having a romantic relationship with a direct report or supervisor
- Serving as a director or officer of another company without disclosure
- Using company information for personal financial gain

#### 6.2.3 Disclosure Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Initial disclosure** | At onboarding, every employee completes a Conflict-of-Interest Declaration Form |
| **Annual renewal** | All employees re-declare annually (in January) |
| **Event-driven disclosure** | Employees must disclose any new conflict within 7 days of it arising |
| **Management disclosure** | G3 and above must disclose business interests of immediate family members |

#### 6.2.4 Review and Management

1. Disclosures are reviewed by the HR Manager and, where material, escalated to the Managing Director
2. Identified conflicts are managed through one or more of:
   - Recusal from relevant decisions
   - Reassignment of duties
   - Divestment of the conflicting interest
   - Enhanced oversight
3. Failure to disclose a known conflict is a disciplinary offence

## 7. Health, Safety and Environment (HSE)

### 7.1 HSE Policy Statement

Awdeer Investments Limited is committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors. We comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Act, 2006 and the Workers Compensation Act, Cap 225.

### 7.2 HSE Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------------|
| **Managing Director** | Overall accountability for HSE; allocates resources |
| **HR Manager** | Coordinates HSE programme; maintains records; leads training |
| **Line Managers** | Enforce safety standards in their areas; conduct risk assessments |
| **All Employees** | Follow safety rules; report hazards and incidents; use PPE where required |
| **HSE Committee** | (If >25 employees) Quarterly meetings; safety audits; recommend improvements |

### 7.3 Workplace Safety Standards

1. **Fire safety** — Fire extinguishers on every floor, tested quarterly; evacuation plan posted; fire drills conducted semi-annually
2. **First aid** — At least one trained first aider per location; stocked first aid kit; emergency numbers posted
3. **Ergonomics** — Proper workstation setup; screen breaks every 2 hours for desk-based roles
4. **Electrical safety** — Regular inspection of wiring and equipment; no personal heaters/appliances
5. **Housekeeping** — Clean and uncluttered work areas; wet floor signs; proper waste disposal
6. **Vehicle safety** — Valid driving permits; vehicle pre-trip inspections; no mobile phone use while driving

### 7.4 Incident Reporting

#### 7.4.1 Incident Categories

| Category | Definition | Examples |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **Near miss** | An event that could have caused injury or damage but did not | Slipping without falling; equipment malfunction caught before failure |
| **Minor incident** | Injury requiring first aid only; minor property damage | Cuts, bruises; broken equipment |
| **Major incident** | Injury requiring medical treatment beyond first aid | Fractures, burns; significant property damage |
| **Fatal / critical** | Death or life-threatening injury | — |

#### 7.4.2 Reporting Procedure

1. **Immediate** — Secure the scene; administer first aid; call emergency services if needed
2. **Within 1 hour** — Report to line manager verbally
3. **Within 24 hours** — Complete the Incident Report Form and submit to HR
4. **Within 48 hours** — HR Manager investigates (or appoints investigation team for major/critical)
5. **Within 7 days** — Investigation report completed with root cause analysis and corrective actions
6. **Statutory reporting** — Fatal and major incidents reported to the relevant government authority as required by the Occupational Safety and Health Act

#### 7.4.3 Workers Compensation

Per the Workers Compensation Act, Cap 225:

- Employers must compensate employees for injuries sustained in the course of employment
- Compensation covers medical expenses, temporary incapacity, permanent incapacity, and death
- Claims are processed through the Department of Occupational Safety and Health, Ministry of Gender, Labour and Social Development

## 8. Lean vs Controlled Versions

### 8.1 When to Use Each Version

| Criteria | Lean | Controlled |
|----------|------|-----------|
| **Team size** | Under 15 employees | 15 – 100+ employees |
| **HR staff** | 0–1 dedicated HR person | 2+ HR team members |
| **Approval layers** | 1–2 approvers | 3–4 approvers |
| **Documentation** | Essential records only | Full documentation trail |
| **Audit requirements** | Annual self-assessment | Quarterly internal audit |
| **System** | Spreadsheets + shared drive | HRIS / ERP module |

### 8.2 Lean Version Summary

In the Lean version:

- **Recruitment:** Line manager + MD approval; simplified scoring
- **Onboarding:** Checklist-based; HR officer handles end-to-end
- **Payroll:** HR prepares, Finance Manager reviews, MD approves
- **Leave:** Manager approves directly; HR records
- **Performance:** Annual appraisal with quarterly informal check-ins
- **Disciplinary:** Manager + HR handle; MD approves dismissals
- **Records:** Physical files + shared drive; annual audit

### 8.3 Controlled Version Summary

In the Controlled version:

- **Recruitment:** Formal requisition → HR screening → panel interview → background check → multi-level approval
- **Onboarding:** Structured programme with day 1 / week 1 / month 1 milestones; buddy system
- **Payroll:** HR prepares → HR Manager reviews → Finance Manager verifies → CFO/MD approves; dual bank signatories
- **Leave:** System-based application → Manager approval → HR verification; real-time balance tracking
- **Performance:** Quarterly formal reviews; 360-degree feedback at G4+; calibration sessions
- **Disciplinary:** Formal hearing with committee; union representation (if applicable); appeals process
- **Records:** HRIS with access controls; quarterly compliance audits; automated reminders
