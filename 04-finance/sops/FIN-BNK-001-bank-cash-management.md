## 1. Purpose

To establish procedures for managing the Company's bank accounts, cash holdings, bank reconciliations, petty cash, mobile money, and short-term cash forecasting. This SOP ensures that all cash is safeguarded, accurately recorded, and optimally deployed.

---

## 2. Scope

Covers all bank accounts (UGX and USD), petty cash funds, mobile money floats, and cash equivalents held by Awdeer Investments Limited and all business lines across all branches.

### 5.2 Bank Signatory Rules

- Transactions above **UGX 50,000,000**: CEO must be one of the signatories
- Transactions above **UGX 200,000,000**: Board resolution required
- Signatory list reviewed and updated annually or upon personnel change
- No single person can both initiate and approve a payment

## 6. Bank Reconciliation Process

### 6.1 Step-by-Step Workflow

| Step | Action | Owner | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Download bank statement (daily from internet banking) | Treasury Officer | Daily by 9:00 AM |
| 2 | Import/enter bank transactions into ERP cash book | Treasury Officer | Daily by 10:00 AM |
| 3 | Match bank statement entries to GL entries (auto-match where possible) | Treasury Officer | Daily |
| 4 | Identify unmatched items on both sides | Treasury Officer | Daily |
| 5 | Investigate unmatched items: obtain supporting documents | Treasury Officer | Within 2 BD |
| 6 | Post missing GL entries (bank charges, interest, direct debits) | Treasury Officer | Same day |
| 7 | Prepare bank reconciliation statement (FIN-TPL-008) | Treasury Officer | Monthly by BD4 |
| 8 | Review and sign-off bank reconciliation | Financial Controller | BD4 |
| 9 | File bank reconciliation with supporting statements | Treasury Officer | BD4 |

### 6.2 Reconciling Items Treatment

| Item Type | Action | Timeline |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Outstanding cheques (>90 days) | Investigate; cancel and re-issue if needed | Monthly review |
| Unidentified deposits | Trace to source; post to suspense if unresolved | 5 BD maximum |
| Bank charges not in GL | Post journal entry | Same day |
| Direct debits/standing orders | Confirm authorization; post to GL | Same day |
| Errors (bank or company) | Notify bank / correct GL; document resolution | 5 BD maximum |

### 6.3 Reconciliation Controls

- Bank reconciliation must be prepared by someone who does NOT process payments
- All reconciling items older than 30 days must have a written explanation
- Reconciling items older than 90 days are escalated to CFO
- Suspense account must be cleared monthly; no items >60 days

## 7. Petty Cash Management

### 7.1 Petty Cash Fund Setup

| Location | Float Amount (UGX) | Custodian |
|----------|-------------------|-----------|
| Head Office — Kampala | 2,000,000 | Office Administrator |
| Kampala Branch 2 | 1,000,000 | Branch Administrator |
| Jinja | 1,000,000 | Branch Accountant |
| Mbarara | 1,000,000 | Branch Accountant |
| Gulu | 1,000,000 | Branch Accountant |
| Mbale | 500,000 | Branch Accountant |
| Namanve Depot | 1,000,000 | Depot Administrator |

### 7.2 Petty Cash Rules

1. Maximum single transaction: **UGX 200,000**
2. Items above UGX 200,000 must go through the normal payment process
3. All disbursements require a completed Petty Cash Voucher signed by the requester and approver
4. Original receipts must be attached to every voucher
5. No personal advances from petty cash
6. No IOUs permitted
7. Petty cash is replenished on the **imprest system** (top-up to original float)

### 7.3 Petty Cash Replenishment Process

| Step | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Custodian prepares replenishment request with all vouchers and receipts | Custodian |
| 2 | Cash count performed (remaining cash + vouchers must equal float) | Senior Accountant |
| 3 | Replenishment request approved by Line Manager (up to UGX 1M) or HOD (above) | Approver |
| 4 | Cheque/transfer raised for replenishment amount | AP Officer |
| 5 | Custodian acknowledges receipt of funds | Custodian |
| 6 | All petty cash expenses posted to GL by cost center | Senior Accountant |

### 7.4 Petty Cash Counts

- **Surprise cash counts:** At least quarterly by Internal Audit or Financial Controller
- **Monthly counts:** By the Branch Accountant or Senior Accountant at month-end
- Variance tolerance: **UGX 5,000** (any excess/shortage above this is investigated)
- Count results documented using FIN-TPL-009

## 8. Mobile Money Management

### 8.1 Overview

The Company uses MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money for customer collections and small disbursements.

### 8.2 Controls

| Control | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Float limits | Maximum UGX 5,000,000 per mobile money account |
| Transaction limit | Maximum UGX 500,000 per single transaction |
| Reconciliation | Daily reconciliation of mobile money statement to GL |
| Access | PIN known only to authorized Treasury staff (maximum 2 persons) |
| Disbursements | Only for pre-approved small payments; not for salaries or supplier payments |

### 8.3 Reconciliation Process

1. Download daily mobile money statement from provider portal
2. Match to GL entries in Account 1060 (MTN) or 1061 (Airtel)
3. Investigate and resolve discrepancies same day
4. Transfer excess float to main bank account weekly

## 9. 13-Week Rolling Cash Flow Forecast

### 9.1 Purpose

To provide a forward-looking view of the Company's liquidity position over the next 13 weeks (approximately one quarter), enabling proactive cash management decisions.

### 9.2 Structure

| Week | Opening Balance | Cash Inflows | Cash Outflows | Net Cash Flow | Closing Balance |
|------|----------------|-------------|---------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Wk 1 | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] |
| Wk 2 | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| Wk 13 | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] | [UGX] |

### 9.3 Cash Inflow Categories

| Category | Source Data |
|----------|-----------|
| Customer collections — cash sales | Sales forecast + historical collection rates |
| Customer collections — credit sales | AR aging report + payment patterns |
| Rental income | Lease schedules |
| Loan drawdowns | Facility agreements |
| Asset disposals | Planned disposal schedule |
| Other income | Historical patterns |

### 9.4 Cash Outflow Categories

| Category | Source Data |
|----------|-----------|
| Supplier payments | AP aging report + payment terms |
| Payroll (net salaries) | Payroll schedule |
| PAYE / NSSF / LST | Payroll schedule (15th of month) |
| VAT payments | VAT computation (15th of month) |
| WHT remittances | WHT register (15th of month) |
| Loan repayments | Loan amortization schedules |
| Capital expenditure | CAPEX budget + committed orders |
| Rent payments | Lease schedules |
| Insurance premiums | Payment schedules |
| Dividends | Board resolutions |

### 9.5 Process

| Step | Action | Owner | Frequency |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Collect inputs from AR, AP, Payroll, Tax, Projects | Treasury Officer | Weekly (Monday) |
| 2 | Update 13-week model with actuals from prior week | Treasury Officer | Weekly (Monday) |
| 3 | Roll forward forecast by one week | Treasury Officer | Weekly (Monday) |
| 4 | Identify weeks with projected cash shortfall | Treasury Manager | Weekly |
| 5 | Recommend actions (accelerate collections, delay payments, draw facility) | Treasury Manager | Weekly |
| 6 | Present forecast to CFO | Treasury Manager | Weekly (Tuesday) |
| 7 | CFO decision on cash deployment / borrowing | CFO | Weekly |

### 9.6 Minimum Cash Balance Policy

- Operating accounts must maintain a minimum balance of **UGX 50,000,000** at all times
- If projected balance falls below minimum, Treasury Manager must alert CFO within 24 hours
- Surplus above **UGX 200,000,000** in operating account must be placed on short-term deposit

## 14. Lean Version

For early-stage or low-transaction-volume periods:

- Weekly bank reconciliation (instead of daily matching)
- Single petty cash fund (Head Office only, UGX 1,000,000)
- 4-week cash forecast (instead of 13-week)
- Monthly treasury report to CFO (instead of weekly)
- Mobile money used only for collections; reconciled weekly

---

## 15. Controlled Version

Full process as described above, plus:

- Daily bank reconciliation with same-day resolution
- Automated bank feed integration with ERP
- 13-week rolling forecast updated every Monday
- Weekly treasury committee meeting (CFO, Treasury Manager, Financial Controller)
- Quarterly bank relationship review
- Annual signatory audit by Internal Audit
- Stress testing of cash position (worst-case scenario modelling)
