## 1. Purpose

## 6. Director's Compliance Obligations Under Uganda Companies Act 2012

### 6.1 Overview

Directors of Awdeer Investments Limited are subject to statutory duties and obligations under the Uganda Companies Act, 2012 (the "Act"). Failure to comply can result in personal liability, fines, disqualification, or criminal prosecution.

### 6.2 Statutory Duties of Directors

| Duty | Section | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Duty to act within powers | s.198 | Directors must act in accordance with the company's constitution (Memorandum and Articles of Association) and exercise powers only for the purposes for which they were conferred. |
| Duty to promote the success of the company | s.198 | Directors must act in good faith and in a way most likely to promote the success of the company for the benefit of its members as a whole. |
| Duty to exercise independent judgment | s.198 | Directors must not subordinate their judgment to another person or body unless authorized by the constitution. |
| Duty to exercise reasonable care, skill and diligence | s.198 | Directors must exercise the care, skill, and diligence that would be exercised by a reasonably diligent person with the general knowledge, skill, and experience of the director. |
| Duty to avoid conflicts of interest | s.199 | Directors must avoid situations where they have, or can have, a direct or indirect interest that conflicts with the interests of the company. |
| Duty to declare interest in transactions | s.200 | Directors must declare to the other directors any interest (direct or indirect) in a proposed transaction or arrangement with the company. |
| Duty not to accept benefits from third parties | s.199 | Directors must not accept any benefit from a third party conferred by reason of being a director or doing (or not doing) anything as a director. |

### 6.3 Filing and Reporting Obligations

| Obligation | Section | Requirement | Deadline |
|-----------|---------|-------------|----------|
| Annual return | s.125 | File annual return with URSB | Within 42 days of the anniversary of incorporation |
| Financial statements | s.165 | Prepare financial statements for each financial year | Within 6 months of financial year-end |
| Auditor appointment | s.167 | Appoint auditors at each AGM | At AGM |
| AGM | s.143 | Hold an Annual General Meeting | Within 18 months of incorporation, then within 15 months of last AGM |
| Register of directors | s.202 | Maintain register of directors; notify URSB of changes | Within 14 days of any change |
| Register of members | s.120 | Maintain a register of members (shareholders) | Ongoing |
| Registered office | s.117 | Maintain a registered office in Uganda; notify URSB of any change | Within 14 days of change |
| Change of directors | s.202 | Notify URSB of appointment, resignation, or removal of directors | Within 14 days |
| Special resolutions | s.154 | File special resolutions with URSB | Within 14 days of passing |

### 6.4 Personal Liability Risks

| Risk Area | Description | Potential Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| Trading while insolvent | Continuing to incur debts when the company cannot pay them as they fall due | Personal liability for company debts; criminal prosecution |
| Fraudulent trading | Carrying on business with intent to defraud creditors | Personal liability; imprisonment up to 7 years |
| Breach of fiduciary duty | Acting in personal interest rather than company interest | Personal liability for losses; removal as director |
| Failure to keep proper books | Not maintaining adequate accounting records | Fine; personal liability if company cannot pay debts |
| Tax evasion | Failure to ensure company complies with tax obligations | Personal liability for unpaid taxes; criminal prosecution under Tax Procedures Code Act |
| Non-compliance with employment law | Failure to comply with Employment Act 2006, NSSF Act | Fines; personal liability |
| Environmental non-compliance | Failure to comply with National Environment Act | Fines; imprisonment |

## 7. Director Information Rights

Under the Companies Act 2012 and good governance practice, directors are entitled to:

| Right | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Access to information | Directors may request any company information reasonably required to perform their duties |
| Access to professional advice | Directors may seek independent professional advice at the company's expense (with Board Chair approval) |
| Access to Company Secretary | Directors may consult the Company Secretary on governance, compliance, and procedural matters |
| Access to management | Directors may request meetings or information from any member of management |
| Board meeting minutes | Directors are entitled to receive minutes of all Board and committee meetings |

**Process for information requests:**
1. Director submits request to Company Secretary (email or in writing)
2. Company Secretary assesses the request and routes to appropriate manager
3. Information provided within 5 business days (or explanation of delay)
4. If the request involves confidential third-party information or legal privilege, Company Secretary consults with Legal before releasing
