## 1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE

### 1.1 Purpose

This document establishes the Internal Audit & Continuous Improvement System for Awdeer Investments Limited (AIL). It provides a structured, risk-based framework for evaluating the adequacy and effectiveness of internal controls, governance, and risk management across all operations.

### 1.2 Scope

This system applies to:
- All business units, departments, and locations of Awdeer Investments Limited
- All processes, transactions, and systems within the organization
- All employees, contractors, and third parties acting on behalf of AIL
- All subsidiaries and joint ventures where AIL has management control

### 1.3 Regulatory Context

This system is designed in compliance with:
- The Companies Act, 2012 (Uganda)
- Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) requirements
- International Standards for the Professional Practice of Internal Auditing (IIA Standards)
- Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) Code of Ethics
- Uganda Institute of Professional Accountants (ICPAU) guidelines
- Applicable sector-specific regulations

### 1.4 Objectives

1. Provide independent, objective assurance on the effectiveness of internal controls
2. Identify risks, control weaknesses, and process inefficiencies
3. Recommend corrective actions and monitor their implementation
4. Support management in achieving organizational objectives
5. Drive continuous improvement across all business processes
6. Detect and prevent fraud, waste, and abuse
7. Ensure compliance with laws, regulations, policies, and procedures

## 2. INTERNAL AUDIT CHARTER

### 2.1 Mandate

The Internal Audit function of Awdeer Investments Limited is established by the Board of Directors through the Audit & Risk Committee. Internal Audit provides independent, objective assurance and consulting services designed to add value and improve the organization's operations.

Internal Audit helps the organization accomplish its objectives by bringing a systematic, disciplined approach to evaluate and improve the effectiveness of risk management, control, and governance processes.

### 2.2 Authority

Internal Audit has the authority to:
- Access all records, personnel, and physical properties relevant to an audit engagement
- Allocate audit resources, set audit frequencies, select subjects, determine scopes of work, and apply techniques required to accomplish audit objectives
- Obtain assistance from personnel in departments being audited as well as specialized services from within or outside the organization
- Report directly to the Audit & Risk Committee on matters of significance
- Communicate directly with the Board Chairman if circumstances require

### 2.3 Independence and Objectivity

| Principle | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Organizational Independence | Internal Audit reports functionally to the Audit & Risk Committee and administratively to the Managing Director |
| Individual Objectivity | Auditors must maintain impartial, unbiased attitudes and avoid conflicts of interest |
| Rotation | Lead auditors rotate across audit areas every 2 years |
| No Operational Responsibilities | Internal Audit staff shall not assume operational duties or implement internal controls |
| Impairment Disclosure | Any impairment to independence or objectivity must be disclosed immediately to the Audit & Risk Committee |

### 2.4 Reporting Lines

```
Board of Directors
        |
Audit & Risk Committee (Functional Reporting)
        |
Head of Internal Audit
        |
    +-----------+-----------+
    |           |           |
Senior      Senior      Audit
Auditor     Auditor     Assistant
(Finance)   (Operations)
```

**Functional Reporting (Assurance):**
- Head of Internal Audit reports to the Audit & Risk Committee
- Audit plans, budgets, and results presented quarterly to the Committee
- Annual assessment of internal audit effectiveness by the Committee

**Administrative Reporting (Day-to-Day):**
- Head of Internal Audit reports to the Managing Director for administrative matters (leave, budget administration, office logistics)
- Administrative reporting does not compromise independence

### 2.5 Responsibilities

**Head of Internal Audit:**
- Develop and execute a risk-based annual audit plan
- Manage internal audit staff and resources
- Report significant findings to the Audit & Risk Committee
- Monitor management's implementation of audit recommendations
- Maintain a quality assurance and improvement program
- Coordinate with external auditors to ensure adequate coverage

**Audit & Risk Committee:**
- Approve the annual audit plan and any significant changes
- Review audit reports and ensure management takes appropriate corrective actions
- Evaluate the performance and independence of the Head of Internal Audit
- Approve the internal audit charter and any amendments
- Meet privately with the Head of Internal Audit at least quarterly

### 2.6 Professional Standards

All internal audit activities shall conform to:
- IIA International Professional Practices Framework (IPPF)
- IIA Code of Ethics
- IIA Standards for the Professional Practice of Internal Auditing
- Applicable laws and regulations of Uganda

### 2.7 Quality Assurance and Improvement Program (QAIP)

| Component | Frequency | Performed By |
|---|---|---|
| Ongoing monitoring | Continuous | Head of Internal Audit |
| Periodic self-assessment | Annual | Internal Audit Team |
| External quality assessment | Every 5 years | Independent external assessor |

### 3.2 Risk Scoring Methodology

Each audit area is scored using a composite risk assessment that considers multiple risk dimensions.

#### 3.2.1 Risk Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Financial Impact | 25% | Potential monetary loss or misstatement |
| Likelihood of Occurrence | 20% | Probability that risks will materialize |
| Complexity of Operations | 15% | Process complexity and volume of transactions |
| Regulatory/Compliance Exposure | 15% | Legal and regulatory requirements |
| Prior Audit Findings | 10% | History of issues and unresolved findings |
| Time Since Last Audit | 10% | Duration since area was last reviewed |
| Management Concern | 5% | Issues raised by management or stakeholders |

#### 3.2.2 Scoring Scale

Each dimension is scored from 1 to 5:

| Score | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Critical | Extreme risk; immediate attention required |
| 4 | High | Significant risk; priority attention needed |
| 3 | Medium | Moderate risk; regular monitoring required |
| 2 | Low | Minor risk; periodic review sufficient |
| 1 | Minimal | Negligible risk; included for completeness |

#### 3.2.3 Composite Risk Score Calculation

**Composite Score = (Financial Impact x 0.25) + (Likelihood x 0.20) + (Complexity x 0.15) + (Regulatory x 0.15) + (Prior Findings x 0.10) + (Time Since Audit x 0.10) + (Management Concern x 0.05)**

1. High-risk areas receive the most audit attention

4. The plan is flexible enough to accommodate emerging risks and ad-hoc requests

## 5. AUDIT PROGRAMS FOR KEY AREAS

### 5.1 Overview

Each key audit area has a dedicated audit program that defines the specific procedures, tests, and evidence to be obtained. Detailed audit programs are maintained as separate SOPs in `/11-internal-audit/sops/`.

### 5.2 Cash and Bank

**Objective:** Ensure cash and bank transactions are properly authorized, recorded, safeguarded, and reconciled.

| Area | Key Tests |
|---|---|
| Cash handling | Physical cash counts, surprise counts, segregation of duties |
| Bank reconciliations | Timeliness, reconciling items, outstanding cheques |
| Payments | Authorization limits, supporting documentation, duplicate payments |
| Petty cash | Fund limits, replenishment, custodian responsibilities |
| Bank signatories | Authorized signatories list, mandate compliance |
| Cash security | Safe access, insurance, cash-in-transit procedures |

**Red Flags:**
- Unexplained cash shortages or overages
- Stale reconciling items (>30 days)
- Payments without proper authorization
- Altered or missing supporting documents
- Single signatory on payments above threshold

**Detailed Program:** See `AUD-CSH-001-cash-audit-program.md`

### 5.3 Procurement and Purchasing

**Objective:** Ensure procurement processes are transparent, competitive, authorized, and deliver value for money.

| Area | Key Tests |
|---|---|
| Requisitions | Proper authorization, budget availability |
| Supplier selection | Competitive bidding, evaluation criteria, conflict of interest |
| Purchase orders | Accuracy, terms, authorization levels |
| Goods receipt | Inspection, quality checks, GRN completeness |
| Invoice processing | Three-way matching, pricing accuracy, duplicate invoices |
| Vendor management | Approved vendor list, performance evaluation, due diligence |

**Red Flags:**
- Sole-source procurement without justification
- Split purchases to avoid approval thresholds
- Vendors with connections to employees or management
- Prices significantly above market rates
- Missing competitive quotations

**Detailed Program:** See `AUD-PRO-001-procurement-audit-program.md`

### 5.4 Payroll

**Objective:** Ensure payroll is accurate, complete, properly authorized, and compliant with statutory requirements.

| Area | Key Tests |
|---|---|
| Employee records | Validity, completeness, authorization of changes |
| Payroll computation | Salary accuracy, deductions, overtime, allowances |
| Statutory compliance | NSSF, PAYE, LST computation and timely remittance |
| Payroll disbursement | Bank transfer accuracy, segregation of duties |
| Ghost employees | Headcount verification, biometric/attendance cross-check |
| Payroll changes | New hires, terminations, salary adjustments authorization |

**Red Flags:**
- Employees without proper documentation
- Payroll amounts exceeding approved salary scales
- Late statutory remittances incurring penalties
- Payments to terminated employees
- Excessive overtime without operational justification

**Detailed Program:** See `AUD-PAY-001-payroll-audit-program.md`

### 5.5 Inventory and Stores

**Objective:** Ensure inventory is properly recorded, safeguarded, valued, and that physical quantities match records.

| Area | Key Tests |
|---|---|
| Receipts | GRN accuracy, quantity verification, quality inspection |
| Storage | Physical security, conditions, access controls |
| Issues | Proper authorization, documentation, FIFO compliance |
| Physical counts | Accuracy, frequency, variance investigation |
| Valuation | Costing method consistency, obsolescence provisions |
| Slow-moving items | Identification, reporting, disposal procedures |

**Red Flags:**
- Significant count variances (>2% by value)
- Unauthorized access to stores
- Missing or altered stock records
- Expired or obsolete stock without provisions
- Issues without requisitions

**Detailed Program:** See `AUD-INV-001-inventory-audit-program.md`

### 5.6 Revenue and Receivables

**Objective:** Ensure revenue is completely and accurately recorded, receivables are valid, and collections are timely.

| Area | Key Tests |
|---|---|
| Sales recording | Completeness, accuracy, cutoff, classification |
| Invoicing | Timeliness, pricing accuracy, authorized discounts |
| Collections | Receipt recording, banking timeliness, allocation |
| Credit management | Credit limits, credit approval, aging analysis |
| Bad debts | Provision adequacy, write-off authorization |
| Revenue recognition | Compliance with accounting policies |

**Red Flags:**
- Unreconciled differences between sales and collections
- Receivables aging beyond 90 days without action
- Unauthorized discounts or credit notes
- Revenue recorded without supporting contracts
- Lapping of customer payments

**Detailed Program:** See `AUD-REV-001-revenue-audit-program.md`

### 5.7 Fixed Assets

**Objective:** Ensure fixed assets are properly authorized, recorded, safeguarded, and valued.

| Area | Key Tests |
|---|---|
| Acquisitions | Authorization, competitive procurement, capitalization |
| Asset register | Completeness, accuracy, asset tagging |
| Depreciation | Correct rates, consistent methods, useful life review |
| Physical verification | Existence, condition, location accuracy |
| Disposals | Authorization, competitive process, proceeds recording |
| Maintenance | Maintenance schedules, cost tracking, capital vs. revenue |

**Red Flags:**
- Assets in register but not physically located
- Physical assets without register entries
- Unauthorized disposals or transfers
- Depreciation not calculated or incorrect rates
- Capital expenditure improperly expensed

**Detailed Program:** See `AUD-AST-001-fixed-assets-audit-program.md`

## 6. SPOT-CHECK PLAN AND AD-HOC AUDIT TRIGGERS

### 6.1 Spot-Check Plan

Spot checks are unannounced, short-duration reviews designed to verify compliance with policies and procedures on a real-time basis.

#### 6.1.1 Spot-Check Schedule

| Area | Frequency | Duration | Performed By |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petty cash count | Monthly (random day) | 1-2 hours | Audit Assistant |
| Cash register/till verification | Bi-weekly | 1 hour | Audit Assistant |
| Stores physical count (sample) | Monthly | 2-4 hours | Senior Auditor - Operations |
| Procurement documentation | Monthly (sample of 5 POs) | 2 hours | Senior Auditor - Operations |
| Attendance/time records | Monthly (random day) | 1-2 hours | Audit Assistant |
| Fixed asset spot verification | Quarterly (sample of 10 assets) | 3 hours | Audit Assistant |
| Bank reconciliation review | Monthly | 1-2 hours | Senior Auditor - Finance |
| Revenue collection verification | Bi-weekly | 1-2 hours | Senior Auditor - Finance |

#### 6.1.2 Spot-Check Principles

1. **Unannounced:** No advance notice to the area being checked
2. **Random:** Timing varies to prevent predictability
3. **Documented:** All spot checks documented using standard forms
4. **Escalated:** Significant issues escalated immediately to Head of Internal Audit
5. **Tracked:** Results logged in the spot-check register for trend analysis

### 6.2 Ad-Hoc Audit Triggers

Ad-hoc audits are initiated outside the annual plan when specific circumstances warrant immediate attention.

#### 6.2.1 Trigger Events

| Trigger | Description | Initiated By |
|---|---|---|
| Fraud suspicion | Whistleblower report, unusual transactions, tip-off | Any employee / Head of IA |
| Significant loss | Unexplained loss, theft, damage above UGX 5,000,000 | Management |
| Regulatory action | URA audit notice, regulatory inquiry | Managing Director |
| System failure | Significant IT system failure, data breach | IT Manager |
| Management request | Specific concern from Board, ARC, or MD | Board/ARC/MD |
| Repeated control failures | Same control failing on 3+ consecutive spot checks | Head of Internal Audit |
| Significant transaction | Unusual or one-off transaction above UGX 50,000,000 | Finance Manager |
| Employee misconduct | Allegations of policy violations | HR Manager |

#### 6.2.2 Ad-Hoc Audit Process

1. **Trigger received** — Documented in Audit Issue Log
2. **Preliminary assessment** — Head of IA evaluates severity and scope (within 24 hours)
3. **Decision to investigate** — Head of IA / ARC Chairman approves (within 48 hours)
4. **Investigation plan** — Scope, timeline, resources defined
5. **Fieldwork** — Evidence collection, interviews, analysis
6. **Reporting** — Findings reported to ARC and management
7. **Follow-up** — Corrective actions tracked through CAPA system

## 7. ISSUE TRACKING AND CAPA SYSTEM

### 7.1 Overview

The Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) system ensures that audit findings are tracked from identification through resolution, with accountability and deadlines.

### 7.2 Finding Classification

| Risk Rating | Color | Description | Response Time | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Red | Material control failure; significant financial loss or regulatory breach imminent | Immediate action (within 7 days) | Audit & Risk Committee |
| **High** | Orange | Significant control weakness; potential for material impact | Action within 30 days | Managing Director |
| **Medium** | Yellow | Moderate control weakness; limited impact if exploited | Action within 60 days | Department Head |
| **Low** | Green | Minor issue; opportunity for improvement | Action within 90 days | Process Owner |

### 7.3 CAPA Workflow

```
Finding Identified
       |
       v
Finding Documented (Audit Report)
       |
       v
Management Response (Agree/Disagree + Action Plan)
       |
       v
CAPA Logged in Tracker
       |
       v
Action Owner Assigned + Due Date Set
       |
       v
Implementation (Action Owner)
       |
       v
Follow-Up Verification (Internal Audit)
       |
       v
   +-------+
   |       |
Resolved  Not Resolved
   |       |
   v       v
Closed    Escalated + New Due Date
```

### 7.4 CAPA Tracker Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Finding ID | Unique identifier (e.g., FND-2026-001) |
| Audit Engagement | Source audit engagement |
| Date Identified | Date finding was identified |
| Finding Description | Clear description of the issue |
| Risk Rating | Critical / High / Medium / Low |
| Root Cause | Underlying cause analysis |
| Recommendation | Auditor's recommended action |
| Management Response | Management's agreed action plan |
| Action Owner | Person responsible for implementation |
| Due Date | Target completion date |
| Status | Open / In Progress / Overdue / Closed / Escalated |
| Verification Date | Date audit verified closure |
| Verified By | Auditor who verified closure |
| Comments | Additional notes and updates |

### 7.5 Escalation Matrix

| Condition | Escalated To | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical finding not addressed within 7 days | ARC Chairman | Emergency ARC meeting |
| High finding overdue by >15 days | Managing Director | Written directive to action owner |
| Medium finding overdue by >30 days | Head of Internal Audit | Formal follow-up with department head |
| Any finding overdue by >90 days | Audit & Risk Committee | Included in quarterly ARC report |
| Pattern of repeated findings (3+ same issue) | ARC + MD | Systemic review initiated |

### 7.6 CAPA Reporting

| Report | Frequency | Recipient | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAPA Status Summary | Monthly | Management Team | All open findings, aging, status |
| CAPA Detailed Report | Quarterly | Audit & Risk Committee | Full tracker with trends and analysis |
| Overdue Items Alert | As needed | MD + Department Heads | Overdue findings requiring immediate attention |
| Annual CAPA Analysis | Annual | Board of Directors | Year-end summary, trends, closure rates |

## 8. PROCESS IMPROVEMENT LOG AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT METHODOLOGY

### 8.1 Continuous Improvement Philosophy

AIL adopts a continuous improvement approach to internal audit and operations. Audit findings are not merely about compliance — they drive process improvement and operational excellence.

### 8.2 Continuous Improvement Methodology: Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)

```
       PLAN
    (Identify issue,
     analyze root cause,
     design solution)
         |
    +---------+
    |         |
    v         |
   DO         |
(Implement    |
 solution)    |
    |         |
    v         |
  CHECK       |
(Measure      |
 results,     |
 verify       |
 effectiveness)|
    |         |
    v         |
   ACT -------+
(Standardize if effective,
 revise if not)
```

### 8.3 Process Improvement Log

All improvement opportunities identified during audits, spot checks, or operations are recorded in the Process Improvement Log.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Improvement ID | Unique identifier (e.g., IMP-2026-001) |
| Source | Audit finding / Spot check / Management suggestion / Employee suggestion |
| Process Area | Department or process affected |
| Current State | Description of current process or issue |
| Proposed Improvement | Description of the improvement |
| Expected Benefit | Quantified benefit (cost savings, time reduction, risk reduction) |
| Priority | High / Medium / Low |
| Owner | Person responsible for implementation |
| Target Date | Expected implementation date |
| Status | Proposed / Approved / In Progress / Implemented / Rejected |
| Actual Benefit | Measured benefit after implementation |
| Lessons Learned | Key takeaways for future improvements |

### 8.4 Root Cause Analysis Tools

Internal Audit uses the following tools for root cause analysis:

1. **5 Whys Analysis** — Iterative questioning to drill down to root cause
2. **Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagram** — Categorize causes (People, Process, Policy, Technology, Environment)
3. **Pareto Analysis** — Focus on the vital few causes that drive the most impact (80/20 rule)

### 8.5 Key Improvement Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Audit plan completion rate | >90% | Audits completed / Audits planned |
| Finding closure rate | >85% within due date | Findings closed on time / Total findings |
| Repeat finding rate | <10% | Repeat findings / Total findings |
| Average time to close findings | <45 days | Average days from identification to closure |
| Process improvements implemented | >80% approved | Improvements implemented / Improvements approved |
| Cost savings from improvements | Track and report | Quantified savings from implemented improvements |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | >4.0/5.0 | Post-audit survey score |

### 8.6 Benchmarking

Internal Audit benchmarks its performance against:
- IIA global benchmarking data
- Industry peers (where available)
- Prior year performance
- Targets set by the Audit & Risk Committee

## 10. LEAN VS CONTROLLED VERSIONS

### 10.1 Overview

AIL operates two versions of the Internal Audit System to accommodate different organizational maturity levels and resource constraints:

| Feature | Lean Version | Controlled Version |
|---|---|---|
| **Target** | Startup phase / limited resources | Mature operations / full staffing |
| **Audit Staff** | 1 Internal Auditor (part-time or full-time) | Head of IA + 2 Senior Auditors + 1 Assistant |
| **Annual Audits** | 4-6 key areas per year | All 16 audit universe areas in cycle |
| **Spot Checks** | Monthly (1-2 areas) | Weekly (rotating all areas) |
| **Audit Programs** | Simplified checklists | Full detailed programs with sampling plans |
| **Reporting** | Summary findings report | Full audit report with risk ratings |
| **CAPA Tracking** | Spreadsheet-based tracker | Dedicated CAPA management system |
| **ARC Reporting** | Semi-annual | Quarterly |
| **Quality Assurance** | Annual self-assessment | Annual self-assessment + external review every 5 years |
| **Technology** | Excel/Google Sheets | Audit management software |
| **External Co-sourcing** | For specialized areas only | As needed for supplemental coverage |

### 10.2 Lean Version — Minimum Requirements

1. **One qualified internal auditor** — reporting to MD, with dotted line to ARC
2. **Risk-based audit plan** covering at least:
   - Cash and bank (every 6 months)
   - Procurement (annually)
   - Payroll (annually)
   - Inventory (annually)
3. **Monthly spot checks** — rotating across petty cash, stores, and attendance
4. **Simple CAPA tracker** — Excel spreadsheet with core fields
5. **Quarterly summary report** to MD, semi-annual report to ARC
6. **Standard audit checklists** for each key area

### 10.3 Controlled Version — Full Requirements

1. **Full audit team** as defined in Section 2.4
2. **Comprehensive risk-based annual audit plan** covering all 16 audit universe areas
3. **Detailed audit programs** with statistical sampling, analytical procedures
4. **Weekly spot checks** with trend analysis
5. **CAPA management system** with automated reminders and escalation
6. **Quarterly detailed reporting** to ARC with KPI dashboard
7. **Quality Assurance and Improvement Program (QAIP)** with external assessments
8. **Continuous auditing techniques** for high-risk areas
9. **Data analytics** for transaction monitoring
10. **Formal root cause analysis** for all High and Critical findings

### 10.4 Transition Path: Lean to Controlled

| Phase | Timeline | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 (Foundation) | Months 1-6 | Hire auditor, establish charter, develop initial plan, implement checklists |
| Phase 2 (Build) | Months 7-12 | Complete first audit cycle, establish CAPA tracker, refine processes |
| Phase 3 (Expand) | Year 2 | Add second auditor, expand audit universe coverage, implement detailed programs |
| Phase 4 (Mature) | Year 3+ | Full team, comprehensive coverage, data analytics, QAIP, technology tools |
