### 6. Evaluation Criteria

Quotations will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Price (total cost of ownership) | 40% |
| Quality / technical compliance | 25% |
| Delivery timeline | 15% |

| Supplier track record / references | 10% |

> **Note:** The lowest-priced quotation will not necessarily be selected. Awdeer Investments Limited reserves the right to select the quotation that offers the best overall value for money.

---

### 7. Terms and Conditions

1. **Right to reject:** Awdeer Investments Limited reserves the right to accept or reject any or all quotations without obligation to provide reasons.
2. **Late submissions:** Quotations received after the closing date and time will not be considered.
3. **Incomplete quotations:** Quotations missing mandatory information may be disqualified.
4. **Prices:** All prices must be in UGX and inclusive of all costs (delivery, packaging, etc.) unless otherwise specified.
5. **Tax compliance:** Suppliers must have a valid TIN. Withholding Tax (WHT) will be deducted at source as per URA regulations.
6. **Confidentiality:** This RFQ and all related information are confidential and may not be shared with third parties.
7. **Conflict of interest:** Suppliers must disclose any relationship with Awdeer Investments Limited employees.
8. **No collusion:** By submitting a quotation, you certify that your quotation is genuine, independent, and not the result of any agreement with other bidders.
9. **Validity:** Your quotation must remain valid for a minimum of 60 days from the closing date.
10. **Communication:** All queries regarding this RFQ must be directed to the contact person named above. Do not contact other Awdeer staff regarding this RFQ.
