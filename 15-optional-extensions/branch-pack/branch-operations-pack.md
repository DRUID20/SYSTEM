## 1. Purpose

This Branch Operations Pack provides the complete operating procedures, controls, reporting formats, and checklists required to run any Awdeer Investments Limited branch location. It ensures consistent standards across all branches and business lines, enabling the Head Office to maintain oversight while giving branch managers clear accountability for day-to-day operations.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all branch locations of Awdeer Investments Limited, including:
- Branch offices (Kampala, Jinja, Mbarara, Gulu, Mbale, and any future locations)
- Retail outlets
- Depot and warehouse locations
- Any satellite office operating under the Awdeer Investments Limited name

## 3. Branch Manager Accountability Framework

### 3.1 Branch Manager Role

The Branch Manager is the single point of accountability for all branch operations. They report to the Operations Manager (or relevant Business Line Manager) and are responsible for:

| Area | Specific Accountability |
|------|------------------------|
| Revenue | Achieve branch sales targets as set in the quarterly plan |
| Cash | Ensure all cash is accurately counted, reconciled, and banked daily |
| Stock | Maintain accurate stock records; keep shrinkage below target (max 1% of revenue) |
| Staff | Manage branch staff attendance, performance, and conduct |
| Customers | Ensure customer service standards are met; resolve complaints within 24 hours |
| Compliance | Ensure branch operates within company policies and local regulations |
| Assets | Safeguard all company assets (equipment, vehicles, inventory, cash) |
| Reporting | Submit all daily, weekly, and monthly reports on time |
| Safety | Maintain a safe working environment; report incidents immediately |

### 3.2 Branch Manager Authority Limits

| Decision | Authority | Requires HQ Approval |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| Petty cash disbursement | Up to UGX 200,000 per transaction | Above UGX 200,000 |
| Customer discount | Up to 5% off listed price | Above 5% |
| Staff overtime authorization | Up to 10 hours/week per employee | Above 10 hours/week |
| Emergency repair/maintenance | Up to UGX 500,000 | Above UGX 500,000 |
| Local procurement (consumables) | Up to UGX 300,000 per purchase | Above UGX 300,000 |
| Customer complaint resolution | Refund/credit up to UGX 500,000 | Above UGX 500,000 |
| Staff disciplinary (verbal warning) | Full authority | Written warning and above |
| Branch closure (emergency) | Full authority with immediate notification | Planned closures |

## 4. Branch Cash Controls

### 4.1 Daily Cash-Up Procedure

**Timing:** End of each business day, before any staff leave the branch.

**Personnel:** Branch Manager (or designated Cashier) performs the count; a second staff member witnesses.

**Procedure:**

```
Step 1: Close all point-of-sale terminals and print end-of-day settlement reports
         |
Step 2: Count all physical cash (notes and coins) in the till/register
         |
Step 3: Count all physical cash in the safe (if applicable)
         |
Step 4: Record the count on the Daily Cash-Up Sheet (Template A below)
         |
Step 5: Calculate expected cash:
         Opening cash balance
         + Cash sales received
         + Other cash receipts
         - Cash refunds issued
         - Petty cash disbursements
         - Cash banked during the day
         = Expected closing cash balance
         |
Step 6: Compare physical count to expected balance
         |
Step 7: Record any variance (over or short)
         |
Step 8: Both the counter and witness sign the cash-up sheet
         |
Step 9: If variance exceeds UGX 50,000 — notify Operations Manager immediately
         |
Step 10: File the signed cash-up sheet; scan and email to Finance by 8:00 AM next day
```

### 4.2 Banking Procedures

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Banking frequency | Daily — all cash above the overnight float limit must be banked |
| Overnight float limit | UGX 500,000 (or as approved by CFO for high-volume branches) |
| Banking method | Direct deposit at designated bank branch, or secure cash-in-transit service |
| Bank deposit slip | Complete in duplicate; retain one copy at branch, one goes with deposit |
| Banking cutoff | Cash must be banked before close of business each day; if banking is not possible (bank closed, security concern), record reason and bank first thing next business day |
| Banking confirmation | Obtain bank receipt/confirmation; attach to daily cash-up sheet |
| Segregation | The person who counts cash should not be the same person who banks it (where staffing allows) |

### 4.3 Cash Limits

| Item | Limit | Authority to Change |
|------|-------|-------------------|
| Overnight safe float | UGX 500,000 | CFO |
| Till float (opening balance) | UGX 200,000 | Operations Manager |
| Maximum single cash receipt | UGX 5,000,000 (above this, require bank transfer) | CFO |
| Petty cash fund | UGX 500,000 per branch | Finance Manager |
| Petty cash single transaction limit | UGX 200,000 | Branch Manager |

### 4.4 Safe Controls

| Control | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Safe access | Branch Manager + one designated backup only. No other staff may access the safe. |
| Safe combination/key | Changed every 6 months or immediately upon staff change. Finance Manager holds sealed backup combination. |
| Safe contents log | Every item placed in or removed from the safe must be logged (date, time, amount, reason, person). |
| Safe count | Full safe count at daily cash-up. Surprise safe count by Operations Manager at least monthly. |
| Safe minimum standards | Fire-rated safe, bolted to floor, not visible from public areas, alarmed if feasible. |
| Key custody | Branch Manager carries safe key during hours; key stored in separate locked drawer overnight (not in the safe). |

### 4.5 Petty Cash Controls

| Control | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Imprest system | Petty cash operates on a fixed imprest. Replenish to the set amount when 75% spent. |
| Voucher required | Every disbursement requires a completed petty cash voucher with receipt attached. |
| Approval | Branch Manager approves all petty cash disbursements. |
| Prohibited uses | No staff loans, no personal purchases, no payments that should go through procurement. |
| Reconciliation | Petty cash reconciled at daily cash-up. Physical cash + voucher total must equal imprest amount. |
| Replenishment | Branch Manager submits replenishment request to Finance with all vouchers and receipts scanned. Finance transfers funds or provides cash top-up. |

## 5. Branch Stock Controls

### 5.1 Daily Stock Count Procedure

**Applies to:** Branches holding physical inventory (Retail, Energy/fuel, Stores/warehouses).

**Timing:** End of each business day, after the last transaction.

**Procedure:**

```
Step 1: Print or download the stock report from ERP/POS system (system quantities)
         |
Step 2: Physically count all stock items in the branch
         - High-value items: count every unit daily
         - Medium-value items: count every unit daily
         - Low-value/bulk items: count a sample daily; full count weekly
         |
Step 3: Record counts on the Branch Stock Count Sheet (Template C below)
         |
Step 4: Compare physical count to system quantity
         |
Step 5: Record any variance (surplus or shortage)
         |
Step 6: If variance exceeds 2% of item value or UGX 100,000 — investigate immediately
         |
Step 7: Branch Manager signs the stock count sheet
         |
Step 8: Report significant variances on the Daily Branch Report
         |
Step 9: File stock count sheet; scan and submit to Operations Manager weekly
```

### 5.2 Stock Receiving Procedure

When goods arrive at the branch:

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Verify delivery against Purchase Order (PO) number | Receiving staff |
| 2 | Count all items received against the delivery note | Receiving staff |
| 3 | Inspect for damage, expiry dates, quality issues | Receiving staff |
| 4 | Record any discrepancies on the delivery note (short delivery, damage, wrong items) | Receiving staff |
| 5 | Sign the delivery note only after confirming quantities and quality | Branch Manager |
| 6 | Enter received quantities into ERP/stock system immediately | Branch Manager or designated staff |
| 7 | Store items in designated locations per the branch layout plan | Store staff |
| 8 | File delivery note and attach to stock receiving record | Branch Manager |
| 9 | Report any discrepancies to Procurement and Operations Manager within 24 hours | Branch Manager |

**Key rule:** Never sign a delivery note before counting and inspecting. If a driver pressures for a quick signature, note "received unchecked" and count within 2 hours; report discrepancies immediately.

### 5.3 Stock Issuing Procedure

When stock leaves the branch (sale, transfer, or internal use):

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Verify the request — sales transaction (POS receipt), transfer request form, or internal requisition | Issuing staff |
| 2 | Pick stock from designated storage area | Issuing staff |
| 3 | Verify quantities match the request before handing over | Branch Manager (for transfers and internal use) |
| 4 | Update ERP/stock system immediately upon issue | Issuing staff / POS system (automatic for sales) |
| 5 | For branch-to-branch transfers: complete a Stock Transfer Form (both branches sign) | Branch Managers of both locations |
| 6 | Retain all documentation (receipts, transfer forms, requisitions) | Branch Manager |

### 5.4 Shrinkage Reporting

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Definition | Shrinkage = System stock quantity - Physical stock quantity (where physical is less) |
| Acceptable threshold | < 1% of branch revenue per month |
| Investigation trigger | Any single item variance > UGX 100,000; or cumulative shrinkage > 1% |
| Investigation process | Branch Manager completes a Branch Incident Report (Template D); Operations Manager reviews; Internal Audit investigates if suspected theft |
| Monthly shrinkage report | Branch Manager submits monthly shrinkage summary to Operations Manager by BD 3 of the following month |
| Escalation | Shrinkage > 2% for two consecutive months triggers a branch audit |
| Write-off authority | Shrinkage write-off up to UGX 500,000/month: Operations Manager. Above UGX 500,000: CFO approval required. |

## 6. Daily Branch Reporting

### 6.1 Daily Branch Report Format

The Branch Manager must submit the Daily Branch Report (Template B below) to the Operations Manager and Finance by **8:00 AM the following business day**.

The report covers four areas:

**Section 1 — Sales Summary**

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Total sales (UGX) | Cash sales + card/mobile money sales + credit sales |
| Sales by payment method | Cash, mobile money, bank transfer, credit |
| Number of transactions | Total customer transactions |
| Top selling items/categories | Top 5 by value |
| Sales vs daily target | Actual vs target, variance % |

**Section 2 — Cash Summary**

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Opening cash balance | From previous day's closing |
| Cash received | Cash sales + other receipts |
| Cash paid out | Petty cash + refunds + other disbursements |
| Cash banked | Amount deposited to bank |
| Closing cash balance | Physical count |
| Variance | Expected vs actual (over/short) |

**Section 3 — Stock Summary**

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Stock received today | Items and quantities received |
| Stock variances identified | Items with count discrepancies |
| Low stock alerts | Items below reorder point |
| Damaged/expired stock | Items identified for write-off or return |

**Section 4 — Incidents and Issues**

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Customer complaints | Number and brief description |
| Staff issues | Absences, late arrivals, conduct issues |
| Maintenance/equipment issues | Any equipment failures or facility problems |
| Security incidents | Any theft, break-in, or safety incidents |
| Other | Any issue requiring HQ attention |
