# Month-End Close Process

**Document ID:** FIN-MEC-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-20
**Process Owner:** Financial Controller
**Department:** Finance & Accounting
**Classification:** Internal

---

## 1. Purpose

To define a structured, repeatable month-end close process that produces accurate, complete, and timely financial statements for Awdeer Investments Limited. The close process ensures all transactions for the period are captured, reconciliations are completed, and management reports are delivered within 10 business days (BD10) of month-end.

---

## 2. Scope

Covers all month-end closing activities across all business lines (Energy, Real Estate, Logistics, Retail, Services) and all branches. Applies to the Finance & Accounting team and all departments that provide inputs to the close process.

## 4. Inputs and Outputs

### Inputs
- Bank statements (all accounts)
- Sales invoices and credit notes
- Purchase invoices and goods received notes
- Payroll summary from HR
- Tax computation schedules
- Fixed asset movement schedule
- Intercompany transaction logs
- Branch financial data packages
- Budget data for variance analysis

### Outputs
- Completed bank reconciliations (all accounts)
- Reconciled subledgers (AR, AP, FA)
- Trial balance (adjusted)
- Income Statement (by business line and consolidated)
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Management Accounts Pack (FIN-TPL-011)
- Completed Month-End Checklist (FIN-TPL-010)

## 6. Controls and Approvals

| Control | Description | Owner |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Journal entry approval | All manual journals require dual approval (preparer + approver) | Financial Controller |
| Bank reconciliation approval | Independent review and sign-off | Financial Controller |
| Subledger reconciliation | Must agree to GL control accounts within UGX 100,000 | Senior Accountant |
| Variance analysis | All variances >10% or >UGX 5,000,000 require written explanation | Financial Controller |
| Period lock | GL period locked after BD10; any post-close entries require CFO approval | CFO |
| Segregation of duties | Journal preparer cannot be the journal approver | Financial Controller |

## 9. Lean Version (Startup / Early Stage)

For periods when the Company is in early operations or has limited transactions:

| Day | Simplified Tasks |
|-----|-----------------|
| BD1-2 | Post all invoices, receipts, and payments; confirm cut-off |
| BD3 | Reconcile bank accounts and petty cash |
| BD4 | Post payroll, accruals, and depreciation |
| BD5 | Review trial balance, prepare P&L and Balance Sheet |
| BD5 | CFO review and close |

**Target:** 5-day close for lean operations.

---

## 10. Controlled Version (Full Operations)

The full BD1-BD10 process as described in Section 5 above, with the following additional controls:

- Formal close calendar published by BD-5 (5 days before month-end)
- Pre-close meeting on last business day of the month
- Daily stand-up during close period (15 minutes, led by Financial Controller)
- Post-close retrospective meeting on BD11
- All reconciliations require independent review
- Quarterly deep-dive review by Internal Audit
