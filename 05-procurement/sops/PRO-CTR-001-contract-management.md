## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for creating, reviewing, approving, executing, monitoring, and closing procurement contracts and Service Level Agreements (SLAs) at Awdeer Investments Limited. It ensures that all contractual arrangements protect the company's interests, establish clear performance expectations, comply with Ugandan law, and are managed throughout their lifecycle.

---

## 2. Scope

**In Scope:**
- Supply contracts for goods
- Service agreements (cleaning, security, IT support, consulting, etc.)
- Framework/blanket agreements for recurring purchases
- Construction and works contracts
- Lease agreements for equipment
- SLAs for service-based engagements
- Contract amendments and change orders
- Contract renewals and terminations

**Out of Scope:**
- Employment contracts (governed by HR-EMP-001)
- Customer/sales contracts (governed by SAL-CTR-001)
- Lease agreements for property (governed by LEG-PRP-001)
- Insurance policies (governed by LEG-INS-001)
- Inter-company agreements (governed by FIN-IC-001)

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### 5.1 Contract Initiation — When Is a Contract Required?

| Scenario | Contract Required? | Contract Type | Template |
|----------|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| One-time purchase below UGX 5,000,000 | No — PO terms sufficient | Purchase Order | PRO-TPL-004 |
| One-time purchase UGX 5,000,000–50,000,000 | Recommended | Simple Supply Contract | Standard template |
| One-time purchase above UGX 50,000,000 | Mandatory | Formal Supply Contract | Standard template |
| Recurring supplies (any value) | Yes | Framework Agreement | Framework template |
| Professional/consulting services (any value) | Yes | Consultancy Agreement | Consultancy template |
| Ongoing services (cleaning, security, IT, etc.) | Yes | Service Agreement + SLA | Service + SLA template |
| Construction/works | Yes | Works Contract | Works template |
| Equipment lease/rental | Yes | Lease Agreement | Lease template |

### 5.2 Contract Drafting — Lean Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Determine contract type based on table above | OM | Immediate |
| 2 | Select appropriate contract template | OM | Same day |
| 3 | Draft contract by populating template with: party details (full legal names, TINs, addresses), scope of supply/work/services, specifications and quality requirements, pricing and payment schedule, delivery terms and timeline, warranty/guarantee period, penalty clauses (late delivery, defects), termination provisions, governing law (Uganda), dispute resolution mechanism | OM | 1–2 business days |
| 4 | Finance Manager reviews payment terms, pricing, tax implications (VAT, WHT) | FM | 1 business day |
| 5 | OM incorporates FM feedback | OM | Same day |
| 6 | Send draft to supplier for review | OM | Same day |
| 7 | Negotiate any supplier-requested changes | OM (+ MD for Tier 3+) | 2–5 business days |
| 8 | Finalize contract | OM | 1 business day |
| 9 | MD signs (Tier 3+) or OM signs (Tier 1–2) | MD / OM | 1–2 business days |
| 10 | Supplier counter-signs | Supplier | 1–3 business days |
| 11 | File original signed contract (physical + scan) | OM | Same day |
| 12 | Log in Contract Register: contract number, supplier, value, dates, renewal | OM | Same day |

### 5.3 Contract Drafting — Controlled Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Procurement Manager determines contract type and complexity | PM | 1 business day |
| 2 | Procurement Officer selects template from contract library | PO | Same day |
| 3 | Procurement Officer drafts contract with all commercial terms | PO | 2–3 business days |
| 4 | Legal Officer reviews draft for: legal compliance with Ugandan law, adequate protection clauses, liability and indemnity provisions, IP rights (if applicable), data protection provisions, anti-corruption and anti-bribery clauses, force majeure provisions, termination rights and notice periods, dispute resolution (arbitration in Uganda) | Legal | 3–5 business days |
| 5 | Finance Manager reviews: payment terms and schedule, pricing structure and currency, tax treatment (VAT, WHT obligations), performance bond / bank guarantee requirements, financial penalties and liquidated damages | FM | 2–3 business days |
| 6 | Technical review by requesting Department Head (specifications, SLA metrics) | DH | 2–3 business days |
| 7 | Procurement Manager consolidates all review comments and finalizes draft | PM | 1–2 business days |
| 8 | Send draft to supplier for review and comment | PO | Same day |
| 9 | Negotiate supplier-requested changes: PM leads negotiation, Legal advises on legal/risk changes, FM advises on financial changes, MD involved for Tier 4+ negotiations | PM + Legal + FM | 3–10 business days |
| 10 | Final contract approved by approval authority: Tier 1–2 — Procurement Manager. Tier 3 — MD. Tier 4 — MD + FM. Tier 5 — MD + Board resolution | Per DoA | 1–5 business days |
| 11 | MD executes (signs) contract on behalf of Awdeer | MD | 1–2 business days |
| 12 | Legal Officer witnesses execution | Legal | Same day |
| 13 | Send two originals to supplier for counter-signature | PO | Same day |
| 14 | Receive counter-signed contract; verify signatures | PO | 3–5 business days |
| 15 | Register contract in ERP: contract number, supplier ID, value, start/end dates, key milestones, renewal terms, alert dates | PO | 1 business day |
| 16 | Distribute copies: original to Legal safe, copy to Finance, copy to requesting DH, digital copy in ERP | PO | 1 business day |

### 5.4 SLA Definition and Structure

| SLA Component | Description | Example |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| **Service description** | Clear definition of service to be provided | "Monthly IT infrastructure maintenance including..." |
| **Performance metrics** | Measurable KPIs for service quality | Uptime ≥ 99.5%, Response time ≤ 4 hours |
| **Measurement method** | How metrics will be tracked and reported | Automated monitoring system; monthly reports |
| **Reporting frequency** | How often supplier reports on SLA metrics | Monthly report by BD 5 of following month |
| **Service hours** | When service is expected to be available/provided | 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM EAT, Monday to Friday |
| **Response times** | Maximum time to respond to requests/issues by priority | Critical: 1 hour; High: 4 hours; Normal: 8 hours; Low: 24 hours |
| **Resolution times** | Maximum time to resolve issues by priority | Critical: 4 hours; High: 8 hours; Normal: 2 BD; Low: 5 BD |
| **Escalation matrix** | Who to contact at each escalation level | Level 1: Account Manager; Level 2: Service Manager; Level 3: Director |
| **Penalties** | Consequences for SLA breaches | 2% of monthly fee per SLA metric breach, capped at 20% |
| **Bonuses/incentives** | Rewards for exceeding SLA targets | Optional: 5% bonus for 3 consecutive months above target |
| **Review meetings** | Regular meetings to review SLA performance | Monthly operational reviews; Quarterly strategic reviews |
| **Termination triggers** | SLA breaches that trigger termination rights | 3 consecutive months of SLA breach on critical metrics |

### 5.5 Contract Monitoring and SLA Management

#### Lean Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Frequency |
|------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | Track key contract dates in calendar (milestones, renewals, expiry) | OM | Ongoing |
| 2 | Review supplier deliverables against contract terms at each delivery/milestone | OM | Per delivery |
| 3 | For service contracts: review SLA metrics monthly | OM | Monthly |
| 4 | Record any SLA breaches or contract non-compliance | OM | As they occur |
| 5 | Raise issues directly with supplier; document discussions | OM | As needed |
| 6 | For persistent issues: formal written notice to supplier | OM | As needed |
| 7 | Review overall contract performance with FM quarterly | OM + FM | Quarterly |
| 8 | Flag contracts for renewal 60 days before expiry | OM | Per contract |

#### Controlled Version

| Step | Action | Responsible | Frequency |
|------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | ERP generates contract milestone and deadline alerts | System | Automatic |
| 2 | Procurement Officer tracks deliverables and SLA metrics in ERP | PO | Ongoing |
| 3 | Supplier submits monthly SLA performance reports | Supplier | Monthly |
| 4 | Procurement Officer verifies supplier-reported SLA data | PO | Monthly |
| 5 | Procurement Manager reviews SLA Dashboard | PM | Monthly |
| 6 | For SLA breaches: issue formal SLA Breach Notice to supplier | PM | Within 5 BD of breach |
| 7 | Calculate and apply SLA penalties per contract terms | PM + FM | Monthly/quarterly |
| 8 | Monthly operational review meetings with strategic suppliers | PM + DH | Monthly |
| 9 | Quarterly strategic review meetings with key suppliers | PM + FM + DH | Quarterly |
| 10 | Annual contract performance review and renewal decision | PM + FM + MD | Annually |
| 11 | ERP alerts for renewal 90 days before expiry | System | Automatic |
| 12 | Produce Contract Performance Report for management | PM | Quarterly |

### 5.6 Contract Amendments and Change Orders

| Step | Action | Lean | Controlled |
|------|--------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Change need identified (scope, price, timeline, specifications) | OM / Requester | PM / DH / Requester |
| 2 | Assess impact on contract value, timeline, and terms | OM | PM + FM + Legal |
| 3 | Prepare Change Order / Amendment document | OM | PM (Legal reviews) |
| 4 | Approval at original contract approval level (or higher if value increases tier) | MD (Tier 3+) | Per DoA — may require Board if total exceeds UGX 200M |
| 5 | Negotiate amendment terms with supplier | OM | PM + Legal |
| 6 | Execute amendment (both parties sign) | MD / OM | MD + Supplier |
| 7 | Update contract register and file | OM | PO updates ERP |
| 8 | Notify Finance of any payment/budget impact | OM | System auto-notifies |

**Key rule:** Cumulative amendments must not increase the original contract value by more than 20% without re-tendering (Controlled version) or MD re-approval (Lean version).

### 5.7 Contract Close-Out

| Step | Action | Lean | Controlled |
|------|--------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Confirm all deliverables received and accepted | OM | PM + DH verify |
| 2 | Confirm all invoices received and paid | FM verifies | AP verifies in ERP |
| 3 | Resolve any outstanding claims or disputes | OM | PM + Legal |
| 4 | Release performance bond/bank guarantee (if applicable) | FM arranges | FM arranges; Legal confirms |
| 5 | Conduct final supplier evaluation | OM | PM using PRO-TPL-006 |
| 6 | Issue Contract Close-Out Certificate | OM | PM issues; MD approves |
| 7 | Update contract register (status: closed) | OM | PO closes in ERP |
| 8 | Archive contract file (minimum 7 years retention) | OM files | ERP archive + Legal safe |
| 9 | Capture lessons learned for future procurements | OM notes | PM adds to lessons learned register |

## 6. Controls and Approvals

| Control | Description | Version |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Mandatory legal review | All contracts above UGX 5M reviewed by Legal | Controlled |
| Financial terms review | FM reviews all payment terms and tax implications | Both |
| Approval per DoA | Contract approval matches or exceeds PO approval level | Both |
| Amendment cap | Cumulative amendments capped at 20% of original value | Both |
| Renewal review | No auto-renewal without fresh approval | Both |
| SLA monitoring | Active tracking of all service contract SLAs | Both |
| Penalty enforcement | SLA penalties calculated and applied per contract | Both |
| Performance bond | Required for Tier 4+ contracts (5–10% of value) | Controlled |
| Board resolution | Tier 5 contracts require formal Board resolution | Controlled |
| Dual signature | Contracts above UGX 50M require two authorized signatories | Controlled |
| Contract register | All active contracts logged with key dates and values | Both |
| Expiry alerts | System/calendar alerts well before contract expiry | Both |
