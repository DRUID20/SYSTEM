## Usage Notes

1. **Resolution numbering:** Use sequential numbering per year. BOD = Board of Directors; SHR = Shareholders; WR = Written Resolution; SR = Special Resolution.
2. **Copies:** Original signed resolution filed in the statutory minute book. Digital copy stored in `/02-governance-administration/` under the relevant meeting folder.
3. **Certified copies:** The Company Secretary may issue certified true copies for banks, regulators, or third parties upon request.
4. **URSB filing:** Special resolutions must be filed with URSB within 14 days. Certain ordinary resolutions (e.g., appointment of auditors) may also require filing.
