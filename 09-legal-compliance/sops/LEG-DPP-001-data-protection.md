## 1. Purpose

To establish the standard operating procedure for protecting personal data processed by Awdeer Investments Limited in compliance with the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (DPPA) of Uganda and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations 2021. This SOP operationalises the data protection principles, rights, and obligations set out in the main Legal, Compliance & Risk system document.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- All personal data processed by Awdeer Investments Limited, whether in electronic or physical form
- All departments, business lines, and locations
- All employees, contractors, and third-party processors

**Categories of personal data processed:**

| Category | Data Subjects | Examples |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| Employee data | Staff, former employees, job applicants | Names, NIN, addresses, bank details, salary, health records, performance data |
| Customer data | Individual and corporate customers | Names, contact details, TIN, transaction history, payment information |
| Supplier data | Individual and corporate suppliers | Director names, NIN, TIN, bank details, contact information |
| Visitor data | Office and premises visitors | Names, ID numbers, photographs, vehicle registration |
| CCTV data | Anyone on premises | Video footage, timestamps |

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Process A: PDPO Registration

#### Step A1: Determine Registration Requirement

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Determine whether Awdeer Investments Limited is required to register as a data controller and/or data processor with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Requirement** | Under Section 28 of DPPA 2019, any person who collects, processes, or holds personal data must register with the PDPO |
| **Assessment** | Awdeer processes employee, customer, and supplier personal data — registration is required |

#### Step A2: Complete Registration

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Submit registration application to PDPO |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Requirements** | Company details, description of data processed, purposes of processing, categories of data subjects, security measures in place, data transfers (if any), contact details of data protection officer |
| **Fee** | Per PDPO schedule of fees |
| **Renewal** | Annually before expiry |

---

### Process B: Data Collection and Processing

#### Step B1: Identify Legal Basis

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Before collecting or processing personal data, identify and document the legal basis |
| **Who** | Department Head (identify need); Legal/Compliance Officer (confirm legal basis) |
| **Legal Bases** | Consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation, vital interest, public interest, legitimate interest (see main document Section 8.3) |
| **Documentation** | Record legal basis in data register against each processing activity |

#### Step B2: Provide Privacy Notice

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Inform data subjects about how their data will be processed |
| **Who** | Department collecting the data |
| **Content (Section 10, DPPA 2019)** | Identity of data controller (Awdeer Investments Limited), purpose of collection, categories of data collected, legal basis for processing, recipients/categories of recipients, retention period, data subject rights, contact details for queries, right to complain to PDPO |
| **Format** | Written notice provided at point of collection (employment contract addendum, customer registration form, supplier onboarding form, website privacy policy) |
| **Language** | Clear, plain English |

#### Step B3: Obtain Consent (Where Required)

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Obtain clear, informed, and specific consent where consent is the legal basis |
| **Who** | Department collecting the data |
| **Requirements** | Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous. Must be an affirmative action (pre-ticked boxes not valid). Separate consent for different processing activities. Easy mechanism to withdraw consent |
| **Record** | Maintain evidence of consent (signed form, timestamp, opt-in record) |
| **Sensitive Data** | Explicit consent required for processing sensitive personal data (health, biometric, ethnic origin, religious beliefs, political opinions) |

#### Step B4: Process Data in Accordance with Principles

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Ensure all processing complies with data protection principles |
| **Who** | All staff processing personal data |
| **Purpose Limitation** | Use data only for the stated purpose |
| **Data Minimisation** | Collect only what is necessary |
| **Accuracy** | Keep data accurate and up to date |
| **Storage Limitation** | Do not retain data beyond the retention period (see main document Section 8.6) |
| **Security** | Apply appropriate technical and organisational measures |

---

### Process C: Data Subject Rights Management

#### Step C1: Receive Request

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Receive and log data subject request |
| **Who** | Any staff member (receive); Legal/Compliance Officer (log and manage) |
| **Request Types** | Access, correction, deletion, objection, data portability, restriction of processing |
| **How** | Request can be verbal or written. Route all requests to Legal/Compliance Officer immediately |
| **Timeline** | Acknowledge within 3 business days |

#### Step C2: Verify Identity

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Verify the identity of the person making the request |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **How** | Request identification (NIN, passport, or other ID). For electronic requests, confirm via registered email or phone |
| **Purpose** | Prevent unauthorised disclosure of personal data |

#### Step C3: Assess and Fulfil Request

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Assess the request and fulfil or refuse with reasons |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Access Request** | Provide copy of all personal data held, processing purposes, recipients, retention period, source of data. Format: written or electronic copy |
| **Correction Request** | Correct inaccurate data and notify any recipients |
| **Deletion Request** | Delete data if no legal basis for retention. Document reasons if refused (e.g., legal obligation to retain) |
| **Objection** | Cease processing unless compelling legitimate grounds exist |
| **Timeline** | Fulfil within 30 days of receipt (extendable by further 30 days for complex requests with notification to data subject) |
| **Fee** | Generally free. Reasonable fee may be charged for manifestly excessive or repetitive requests |
| **Refusal** | If request refused, provide reasons and inform of right to complain to PDPO |

#### Step C4: Record and Close

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Record the request, action taken, and outcome in the data subject request register |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Record** | Date received, data subject identity, request type, action taken, date fulfilled, any refusal and reasons |

---

### Process D: Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

#### Step D1: Identify Need for DPIA

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Determine whether a DPIA is required for new or changed processing activities |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Triggers** | New system or technology processing personal data, large-scale processing of sensitive data, systematic monitoring of public areas (CCTV expansion), automated decision-making, cross-border data transfers, new business line involving personal data |

#### Step D2: Conduct DPIA

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Systematically assess the impact of the processing on data subjects' rights |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer with relevant department |
| **Content** | Description of the processing and purposes, assessment of necessity and proportionality, assessment of risks to data subjects, measures to mitigate risks, consultation with data subjects (where appropriate) |
| **Approval** | MD approves DPIA and risk mitigation measures |
| **PDPO Consultation** | If DPIA identifies high residual risk, consult PDPO before proceeding |

---

### Process E: Data Breach Management

#### Step E1: Detect and Report Internally

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Identify a personal data breach and report internally |
| **Who** | Any staff member (detect); report immediately to Legal/Compliance Officer and IT Administrator |
| **Examples** | Unauthorised access to personal data, loss or theft of device containing personal data, accidental disclosure (email to wrong recipient, document left unattended), system hack or cyber attack, ransomware encryption of personal data, physical break-in affecting data storage |
| **Timeline** | Report internally within 4 hours of discovery |

#### Step E2: Contain and Assess

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Contain the breach and assess severity |
| **Who** | IT Administrator (technical containment); Legal/Compliance Officer (assessment) |
| **Containment** | Isolate affected systems, change passwords, revoke access, secure physical evidence |
| **Assessment** | What data was affected? How many data subjects? Is data encrypted? What is the likely harm to data subjects? Can the breach be reversed? |
| **Timeline** | Within 24 hours of discovery |

#### Step E3: Notify PDPO

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Notify the Personal Data Protection Office if the breach is likely to result in risk to data subjects |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Timeline** | Within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach (Section 24, DPPA 2019) |
| **Content** | Nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, likely consequences, measures taken to address the breach, contact details of Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Exemption** | Notification not required if the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects (document reasoning) |

#### Step E4: Notify Affected Data Subjects

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Notify affected data subjects if the breach is likely to result in high risk |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer (prepare); MD (approve) |
| **Timeline** | Without undue delay |
| **Content** | Clear description of the breach, likely consequences, measures taken, advice on self-protection measures, contact details for further information |
| **Method** | Direct communication (email, letter, phone call). Public notice only if direct communication is disproportionate |

#### Step E5: Investigate and Remediate

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Conduct full investigation and implement remediation measures |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer + IT Administrator |
| **Investigation** | Root cause analysis, scope of impact, evidence preservation |
| **Remediation** | Fix vulnerability, strengthen controls, update procedures, disciplinary action if warranted |
| **Report** | Complete breach investigation report within 30 days |
| **Lessons Learned** | Update security measures and procedures based on findings |

---

### Process F: Third-Party Data Processing

#### Step F1: Assess Third-Party Processing

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Before sharing personal data with a third party, assess whether they are a data processor |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Examples** | Payroll service provider, cloud hosting provider, external auditor, IT support company, courier/delivery service |

#### Step F2: Due Diligence on Processor

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Assess the third party's data protection capabilities |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Checks** | Data protection policies and practices, technical security measures, staff training, breach notification capability, data location (any cross-border transfers?), PDPO registration status |

#### Step F3: Execute Data Processing Agreement

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Enter into a written data processing agreement (Section 19, DPPA 2019) |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Required Clauses** | Subject matter and duration, nature and purpose of processing, types of personal data, categories of data subjects, obligations and rights of data controller, security measures required, sub-processing restrictions, breach notification obligations, data return/destruction upon termination, right to audit |

#### Step F4: Monitor Processor Compliance

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Ongoing monitoring of processor compliance |
| **Who** | Legal/Compliance Officer |
| **Methods** | Annual review of processor practices, audit rights exercised (for high-risk processors), breach notification monitoring, certification/compliance evidence requests |

---

### Process G: Data Retention and Disposal

#### Step G1: Apply Retention Schedule

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Retain personal data only for the period specified in the data retention schedule |
| **Who** | All staff (comply); Legal/Compliance Officer (monitor) |
| **Schedule** | See main document Section 8.6 for retention periods |

#### Step G2: Secure Disposal

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Action** | Securely dispose of personal data when retention period expires |
| **Who** | IT Administrator (electronic data); Department staff (physical data) |
| **Electronic Data** | Permanent deletion, drive wiping (DoD standard), degaussing, physical destruction of storage media |
| **Physical Data** | Cross-cut shredding (minimum P-4 security level), secure document destruction service with certificate of destruction |
| **Record** | Maintain disposal log: date, data description, disposal method, person responsible |

## 9. Lean vs Controlled Version

### Lean Version (Small Team < 15 people)

| Aspect | Lean Approach |
|--------|---------------|
| **PDPO Registration** | MD ensures registration via HR & Admin Officer or external advisor |
| **Data Register** | Simple spreadsheet listing what data is collected, for what purpose, and where stored |
| **Privacy Notices** | Standard notice in employment contracts. Brief customer privacy notice on invoices/forms |
| **Consent** | Simple consent checkbox on relevant forms |
| **Data Subject Requests** | MD handles personally. Response within 30 days |
| **DPIA** | Conducted by external advisor for major system changes |
| **Breach Management** | MD leads response with IT support. External advisor consulted if significant |
| **Third-Party Agreements** | Simple data protection clause in supplier contracts |
| **Training** | MD covers data protection basics in induction and annual meeting |
| **Retention** | HR & Admin Officer manages physical files. IT support manages digital deletion |
| **Tools** | Spreadsheet register, physical and digital filing, basic access controls |

### Controlled Version (Mid-size Team 15-100+ people)

| Aspect | Controlled Approach |
|--------|---------------------|
| **PDPO Registration** | Legal/Compliance Officer manages registration and renewal |
| **Data Register** | Comprehensive data processing inventory in ERP/database |
| **Privacy Notices** | Tailored notices for each data collection point. Website privacy policy. Cookie notice |
| **Consent** | Consent management system with timestamps and withdrawal mechanism |
| **Data Subject Requests** | Legal/Compliance Officer manages via formal request log with SLA tracking |
| **DPIA** | Legal/Compliance Officer conducts with department input. Formal DPIA report to MD |
| **Breach Management** | Formal incident response team (Legal, IT, HR). Breach playbook. External counsel on standby |
| **Third-Party Agreements** | Full data processing agreements. Processor risk assessment. Annual compliance reviews |
| **Training** | Structured training programme. Online modules. Role-specific training. Annual refresher with assessment |
| **Retention** | Automated retention alerts. Scheduled disposal runs. Certificate of destruction |
| **Tools** | ERP data management, document management system, encryption tools, access management, consent management |

## 10. Key DPPA 2019 Penalties

| Offence | Penalty |
|---------|---------|
| Collecting or processing data without consent or legal basis | Fine not exceeding UGX 4,800,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 10 years or both |
| Failure to register with PDPO | Fine not exceeding UGX 4,800,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 10 years or both |
| Obtaining access to personal data without authority | Fine not exceeding UGX 4,800,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 10 years or both |
| Disclosure of personal data in contravention of the Act | Fine not exceeding UGX 4,800,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 10 years or both |
| Selling or offering to sell personal data | Fine not exceeding UGX 4,800,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 10 years or both |
| Failure to comply with enforcement notice | Fine or imprisonment per the Act |

> **Note:** The PDPO may also issue enforcement notices, impose administrative fines, and order cessation of processing.
