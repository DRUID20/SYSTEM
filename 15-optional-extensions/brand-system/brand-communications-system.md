# Awdeer Investments Limited — Brand & Communications System

**Document ID:** EXT-BRD-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-20
**Owner:** Managing Director / Marketing Manager
**Classification:** Internal

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the brand and communications standards for Awdeer Investments Limited. It ensures all external and internal communications present a consistent, professional image that reflects the company's values and meets legal requirements under Ugandan law. All employees, contractors, and third parties acting on behalf of the company must follow these standards.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all communications issued by or on behalf of Awdeer Investments Limited, including:

- Email correspondence
- Letters and printed documents
- Proposals, quotations, and contracts
- Presentations
- Social media posts
- Marketing materials (brochures, flyers, advertisements)
- Signage (office, branch, vehicle)
- Website content

```
intended solely for the addressee(s). If you have received this email in error,
```

### 3.2 Email Signature Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Name format | Full legal name as per employment contract (no nicknames) |
| Job title | Official job title as per HR records |
| Phone numbers | Include country code (+256); office line and mobile |
| Logo | Company-approved logo only; supplied by Marketing in PNG format; max 200px wide |
| Font | System default (no custom fonts to avoid rendering issues) |
| Colors | Company primary color for name/title; black for body text |
| Confidentiality notice | Mandatory on all external emails; recommended on internal |
| Social media links | Include only official company social media accounts (if approved) |
| Prohibited | Personal quotes, religious/political statements, animated GIFs, personal social media links, multiple logos |

### 3.3 Setup and Enforcement

- IT Administrator configures the standard signature template in the email system (Google Workspace / Outlook)
- New employees receive their signature as part of IT onboarding (Day 1)
- Marketing reviews compliance quarterly
- Non-compliant signatures must be corrected within 2 business days of notification

| Setting | Specification |
|---------|--------------|

| Bottom margin | 25mm |
| Left margin | 25mm |

| Columns | Single column (default) |

### 5.5 Table Formatting Standards

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Header row | Bold white text on primary color background; or bold primary-color text on light gray background |
| Header alignment | Left-aligned for text columns; right-aligned for numeric columns |
| Body text | 10pt or 11pt Calibri/Arial |
| Row height | Minimum 7mm for readability |
| Alternating rows | Optional light gray (#F5F5F5) fill for tables with 5+ rows |
| Borders | Thin borders (0.5pt), dark gray (#999999); or top/bottom borders only for a cleaner look |
| Currency columns | Right-aligned; use UGX format with thousands separators (e.g., UGX 1,500,000) |
| Percentage columns | Right-aligned; one decimal place (e.g., 12.5%) |
| Total rows | Bold; top border to separate from data rows |

### 5.6 Document Naming Convention

All documents follow the company naming convention from the BOS README:


Examples:

- `SAL-CRM-003_Customer_Proposal_v2.0.pptx`

## 6. Presentation Template Standards

### 6.1 Slide Master Specifications

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Slide size | Widescreen 16:9 (33.87cm x 19.05cm) |
| Font | Calibri or Arial throughout |
| Title slide | Company logo (centered or top-left), presentation title (24-28pt bold, primary color), subtitle/date (16pt, secondary color), presenter name (14pt) |
| Section divider slide | Primary color background, white text, section title centered (28pt bold) |
| Content slide | White background, heading (20pt bold primary color), body text (16-18pt, dark text), logo watermark or small logo in bottom corner |
| Chart/data slide | Title (20pt), chart centered, source/date noted below chart in 10pt |
| Closing slide | Company logo, "Thank You" or contact details, company website/email |

### 6.2 Presentation Content Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Maximum text per slide | 6 bullet points, 8 words per bullet (speak, don't read) |
| Charts and graphs | Use company colors; label axes; include data source |
| Images | High resolution only (minimum 150 DPI); relevant to content; no clip art |
| Animations | Minimal; use simple transitions (fade) only; no flying text or spinning effects |
| Slide count | Target 1 slide per 2 minutes of presentation time |
| Footer | Include slide number, presentation title, and "Confidential" if applicable |
| Template file | Master template maintained by Marketing; available on shared drive |

## 7. Social Media Guidelines

### 7.1 Official Accounts

Only the following official accounts may represent Awdeer Investments Limited on social media:

| Platform | Account Name | Managed By |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| LinkedIn | Awdeer Investments Limited | Marketing / MD |
| Twitter/X | @AwdeerUG (placeholder) | Marketing |
| Facebook | Awdeer Investments Limited (placeholder) | Marketing |
| Instagram | @awdeer_ug (placeholder) | Marketing |

> **Note:** Account names are placeholders. Confirm actual handles before activation.

### 7.2 Who Can Post

| Action | Authorized Persons |
|--------|-------------------|
| Post on official company accounts | Marketing Manager (or designated social media officer) |
| Approve posts | MD or Marketing Manager |
| Respond to comments/messages | Marketing Manager or designated staff |
| Post about the company on personal accounts | Only with prior Marketing approval; must include disclaimer: "Views are my own" |

### 7.3 Content Guidelines

| Category | Guideline |
|----------|-----------|
| **Do post** | Company news, achievements, events, job openings, industry insights, CSR activities, thought leadership |
| **Do not post** | Financial results (unless publicly filed), client names (without consent), internal disputes, political opinions, religious content, competitor criticism, unverified claims |
| **Tone** | Professional, respectful, informative; avoid slang, jargon, or aggressive language |
| **Images/video** | Use only company-owned or properly licensed media; include branding where possible |
| **Hashtags** | Use relevant industry and company hashtags; create a company hashtag (e.g., #AwdeerInvestments) |
| **Response time** | Respond to comments and messages within 24 hours during business days |
| **Crisis posts** | Any post during a crisis or negative publicity must be approved by MD before publishing |

### 7.4 Employee Personal Social Media

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Identification | If you identify yourself as an Awdeer employee, state: "Views are my own and do not represent Awdeer Investments Limited" |
| Confidentiality | Never share confidential company information, client details, financial data, or internal communications |
| Professionalism | Do not post content that could damage the company's reputation or client relationships |
| Intellectual property | Do not share company documents, presentations, or proprietary information |
| Disputes | Do not air workplace grievances on social media; use internal channels |

### 7.5 Social Media Incident Response

If a negative post, complaint, or crisis emerges on social media:

```
Step 1: Staff member who identifies the issue notifies Marketing Manager immediately
         |
Step 2: Marketing Manager assesses severity:
         - Low (routine complaint): Respond per standard template within 4 hours
         - Medium (multiple complaints or media attention): Notify MD; draft response for approval
         - High (viral, legal risk, safety concern): Notify MD and Legal immediately; hold all posting
         |
Step 3: Approved response posted; monitor for 48 hours
         |
Step 4: If escalating, engage external PR support (if retained)
         |
Step 5: Document incident and response in the communications log
```

## 8. External Communication Approval Process

### 8.1 What Requires Approval

All external communications that represent or could be attributed to Awdeer Investments Limited require approval before release.

| Communication Type | Approval Required From |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| Press release | MD |
| Media interview | MD |
| Social media post (official account) | Marketing Manager (routine) or MD (sensitive) |
| Marketing material (brochures, ads, flyers) | Marketing Manager + relevant Business Line Manager |
| Customer proposal or quotation | Business Line Manager (below UGX 50M) or MD (above UGX 50M) |
| External presentation (conferences, events) | MD |
| Website content updates | Marketing Manager |
| Regulatory correspondence | CFO or Legal (as applicable) + MD |
| Response to media inquiry | MD (or designated spokesperson only) |
| Charitable or sponsorship commitment | MD + CFO |
| Investor or shareholder communication | MD + Board Chair |

### 8.2 Approval Process

```
Step 1: Author drafts the communication
         |
Step 2: Author's line manager reviews for accuracy and tone
         |
Step 3: Marketing reviews for brand compliance (formatting, logo use, tone)
         |
Step 4: Relevant approver (per table above) reviews and approves or requests changes
         |
Step 5: If legal or compliance implications, Legal reviews before final approval
         |
Step 6: Approved communication is released/published
         |
Step 7: Copy filed in the communications archive (shared drive / document management system)
```

### 8.3 Spokesperson Policy

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Authorized spokespersons | MD is the primary spokesperson. CFO may speak on financial matters. Business Line Managers may speak on operational matters within their line, with MD approval. |
| All other staff | Must not speak to media, analysts, or external stakeholders on behalf of the company without prior MD approval |
| Inquiries | All unsolicited media or external inquiries must be forwarded to the MD's office immediately |
| Preparation | Before any external engagement (interview, panel, conference), the spokesperson must be briefed by Marketing on key messages and company lines |
