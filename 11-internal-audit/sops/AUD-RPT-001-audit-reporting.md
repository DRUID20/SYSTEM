## 1. PURPOSE

To establish standardized procedures for preparing, reviewing, approving, distributing, and following up on internal audit reports. This SOP ensures that audit findings are communicated clearly, management actions are tracked, and the Audit & Risk Committee receives timely and accurate information.

---

## 2. SCOPE

This SOP applies to:
- All internal audit engagement reports (scheduled, ad-hoc, and special investigations)
- Quarterly and annual summary reports to the Audit & Risk Committee
- Follow-up procedures on prior audit findings
- All members of the Internal Audit team and report recipients

## 5. STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

### CONTROLLED VERSION

#### Phase 1: Report Drafting (Within 5 business days of exit conference)

**Step 1.1: Compile Report Content**

The lead auditor prepares the draft report using the standard template (AUD-TPL-001) with the following sections:

1. **Report Header:** Engagement title, report number, date, classification
2. **Executive Summary:** Overall assessment, scope summary, key themes (1 page max)
3. **Audit Scope and Objectives:** What was audited and why
4. **Methodology:** Approach, sampling, period covered
5. **Overall Opinion:** Overall control effectiveness rating
6. **Detailed Findings:** Each finding in 5 Cs format with risk rating
7. **Summary of Recommendations:** Table of all recommendations
8. **Management Responses:** Action plans, owners, and due dates
9. **Prior Audit Follow-Up:** Status of findings from prior engagements
10. **Appendices:** Supporting schedules, data, and references

**Step 1.2: Overall Opinion Rating**

Assign an overall opinion based on the aggregate findings:

| Opinion | Description | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Effective** | Controls are well-designed and operating effectively | No Critical/High findings; <=2 Medium findings |
| **Generally Effective** | Controls are adequate with some improvement needed | No Critical findings; <=1 High finding; <=3 Medium findings |
| **Needs Improvement** | Significant control weaknesses identified | No Critical findings; >=2 High findings or >=4 Medium findings |
| **Ineffective** | Material control failures; significant risk exposure | >=1 Critical finding or >=3 High findings |

**Step 1.3: Format and Quality Check**

- Ensure consistent formatting per template
- Verify all finding numbers match between sections
- Confirm risk ratings are consistently applied
- Check for factual accuracy and clarity of language
- Ensure management responses are complete

#### Phase 2: Report Review (Within 3 business days of draft completion)

**Step 2.1: Lead Auditor Self-Review**

- Read the complete report for flow and consistency
- Verify all findings are supported by working paper evidence
- Ensure recommendations are practical and proportionate
- Check that management responses address the root cause

**Step 2.2: Head of IA Review**

The Head of IA reviews the draft for:
- Accuracy and completeness of findings
- Appropriateness of risk ratings
- Quality and clarity of writing
- Alignment with audit objectives and scope
- Adequacy of management responses
- Overall opinion consistency with findings

**Step 2.3: Address Review Comments**

Lead auditor revises the report based on review feedback. All review comments are documented and resolved before finalization.

#### Phase 3: Report Finalization and Distribution (Within 3 business days of review)

**Step 3.1: Obtain Final Management Responses**

If management responses were not fully obtained during exit conference:
- Send draft findings to auditee department head
- Allow 5 business days for formal written response
- If no response received, issue report noting "Management response not received within the prescribed timeframe"

**Step 3.2: Finalize and Sign Report**

- Head of IA signs the final report
- Report is assigned a unique report number (format: AIL-AUD-RPT-YYYY-NNN)
- Report is dated and classified (Confidential)

**Step 3.3: Distribute Report**

| Recipient | Copy Type | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Audit & Risk Committee | Full report | Within 5 business days of signing |
| Managing Director | Full report | Within 5 business days of signing |
| Auditee Department Head | Full report | Within 5 business days of signing |
| Other Department Heads (affected) | Relevant findings only | Within 5 business days of signing |
| External Auditors | Full report (upon request) | As requested |

**Total reporting timeline target: 15 business days from exit conference to distribution.**

#### Phase 4: CAPA Logging (Same day as report distribution)

**Step 4.1: Enter Findings in CAPA Tracker**

For each finding in the report:
- Assign a unique Finding ID (format: FND-YYYY-NNN)
- Enter all CAPA fields: finding description, risk rating, root cause, recommendation, management response, action owner, due date
- Set initial status to "Open"

**Step 4.2: Notify Action Owners**

Send formal notification to each action owner confirming:
- Their assigned finding(s)
- Agreed corrective action(s)
- Due date(s)
- Expectation for progress updates

#### Phase 5: Follow-Up (Ongoing)

**Step 5.1: Follow-Up Schedule**

| Risk Rating | First Follow-Up | Subsequent Follow-Ups |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | 7 days after due date | Weekly until closed |
| High | 15 days after due date | Bi-weekly until closed |
| Medium | 30 days after due date | Monthly until closed |
| Low | 45 days after due date | Quarterly until closed |

**Step 5.2: Follow-Up Procedures**

For each open finding at the follow-up date:
1. Contact the action owner to request evidence of implementation
2. Review the evidence provided:
   - Documentary evidence (revised policy, new form, training record)
   - System evidence (configuration change, new report)
   - Physical evidence (observation of new process)
3. Assess whether the corrective action adequately addresses the finding
4. Determine status:
   - **Closed** — Action fully implemented and effective
   - **In Progress** — Action partially implemented; on track for revised date
   - **Overdue** — Due date passed without adequate implementation
   - **Escalated** — Repeated delays or inadequate action; escalated per matrix

**Step 5.3: Document Follow-Up Results**

Update the CAPA tracker with:
- Follow-up date
- Evidence reviewed
- Assessment of implementation
- Updated status
- Verified by (auditor name)

**Step 5.4: Escalation**

Follow the escalation matrix defined in the Internal Audit System (Section 7.5):
- Critical overdue > 7 days: Escalate to ARC Chairman
- High overdue > 15 days: Escalate to MD
- Medium overdue > 30 days: Formal follow-up with department head
- Any overdue > 90 days: Include in ARC quarterly report

#### Phase 6: Periodic Summary Reporting

**Step 6.1: Quarterly Report to ARC**

Prepare a quarterly summary report including:
1. Audit engagements completed during the quarter
2. Summary of key findings by risk rating
3. CAPA tracker status (open, in progress, closed, overdue)
4. Aging analysis of open findings
5. Trend analysis (repeat findings, common themes)
6. Audit plan progress vs. schedule
7. Resource utilization
8. Upcoming quarter's planned activities

**Step 6.2: Annual Report**

Prepare an annual summary including:
1. Full year audit activity vs. plan
2. Summary of all findings by category and risk rating
3. Finding closure rates and average closure time
4. Year-over-year trend analysis
5. Key themes and systemic issues
6. Process improvement recommendations
7. Internal audit performance metrics
8. Quality assurance assessment results
9. Next year's audit plan preview
