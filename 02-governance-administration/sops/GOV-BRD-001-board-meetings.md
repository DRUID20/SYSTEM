## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the end-to-end process for planning, convening, conducting, and documenting Board of Directors meetings for Awdeer Investments Limited, ensuring compliance with the Uganda Companies Act 2012 and the company's Articles of Association.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- Ordinary Board meetings (scheduled quarterly)
- Special / extraordinary Board meetings
- Annual General Meetings (AGMs)
- Extraordinary General Meetings (EGMs)
- Board Committee meetings (Audit & Risk, Finance & Investment, Nominations & Remuneration)
- Written / circular resolutions

This SOP does **not** cover:
- Informal management meetings
- Departmental team meetings

---

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

### 3.1 Key Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Chairperson** | Chair the meeting; approve the agenda; ensure orderly conduct; sign minutes |
| **Managing Director (MD)** | Prepare MD report; present strategic matters; contribute to agenda |
| **CFO** | Prepare financial reports; present budget vs actuals; present risk report |
| **Company Secretary (CoSec)** | End-to-end meeting coordination; notice, pack, minutes, filings |
| **Head of Investment** | Prepare investment performance report; present deal pipeline |
| **Board Members** | Attend meetings; review pack in advance; deliberate and vote |
| **Invitees / Presenters** | Present specific topics as required; no voting rights |

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Phase 1: Planning (30-14 days before meeting)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Confirm meeting date from annual calendar (or schedule special meeting) | CoSec | 30 days before |
| 1.2 | Request agenda items from Chairperson, MD, and all directors | CoSec | 21 days before |
| 1.3 | Prepare draft agenda based on inputs, standing items, and action tracker | CoSec | 18 days before |
| 1.4 | Submit draft agenda to Chairperson for approval | CoSec | 16 days before |
| 1.5 | Chairperson approves or amends agenda | Chairperson | 15 days before |
| 1.6 | Issue formal notice of meeting to all directors with approved agenda | CoSec | 14 days before (minimum) |

> **Notice requirements (Companies Act 2012):**
> - Board meetings: 14 days written notice (unless Articles specify otherwise or all directors waive notice)
> - AGM: 21 days notice
> - EGM: 14 days notice

### Phase 2: Board Pack Preparation (14-7 days before meeting)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Request report inputs from MD, CFO, HODs | CoSec | 14 days before |
| 2.2 | MD prepares MD / strategy report | MD | 10 days before |
| 2.3 | CFO prepares financial report and risk update | CFO | 10 days before |
| 2.4 | Head of Investment prepares investment report | Head of Investment | 10 days before |
| 2.5 | CoSec prepares compliance and action tracker reports | CoSec | 10 days before |
| 2.6 | CoSec compiles all reports into board pack format | CoSec | 8 days before |
| 2.7 | CoSec prepares draft resolutions for items requiring Board decision | CoSec | 8 days before |
| 2.8 | Final quality check on board pack (completeness, page numbering) | CoSec | 7 days before |
| 2.9 | Distribute board pack to all directors (secure email / portal) | CoSec | 7 business days before |

### Phase 3: Meeting Day

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Confirm logistics (venue / virtual link, refreshments, equipment) | CoSec |
| 3.2 | Prepare attendance register | CoSec |
| 3.3 | Directors sign attendance register | All directors |
| 3.4 | CoSec confirms quorum is present | CoSec |
| 3.5 | Chairperson opens meeting, confirms quorum, asks for conflicts of interest | Chairperson |
| 3.6 | Record any declared conflicts of interest | CoSec |
| 3.7 | Chairperson takes meeting through agenda item by item | Chairperson |
| 3.8 | CoSec records key discussion points, decisions, and votes | CoSec |
| 3.9 | For each resolution: Chairperson states the resolution, calls for vote, CoSec records result | Chairperson / CoSec |
| 3.10 | Action items summarised at end of meeting with owners and deadlines | Chairperson / CoSec |
| 3.11 | Chairperson confirms date of next meeting | Chairperson |
| 3.12 | Chairperson closes meeting | Chairperson |

### Phase 4: Post-Meeting (1-14 days after meeting)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | CoSec drafts minutes of meeting | CoSec | Within 5 business days |
| 4.2 | CoSec circulates draft minutes to all directors for review | CoSec | Within 7 business days |
| 4.3 | Directors provide comments or corrections | Directors | Within 5 business days of receipt |
| 4.4 | CoSec finalises minutes incorporating feedback | CoSec | Within 2 business days of receiving comments |
| 4.5 | Chairperson signs final minutes | Chairperson | At next meeting (or electronically) |
| 4.6 | Signed minutes filed in statutory minute book | CoSec | Same day as signing |
| 4.7 | Digital copy stored in document management system | CoSec | Same day |
| 4.8 | Action tracker updated and distributed to action owners | CoSec | Within 2 business days |
| 4.9 | Certified copies of resolutions prepared (if needed for banks, URSB, etc.) | CoSec | As required |
| 4.10 | Any statutory filings triggered by resolutions submitted | CoSec | Per statutory deadlines |

### Phase 5: Written / Circular Resolutions (Between Meetings)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | MD or director identifies urgent matter requiring Board approval | MD / Director | As needed |
| 5.2 | CoSec drafts written resolution with supporting background paper | CoSec | Within 2 business days |
| 5.3 | Chairperson approves draft resolution for circulation | Chairperson | Within 1 business day |
| 5.4 | Resolution circulated to all directors for signature | CoSec | Same day |
| 5.5 | Directors sign and return (physical or electronic signature) | Directors | Within 5 business days |
| 5.6 | CoSec confirms all signatures received; resolution is effective | CoSec | Upon receipt of last signature |
| 5.7 | Signed resolution filed in minute book and digital system | CoSec | Same day |
| 5.8 | Resolution noted in next Board meeting minutes | CoSec | Next meeting |

## 6. Controls & Approvals

| Control | Description | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Quorum check** | Meeting cannot proceed unless quorum (majority of directors) is confirmed | CoSec |
| **Notice period** | Meeting invalid if proper notice not given (unless waived in writing by all directors) | CoSec |
| **Board pack deadline** | Pack must be circulated 7 business days before meeting; late papers only with Chairperson approval | CoSec |
| **Conflict of interest** | Conflicts must be declared at start of meeting; conflicted director may not vote on that matter | Chairperson |
| **Voting records** | All resolutions recorded with vote count; dissenting votes noted | CoSec |
| **Minutes accuracy** | Minutes reviewed by all directors before finalisation | CoSec |
| **Minutes signing** | Only signed minutes constitute the official record | Chairperson |
| **Filing** | Statutory minute book maintained as required by Companies Act | CoSec |
| **Resolution validity** | Written resolutions require signature of all directors to be valid | CoSec |
| **Related party disclosure** | Any related party transaction must be disclosed and minuted; director must abstain from voting | Chairperson / CoSec |
