## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines the end-to-end recruitment process from staff requisition to offer acceptance, ensuring consistent, fair, and legally compliant hiring across Awdeer Investments Limited.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all recruitment activities for permanent, contract, temporary, and intern positions across all departments and grade levels (G1–G5).

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Phase 1: Requisition and Approval

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 1.1 | Hiring manager identifies vacancy and completes Staff Requisition Form | Hiring Manager | Day 1 | Must justify need (new role, replacement, or expansion) |
| 1.2 | Verify position exists in approved budget and org chart | HR Officer | Day 1–2 | If not budgeted, requires MD approval |
| 1.3 | Review and approve requisition | HR Manager (G1–G2); MD (G3+) | Day 2–3 | Lean: HR Manager + MD verbal; Controlled: formal sign-off chain |
| 1.4 | Update or create job description | HR Officer + Hiring Manager | Day 3–4 | Use HR-TPL-001; ensure alignment with grade framework |

### Phase 2: Sourcing

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 2.1 | Post vacancy internally (notice board, email, intranet) | HR Officer | Day 5 | Minimum 5 working days internal posting |
| 2.2 | Review internal applications | HR Officer + Hiring Manager | Day 10 | Assess against minimum requirements |
| 2.3 | If no suitable internal candidates, post externally | HR Officer | Day 11 | Use approved channels: job boards, website, social media |
| 2.4 | For G4+/specialist roles, engage recruitment agency (if approved) | HR Manager | Day 11 | Agency terms pre-approved by Finance |
| 2.5 | Receive and log all applications | HR Officer | Day 11–25 | Track in recruitment tracker |
| 2.6 | Close application period | HR Officer | Day 25 | Minimum 10 working days for external posting |

### Phase 3: Screening and Shortlisting

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 3.1 | Screen CVs against job requirements (education, experience, skills) | HR Officer | Day 26–28 | Use screening checklist |
| 3.2 | Create longlist of qualified candidates | HR Officer | Day 28 | — |
| 3.3 | Hiring Manager reviews longlist and selects shortlist | Hiring Manager + HR | Day 29–30 | Shortlist = 3–6 candidates per position |
| 3.4 | Controlled: Conduct written test or skills assessment | HR Officer | Day 31–33 | For technical, finance, or IT roles |
| 3.5 | Notify shortlisted candidates and schedule interviews | HR Officer | Day 33–35 | Provide interview details, logistics |
| 3.6 | Send regret notifications to unsuccessful candidates | HR Officer | Day 33–35 | Professional email/letter |

### Phase 4: Interview and Assessment

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 4.1 | Prepare interview questions (structured, role-based) | HR Officer + Hiring Manager | Day 35–36 | Standardised questions for all candidates |
| 4.2 | Conduct first-round interviews | Interview Panel | Day 37–40 | Lean: Hiring Manager + HR Officer; Controlled: Panel (3 members) |
| 4.3 | Complete interview scorecards | Each panellist | Same day | Use HR-TPL-002; score independently before discussion |
| 4.4 | Panel discussion and consensus ranking | Panel | Same day | Document consensus scores and rationale |
| 4.5 | Conduct second-round interview (if applicable) | Department Head / MD | Day 41–43 | For G3+ or when panel cannot reach consensus |
| 4.6 | Select preferred candidate and backup candidate | Hiring Manager + HR Manager | Day 43 | Document reasons for selection |

### Phase 5: Pre-Employment Checks

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 5.1 | Conduct reference checks (minimum 2; 3 for Controlled) | HR Officer | Day 44–48 | Must include most recent employer |
| 5.2 | Verify academic certificates | HR Officer | Day 44–48 | Contact institutions or use NCHE verification |
| 5.3 | Conduct background check (G3+ or finance roles) | HR Officer / agency | Day 44–48 | Police clearance; credit check for finance roles |
| 5.4 | Arrange pre-employment medical (if applicable) | HR Officer | Day 44–48 | Required for field/physical roles |
| 5.5 | Review all check results and confirm suitability | HR Manager | Day 49 | Any red flags = escalate to Hiring Manager + MD |

### Phase 6: Offer and Acceptance

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 6.1 | Prepare offer letter | HR Officer | Day 49 | Use HR-TPL-003; include salary, grade, benefits, start date |
| 6.2 | Approve offer | HR Manager (G1–G2); MD (G3+) | Day 50 | Controlled: follows approval matrix |
| 6.3 | Present offer to candidate (verbal, then written) | HR Manager | Day 50–51 | Allow 3 working days for acceptance |
| 6.4 | Negotiate terms (if requested) | HR Manager | Day 51–53 | Within approved salary band; MD approval if above midpoint |
| 6.5 | Receive signed offer letter | HR Officer | Day 53–54 | If declined, extend to backup candidate |
| 6.6 | Prepare employment contract | HR Officer | Day 54–55 | Contract signed on or before first day |
| 6.7 | Notify unsuccessful shortlisted candidates | HR Officer | Day 55 | After offer acceptance confirmed |
| 6.8 | Initiate onboarding process (HR-ONB-001) | HR Officer | Day 55 | Trigger pre-boarding tasks |

## 6. Controls and Approvals

| Control | Description | Lean | Controlled |
|---------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Budget verification | Every requisition checked against approved headcount budget | Yes | Yes |
| Approval chain | Requisition and offer approved per authority matrix | 2 levels | 3–4 levels |
| Structured interviews | Standardised questions and scoring | Yes (basic) | Yes (full scorecards) |
| Independent scoring | Panellists score before group discussion | — | Yes |
| Reference checks | Minimum references verified before offer | 2 | 3 |
| Background checks | Criminal and/or credit checks | G4+ only | G3+ and finance |
| Anti-discrimination | Interview questions reviewed for compliance | HR check | HR + Legal check |
| Conflict of interest | Panel members must declare any relationship with candidates | Verbal | Written declaration |
| Offer approval | Salary offer within approved band | HR Manager | MD for above midpoint |
| Documentation | Complete recruitment file maintained | Essential docs | Full documentation trail |

## 8. KPIs

| KPI | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|-----|--------|----------------------|
| Time to hire (requisition to acceptance) | ≤ 45 working days | Per vacancy |
| Time to fill (requisition to start date) | ≤ 60 working days | Per vacancy |
| Offer acceptance rate | ≥ 85% | Quarterly |
| Cost per hire | ≤ UGX 2,000,000 (G1–G2); ≤ UGX 5,000,000 (G3+) | Quarterly |
| Quality of hire (performance rating at 12 months) | ≥ 3.0 average | Annually |
| Internal fill rate | ≥ 30% of vacancies | Annually |
| Candidate satisfaction score | ≥ 80% | Per vacancy (survey) |
| Diversity of shortlists | ≥ 1 candidate of each gender on shortlist | Per vacancy |
| 90-day retention rate (new hires) | ≥ 90% | Quarterly |

## 9. Lean vs Controlled Version Summary

| Aspect | Lean | Controlled |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Requisition approval | HR Manager + MD (verbal for G1–G2) | Formal sign-off chain per matrix |
| Internal posting | Email/notice board, 5 days | Intranet + email + board, 5 days |
| External posting | 1–2 channels | 3+ channels + agency for G4+ |
| Screening | HR Officer screens; Hiring Manager confirms | HR screens → HR Manager shortlists |
| Assessment | Interview only | Written test + panel interview |
| Interview panel | 2 members (Hiring Manager + HR) | 3 members (HR + Hiring Manager + cross-functional) |
| Scoring | Basic rating | Full scorecard with independent scoring |
| References | 2 references | 3 references + background check |
| Offer approval | HR Manager (G1–G2); MD (G3+) | HR Manager → Dept Head → MD |
| Documentation | Essential records filed | Full recruitment file with audit trail |
| Tracking | Spreadsheet | HRIS/ATS module |
