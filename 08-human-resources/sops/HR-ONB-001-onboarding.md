## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the step-by-step process for onboarding new employees into Awdeer Investments Limited, ensuring a consistent, welcoming, and compliance-driven integration experience from pre-boarding through the end of the probation period.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all new employees (permanent, contract, temporary), internal transfers, promotions to new roles, and re-hires. Interns follow a simplified version.

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Phase 1: Pre-Boarding (Before Day 1)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 1.1 | Send welcome email with Day 1 details (time, location, what to bring, dress code) | HR Officer | 5 days before start | Include parking/transport info |
| 1.2 | Request pre-employment documents: national ID, TIN certificate, NSSF number, academic certificates, passport photos (2), bank details, medical report (if applicable) | HR Officer | With welcome email | Checklist attached |
| 1.3 | Prepare employment contract for signing | HR Officer | 3 days before start | Legal review for G3+ |
| 1.4 | Set up workstation (desk, chair, stationery) | Admin/Office | 2 days before start | Confirm with Line Manager |
| 1.5 | Create email account, system access, phone extension | IT Officer | 2 days before start | Per IT access request form |
| 1.6 | Prepare onboarding pack: company handbook, org chart, policies summary, stationery, ID badge | HR Officer | 2 days before start | Branded folder |
| 1.7 | Assign buddy/mentor (Controlled version) | HR Manager + Line Manager | 3 days before start | Same department, experienced employee |
| 1.8 | Notify team and reception of new joiner | Line Manager | 3 days before start | Email to team |
| 1.9 | Add employee to payroll system | HR Officer | Before Day 1 | Ensure first salary is processed |
| 1.10 | Register employee with NSSF (if new registration needed) | HR Officer | Before Day 1 or within first week | Use NSSF employer portal |

### Phase 2: Day 1 — Orientation

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 2.1 | Welcome at reception; introduce to HR | HR Officer / Receptionist | 8:00 AM | Warm, professional welcome |
| 2.2 | HR orientation session | HR Officer | 8:30 – 10:00 AM | Cover: company history, mission, values, org structure, policies overview, code of conduct, benefits, key contacts |
| 2.3 | Sign employment contract | HR Officer + Employee | During HR orientation | Both parties sign; employee receives copy |
| 2.4 | Sign code of conduct acknowledgement | Employee | During HR orientation | Filed in personnel folder |
| 2.5 | Sign NDA / confidentiality agreement | Employee | During HR orientation | Filed in personnel folder |
| 2.6 | Sign conflict of interest declaration | Employee | During HR orientation | Filed in personnel folder |
| 2.7 | Collect pre-employment documents (if not already received) | HR Officer | During HR orientation | Check completeness per checklist |
| 2.8 | Issue ID badge and access card | Admin/Security | 10:00 AM | Photo taken if not pre-arranged |
| 2.9 | IT setup and systems training | IT Officer | 10:00 – 11:00 AM | Email, ERP, file sharing, printing |
| 2.10 | Office tour: departments, facilities, safety exits, first aid, kitchen, toilets | HR Officer / Admin | 11:00 – 11:30 AM | Include emergency assembly point |
| 2.11 | Introduction to line manager and team | HR Officer → Line Manager | 11:30 AM | Formal handover to department |
| 2.12 | Workstation setup and equipment sign-off | Line Manager + IT | 11:30 AM – 12:00 PM | Laptop, phone, stationery |
| 2.13 | Lunch with team / buddy | Line Manager / Buddy | 12:00 – 1:00 PM | Social integration |
| 2.14 | Review of job description and 90-day expectations | Line Manager | 2:00 – 3:00 PM | Set initial priorities |
| 2.15 | Review first week plan | Line Manager | 3:00 – 3:30 PM | Written schedule for the week |
| 2.16 | End-of-day check-in | HR Officer | 4:30 PM | Any questions or concerns |

### Phase 3: First Week (Days 2–5)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 3.1 | Complete remaining document submissions | HR Officer + Employee | Days 2–3 | Bank details, medical report |
| 3.2 | Attend department-specific induction sessions | Line Manager | Days 2–5 | Processes, tools, workflows |
| 3.3 | Meet key internal stakeholders and cross-functional contacts | Line Manager arranges | Days 2–5 | Schedule 15-minute introductions |
| 3.4 | Begin role-specific training | Line Manager / Trainer | Days 2–5 | As per training plan |
| 3.5 | Read and acknowledge all key policies | Employee | Days 2–5 | Signed acknowledgements to HR |
| 3.6 | Controlled: Daily check-in with buddy | Buddy | Days 2–5 | Informal support |
| 3.7 | End-of-week check-in with line manager | Line Manager | Day 5 | 30-minute meeting; any issues |

### Phase 4: First Month (Days 6–30)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 4.1 | Complete all compliance training (anti-harassment, HSE, data protection) | HR Officer coordinates | Weeks 2–4 | Track completion |
| 4.2 | Establish 90-day performance goals (3–5 goals) | Line Manager + Employee | Week 2 | Documented and signed |
| 4.3 | Begin contributing to core job responsibilities | Employee | Weeks 2–4 | Supervised initially |
| 4.4 | Informal check-in with line manager | Line Manager | End of Week 2 | Progress, challenges |
| 4.5 | Controlled: Weekly buddy meetings | Buddy | Weeks 2–4 | Social and operational support |
| 4.6 | 30-day review with HR and line manager | HR Officer + Line Manager | Day 30 | Structured review meeting |
| 4.7 | New hire satisfaction survey | HR Officer | Day 30 | Anonymous or confidential |
| 4.8 | Update onboarding checklist — Phase 1 complete | HR Officer | Day 30 | File in personnel folder |

### Phase 5: Months 2–3 (Days 31–90)

| Step | Action | Responsible | Timeline | Notes |
|------|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| 5.1 | Continue role execution and skill development | Employee + Line Manager | Months 2–3 | Increasing independence |
| 5.2 | Monthly check-in with line manager | Line Manager | End of Month 2 | Documented notes |
| 5.3 | Controlled: Bi-monthly buddy meeting | Buddy | Month 2 | Reduced frequency |
| 5.4 | Complete all remaining training plan items | Employee | By Day 80 | Track against plan |
| 5.5 | Prepare probation review documentation | Line Manager | Day 80–85 | Evidence of performance against goals |
| 5.6 | Conduct formal probation review | Line Manager + HR Manager | Day 85–90 | See HR-PRB-001 |
| 5.7 | Issue confirmation or extension letter | HR Manager | Day 90 | Per probation outcome |
| 5.8 | Close onboarding checklist — fully complete | HR Officer | Day 90 | All items signed off; filed |

## 6. Controls and Approvals

| Control | Description | Lean | Controlled |
|---------|-------------|------|-----------|
| Pre-boarding checklist | All tasks completed before Day 1 | HR Officer checks | HR Manager verifies |
| Document completeness | All mandatory documents collected and verified | HR Officer checks | HR Manager audits within 5 days |
| Contract signing | Employment contract signed on Day 1 | Yes | Yes (Legal review for G3+) |
| Policy acknowledgement | All key policies read and signed | Within first week | Within first week; tracked in HRIS |
| IT access verification | Correct access levels provisioned | IT Officer confirms | IT Manager approves; HR verifies |
| 30-day review | Formal check-in conducted and documented | Line Manager + HR | Structured form; filed in HRIS |
| 90-day probation review | Formal assessment before confirmation | Line Manager + HR Manager | Panel: Line Manager + HR + Dept Head |
| Onboarding checklist sign-off | All checklist items completed | HR Officer + Line Manager sign | HR Manager reviews and signs |

## 9. Lean vs Controlled Version Summary

| Aspect | Lean | Controlled |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Pre-boarding | HR Officer handles all tasks | HR Officer + IT + Admin; HR Manager verifies |
| Day 1 orientation | HR Officer leads (2 hours) | Structured half-day programme |
| Buddy system | No formal buddy | Assigned buddy for 90 days |
| Induction materials | Printed pack + verbal briefing | Digital onboarding portal + printed pack |
| Check-ins | Informal; line manager only | Structured; line manager + HR + buddy |
| 30-day review | Line Manager conversation (notes) | Formal review form; HR present |
| Training tracking | Spreadsheet | HRIS training module |
| Onboarding checklist | Paper-based | System-based with automated reminders |
| Stakeholder meetings | Organic introduction | Scheduled calendar invites |
